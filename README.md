# PokeCraft

A browser game that mixes a Minecraft-style voxel world with creature collecting.
Explore a procedurally generated block world, build and dig, battle and catch
wild creatures in Capture Cubes, and beat all 31 Gym Leaders to collect every badge.

Built with plain JavaScript and [three.js](https://threejs.org/). There is no build step.

## Running it

ES modules must be served over HTTP, so opening `index.html` directly will not work. From the repo root:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
# or: npx serve .
```

Then open <http://localhost:8000> in a desktop browser.

The game is fully static, so it also works on GitHub Pages: enable Pages for the branch and open the URL.

### On a tablet

The game works on iPads and Android tablets with touch controls, which turn on automatically. You can get it onto a tablet in two ways:

- **GitHub Pages** (easiest): enable Pages for this repo and open the URL on the tablet.
- **From your computer**: start the server with `python3 -m http.server 8000`, find your computer's local IP address (for example `192.168.1.20`), and open `http://192.168.1.20:8000` on the tablet. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi.

On iPad, use Share → **Add to Home Screen** for a full-screen, app-like experience.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD | Move |
| Space | Jump / swim up (swim into a bank and hold it to climb out). In the Sunken City: a **kick** forward in the way you look |
| Shift | Sprint |
| Mouse | Look |
| Left click | Break block, battle the creature under the crosshair, or talk to a person |
| Right click | Place the selected block |
| 1–9 / mouse wheel | Pick a block from the hotbar |
| B | Blocks screen: choose which blocks are in your hotbar |
| E | Battle the targeted creature, or talk to a person (on a Doormat with nothing targeted: your home); step through the Portal Door |
| H | Home screen, while you stand on a Doormat |
| P / Tab | Team & storage screen |
| R | Ride / get off (a creature with the "Can be ridden" badge; tablets: the Ride button) |
| V | Cube Planets only: the steady camera behind you, or back to your own eyes (tablets: the Ride button says Camera there) |
| C | Creature Book: every creature you've seen and caught |
| G | Where to go: pick a gym or place and the arrow follows it (on a tablet, tap the arrow line at the top right) |
| M | Champion missions (after you are the Champion) |
| E / Enter / Y | Say **yes** to a question (sleep, rematch, a friend's invite). Press it fresh; a held key does not count |
| Esc / N / Backspace | Say **no** ("Not now") to a question; otherwise Esc pauses (Resume, Main menu, Play together) |

On a tablet:

| Touch | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Left joystick | Walk (push all the way to run) |
| Drag on the right side | Look around |
| Tap a creature or person | Battle it, or talk to them |
| Big round action button | Does what you look at, and its word says what: **Battle!**, **Talk**, **Heal**, **Open**, **Sleep**, **Press**, **Break**, or the **Yes** word while a question is showing. Greyed out when there is nothing to do |
| Place button (above Jump) | Build the picked block at the crosshair (greyed out when you can't; tap it to hear why) |
| Ride button (above the action button) | Get on your rideable creature (only there when you have one; says Land while flying) |
| Jump button | Jump or swim up (says **Up** while flying, with a **Down** button right under it, and **Swim** in the Sunken City: it kicks you forward) |
| Tap the hotbar | Pick a block (tap the picked one again to change it) |
| Tap the creature card (top left) | Your team |
| Book button | Creature Book |
| Menu button (&#9776;) | Pause screen with big buttons: Team, Blocks, Book, Missions (Champions), Where to go, My home (on a Doormat), Sound, Effects, Play together, Main menu |

Buttons never move or disappear: they stay in the same place and turn grey when they can't do anything.

## Features

- **Voxel world**: a 288×368 terrain (the first 224×224 is the original land; newer land is to the east and south, and the newest strip is in the far south, waiting for new gyms) with a day/night cycle and 11 kinds of places (biomes): plains, flower meadows, forests, swamps, beaches, lakes, deserts with cacti, snowy pine forests with frozen lakes, mountains, snowfields and high peaks with glowing star crystals. Under the hills there are also **caves** (a 12th kind of place): look for a **stone arch with two lamps** on a hill, and walk down its stairs into big, lit caves with huge halls (crystal halls, glowing mushroom halls, underground lakes and a giant fossil with a ribcage), wide tunnels to explore, and lamp posts at the old small holes so you can spot them at night. Star crystals and glowing mushrooms light the way, and rock, metal, ghost and bone creatures live down there. Your lead creature glows to light your way underground, and **treasure chests** hide 3 Capture Cubes each.
- **29 building blocks**, including glass, fluffy cloud blocks, stone bricks, lamps (they glow and light up the ground around them at night), Glowcap mushrooms (they glow too), ice, sandstone, cactus and flower bushes. Press **B** (or tap the picked hotbar slot again) to choose which 9 are in your hotbar. Dig into rock to find coal and gold ore; gold you find in the ground sometimes hides a Capture Cube (gold you placed yourself doesn't).
- **Treasure blocks**: **Sunstone**, **Ruby** and **Sapphire** are rare gems hidden in the rock. Look for them glinting in **cave walls**, or dig deep under the hills. They are the only blocks you have to collect: each one you mine adds 1 to your count, each one you place uses 1. The hotbar and the Blocks panel show how many you have ("Ruby ×3"); at 0 they are greyed out. Your gems are saved with your game and stay yours when you visit a friend's world.
- **Squad creatures** are little crews standing on each other's shoulders (hat kids, sock puppets, bucket buddies and a coat with three kids in it). Every hit knocks one off the top, and the fewer are left, the weaker it hits. Moves that hit many times knock off more of them. The **Big Top Gym** (south of the start, a striped circus tent) has an acrobats' pyramid inside: climb it one battle at a time.
- **Riding**: some big creatures can be ridden (about 1 in 4 of them): look for the "Can be ridden" badge on your team screen, then press R or tap Ride. Land creatures run fast and jump high, water creatures swim on top of the water, sky creatures fly low (Space up, Shift down). Gyms are for walking, so you can't ride into them.
- **Hide and seek** (play together): pause, then Play together, then "Hide and seek with ..." (stand close to your friend). One of you hides while the other has a dark screen and waits 60 seconds. Then the seeker follows the hot/cold compass (Cold, Warm, Hot!, Very hot!) and taps or clicks the hider, or bumps into them, to find them. A Camo creature next to the hider fools the compass! You swap every round and the score is shown. No battles or riding during the game, and you stay inside the play area and out of the gyms.
- **328 original creatures**: 7 for each of 46 types, plus the six Custom guardians (47 types in all, see below). **Tiny**, **Pirate**, **Racer** and **Ball** creatures (wave 14), **Portal**, **Funfair**, **Surprise**, **Doodle** and **Atlantis** creatures (wave 15) **Knight** creatures (the Castle), **Bubble** creatures (the Bubble Well), **Gravity** creatures (Cube Planets) and **Mirror** creatures (the Mirror Palace) live only in their Picture Worlds in the Swirl. They are not easy to find: only a few are out at once, and the big evolved ones of each world only come out once you got a bit further there (the Book says when, e.g. "once you won a golden ticket"). **Food** creatures are funny dishes with faces: sushi rolls on the beach, burgers on the plains, spaghetti bowls in meadows and forests, and an ice cream cone in the snow. **Squad** creatures are little crews standing on each other's shoulders (kids in beanies, sock puppets, bucket buddies, three kids in one coat): every hit knocks one off the top, and a smaller crew hits softer. **Dream** creatures only live in the Dream realm and look held together by nothing (a head-stander, a toddler's drawing, a balloon-head, an umbrella that rains upward). Each lives where it fits: pixies in flower meadows, toxic creatures in swamps, fire and rock ones in the desert, ice ones only in snowy places, cosmic ones come out at night, most of all on the high peaks, and **Bone** creatures (skulls, skeleton dogs, fossil dinos) live only in caves. **Alien** creatures (flying saucers, three-eyed visitors, space pods) drop in at night. Ghosts also only come out at night. **Sky** creatures (kites, balloons, sky whales) float over the mountain tops, **Slime** creatures wobble around swamps and caves, **Light** creatures (sun foxes, lighthouses) love sunny meadows and beaches, and **Paper** creatures (paper planes, origami frogs, storybooks) wander plains and forests. Three types have their own special way to be found: **Time** creatures (hourglasses, metronomes, alarm clocks) only come out for a minute at sunrise and sunset, when the sky turns orange and you hear a soft tick-tock. **Camo** creatures look exactly like a block (leaves, sand, stone or snow) and never move: watch for a blink, then hit or tap the block. **Cyber** creatures (pixel blobs, mouse pointers, Wi-Fi butterflies) only appear when two friends play together close by. **Cozy** creatures (blanket bunnies, mitten kittens, pillow sheep) and **Treasure** creatures (rolling coins, crystal foxes, gem rings, which sparkle all the time) only visit a home you build for them (see **Homes**).
- **Parkour creatures** (a lemur on a rope, a leaping ferret, pole-vaulters and a stick runner) sit up high: on treetops, roofs and pillars you build. Come close and they hop away; chase them a few hops until they are out of breath, then press E or tap them to battle.
- **Ninja creatures** (a masked raccoon that tiptoes, a little crow peeking over its wing, a squirrel that flicks leaves like throwing stars and a bamboo shoot hiding among the grass) sneak about in forests and tall grass, mostly in the evening and at night. Run at one and it vanishes in a puff of smoke and pops up a few blocks away! **Walk up slowly** (no running) and you can reach it and battle. After three puffs it is out of breath, so even fast runners catch it.
- **Ball creatures and the Ball Stadium** (a Picture World, Playland): Boinkle (a rubber ball with puppy ears) and Goalhound, Sealbop (a seal on a beach ball) and Beachboomer, Fuzzchick (a tennis-ball chick) and Smashbird, and Strikehorn (a bowling-ball rhino) are round, shiny and hop when they walk. They live in the stadium zone of **Playland**. Step through the Stadium picture in the Swirl (Champions only) and you arrive beside a big football stadium. **Run into the ball** to dribble it. To shoot, **hold Kick!** next to it (the big button, E or the mouse button; the button changes its word, so there is no new button): a ring fills round the ball, and you **let go while it is green** for a hard, low shot. Let go too early and it is a soft roll the keeper easily stops; hold too long and it flies over the bar. The ball goes exactly where you face. Score **5 goals** past the two goalkeepers (and **5 baskets** on the court) before Captain Kickley battles you; while you are on the pitch or the court the top corner shows your score (⚽ 1/5 · 🏀 0/5), and after a battle with him it starts again at 0. The keepers **dive** at your shot, so aim at the side away from the keeper, and closer shots give them less time. **Defender Dee** runs at the ball as soon as you touch it, anywhere on the pitch, and kicks it out over the sideline (then it goes back to the middle), so dribble away from him and shoot before he gets there. Every goal makes the next one harder (a smaller green part, quicker keepers, a quicker Dee). A miss or a save just puts the ball back, nothing is ever lost, and every goal is saved at once. Then **Captain Kickley** battles you for the Ball Stadium **star**; after that he plays rematches (5 goals and 5 baskets first). Behind the pitch is a **basketball court**: stand next to the ball and **hold Shoot!**: you jump with the ball over your head. **Let go at the top of the jump** (the ball shines then) and it drops through the hoop; too early it falls short, too late it bounces off the back. Close shots are easier, long ones and every new basket need better timing. **Blocker Bo** jumps in front of you (shoot while he lands); the first 3 baskets of each visit give a Capture Cube. Before the Captain battles you the first time, also beat his two helpers: **Winger Wes** (on the pitch) and **Hoops Hazel** (on the court), like the trainers in a gym. You beat them once; rematches only need the goals and baskets. The ball is your own: a friend playing with you has their own ball and their own goals.
- **Homes**: put a **Doormat** in the floor (Blocks panel), stand on it. A home needs a floor from 8x8 up to 20x20, closed walls at least 3 high with **one door, 1 or 2 wide and 2 high**, and a full roof; the Doormat goes inside, by the door. **Cozy home**: half of the wall blocks are wood, plus 3 of Bed, Fireplace, a Rug (4 wool blocks on the floor), a Lamp and 2 Windows (glass); all 5 hearts means more visitors. **Treasure home**: 15 gems (Sunstone, Ruby, Sapphire) in the walls, floor or ceiling; the more gems, the more often treasure creatures visit (up to 5 times as often with 45). There is nothing to pick: your home is every kind it can be, so gems in a wooden cabin make it cozy **and** treasure! The meter shows each kind you have started and the red gaps glow in the walls and roof; **What can my home be?** shows every kind, each with its own **Show me a house** that spins an example. Treasure homes sparkle. When it is ready, cozy or treasure creatures may come to visit (treasure ones rarely!). One home per player: a new Doormat moves your home (the old one turns into Planks). New blocks: **Doormat**, **Bed**, **Fireplace** (it flickers and lights the room) and eight wool colours for rugs.
- **Dreams**: at night, look at the **Bed** in your own **cozy home** and press **E** (or tap it): "Go to sleep and dream?" You wake up in the **Dream realm**, a big sky of floating islands over a soft cloud sea: lavender grass, candy trees, trees that hang upside down under the islands, and giant everyday things (a pencil, toy blocks, a rubber duck, a spoon). Everything is light and bouncy: **jumps are twice as high** (moon jumps!), and **Bounce Blocks** are springs between the islands: stand on one, press jump and fly up high, steer in the air and land on the next island. If you fall off, you land softly on the cloud sea; the arrow shows the way to the nearest bounce block. A little **moon** on the screen sets while the night goes on, and when it is gone it is morning and you wake up beside your bed. The **Upside-Down House** (the Dream gym) floats above one island. Only **Dream creatures** (Flipsy, Scribbit, Bobbloon, Drizzup and their friends) live there. Touch the glowing **Wake Stone** (the arrow points to it once you have the Wonder Badge) to wake up too; losing a battle wakes you up as well. What you build in the dream stays there for next time. Friends you play with don't come along: they get a message, and you can't see each other until you wake up.
- **The Portal Door and the Swirl** (Champions only): beside the tower door stands a big **Portal Door**. It is dark until you are the Champion; then it swirls. Press **E** (or tap or click it) and you step into the **Swirl**: a pastel sky of painted clouds with floating decks, **rivers that run upwards** (stand in one and it carries you up), spiral and round-and-round stairs, and eleven **picture frames** hanging in the air, each covered with a cloth until its world opens (five are secret worlds that still wait for a name). Far away stands the **Wizard's tower**, shut tight, behind a **star bridge**: one golden stone lights up for each of the 12 stars (eleven from the framed Picture Worlds and one from the Portal House, which is in the Swirl itself). The 12 stars show in the top-right corner. The arrow points to the Portal Door home (or to the nearest river if you fall into the clouds). Step into the Portal Door in the Swirl to go home. There are no wild creatures and no morning in the Swirl, and you can build on its decks. The Swirl has four moods: the bright **Sherbet Sky** where you arrive, the glowing **Moonmilk Mist** by the north frames, the warm **Sundrop Shore** by the south frames and the starry **Inkwell Deep** out by the Wizard's tower. Look out for tumbling frames, upside-down crayons, a painter's palette and a giant paintbrush!
- **The Race Track** (Playland, through the 🏎️ frame in the Swirl; Champions): talk to **Racer Rio** at the start and race him round the track: **3 laps** of a long lap (about 470 blocks), 3 flags to drive through. You race in **your own kart**: push the stick (or W) to go, it **speeds up**, steers where you push and the view follows it; pull back to **brake**. Tight corners need slowing down first, or the kart runs wide onto the **red and white kerbs**, which slow you right down just like the grass. Tyre walls and rails bounce you back. The lap has a **snake** of S-bends, a **jump ramp** over the pond (go fast: too slow and you splash in, then try again from just before the ramp), the **orange flyover** (up a ramp, round a high bend with rails, then a **big drop** that makes you faster), a **tunnel** through a hill, a dip of **hairpins** and a **chicane**. Thin **boost strips** kick your kart up to boost speed, and they boost Rio too. Wait for the lamps: 3, 2, 1, GO! If you keep losing, Rio goes just a tiny bit easier (keep trying: practice is what wins). Win the race and beat his two **Pit Crew** helpers by the garage (Pia and Max), then talk to him again to battle him for the star. A won race can always be raced again just for fun (while the Pit Crew still wait, or after a "Not now" to a rematch). With the star, Playland starts up again: karts drive laps and the boost pads blink. Wild **Racers** live there: they rev, then zoom away in a straight line (faster than you can run!) and are out of breath after three dashes. The big ones (Vroomster, Blastskate, Karthog) only come out after you beat Rio in a race.
- **Racer creatures** (🏎️ fast but fragile): Vroomlet > Vroomster (a wind-up toy car), Zipskate > Blastskate (a rocket roller skate), Pitpig > Karthog (a go-kart pig) and Wheelie (a happy tyre). Moves: Wheel Spin, Ramp Jump, Zoom Dash (goes first), Rev Up, signature Photo Finish (Vroomster) and Nitro Blast (Blastskate, hits 2 to 4 times), big moves Pit Crew, Full Throttle and Race Day. Vroomster and Karthog can be ridden (1 in 4).
- **The Funfair** (Playland, through the 🎡 frame in the Swirl; Champions): a fair that stands still, waiting for the festival. A board on the striped arch has three empty **ticket** slots. Win a **golden ticket** at each of three games. Each game has **three rounds** that get harder, with a little clock or one lap per round and the score in the corner; a missed round simply starts again (nothing else is lost), and after two misses the game slows down a little to help: **Bumper Cars** (stand by a car and press **Drive!**; the stick is where you want to go and the car keeps sliding, so you have to plan: bump the balloon on the **back** of a bumper bot's car; hits from the front or the side only bounce, Jump honks the horn. Round 1: four easy bots. Round 2: five **Zoomers** zoom off when they see you coming, so follow them and bump them when they get tired and slow. Round 3: all six, and the **Spinners** turn round to look at you, so line up behind one and come in fast), **Balloon Bounce** (the big trampoline: **hold Jump** to bounce higher, let go to come down and steer in the air. Round 1: five slow balloons up high. Round 2: balloons that wobble in figures of eight and zigzags, and **target balloons** (a red-and-white target on top) that only pop when you land on top of them. Round 3: **combo!** Pop one on every bounce for x2 and x3 points, gold balloons are worth the most: get 30 points) and **Gold Ring Ride** (press **Ride!** at the carousel, push the stick **in or out** to change horses and **jump** for the high rings. Each round is one lap: catch enough points before the lap is over. Gold rings are worth 3, silver 1, and the carousel turns faster every round). Each ticket lights a slot, and the roller coaster's lights come on one third at a time. With three tickets the **roller coaster** opens: a long ride up the lift hill, down the big drop and round to the **Sky Stage** high above the fair, where **Merry Mae, the Fairkeeper** battles you for the star, once you have beaten her helpers **Bumper Bea** and **Bouncy Ben**. **Slide!** takes you back down. A rematch uses the tickets again, and the games start again from round 1, a bit harder (quicker bots and balloons, a faster carousel, a few more points). After the star the fair wakes up: the Ferris wheel and the coaster are all lit and the fair tune plays. Ticket Tess at the arch tells you what to do.
- **Funfair creatures** (🎡 the fair's rides and treats come alive, each wearing a string of little bulbs): Merrycolt > Galloprance (a painted carousel horse on a golden pole; Galloprance can be ridden, 1 in 4), Flufflamb > Candyram (a lamb of pink candy floss with lollipop horns), Twistpup > Balloodle (a balloon dog, then a balloon poodle) and Tixit (a walking paper ticket). Moves: Ticket Toss, Bumper Bash, Zip Ride (goes first), Fun House (lowers attack), signature Grand Gallop (Galloprance) and Balloon Burst (Balloodle, 2 to 5 pops), big moves Fun for All, Prize Wheel and Fair Day. They live only in the Funfair zone.
- **Tiny creatures** (wave 14) live only in the **Giant House**, and only in small hiding places: **Antsy** and **Antlas** (an ant carrying a crumb, then a whole sugar cube), **Thimbit** and **Spoolmouse** (a mouse in a thimble, then on a rolling cotton spool with a sewing pin), **Dustbun** and **Fluffnado** (a dust bunny, then a whirl of fluff) and **Pipsqueak** (a seed that squeaks; it only comes out once you found all 12 cookie crumbs). They all have big shiny eyes and they never move: they sit in a tipped cup, under a leaf pile, in a flowerpot, a slipper or a crack in the wall and **peek out now and then**. Come closer and they stay out; press E or tap one to battle it. Tiny moves are strong against Racer, Food and Cyber, but fire, sky and ball are strong against them. Their big moves are Hide in a Cup, Ant Army and Small World.
- **The Giant House** (the first Picture World, the **house frame** in the Swirl, Champions only): you step into the picture and shrink to the size of the Wizard's pet mouse, so the kitchen is **huge**. **Nibbles, the Wizard's Mouse** sits in a **fort of sugar cubes** round a giant cookie on the table, with the first **star**. One way up, with four puzzles; Nibbles' **yellow notes** draw a clue at each one. (1) Ride the **toy train** to the fan, pull the **tea towel** off it, wind the **wind-up key** (it runs the fan for 12 seconds) and run into the wind: it blows you up to the shelf. (2) On the counter, put the **plug** in the sink first, then turn on the **tap**: the sink and the deep river fill up, and a **bottle cap** floats you along it (without the plug the water just runs away, and the dry river is too deep to climb out at the far end). (3) A big **cat** is asleep on the book stairs down to the table. Push the **ball of yarn** off the counter: the cat jumps down after it and plays for a while. Quick, down the books! (4) On the table, **roll the marble** down the ruler, then stand in the **spoon**: the marble lands on the handle and the spoon flings you over the fort wall onto the cookie. The fort's little back door only opens from inside. Two **secret ways** are there for the curious: a **mouse hole** in the wall (it skips the fan) and a **keyhole** in a cupboard. Look for **12 cookie crumbs** (+1 Capture Cube each, +5 for all of them); once you have all twelve, the shy **Pipsqueak** comes out of hiding. Nibbles has four creatures (levels 59-62) and gives rematches once you hold the star.
- **The Pirate Seas** (the first open picture frame in the Swirl; with Atlantis it is the world **Deep Blue**, so its frames say "Deep Blue · Pirate Seas" and "Deep Blue · Atlantis"): step into the frame and you arrive on **Sandy Start**, a little island in a big sunny sea. Stand on the **cannon** and press **E** (or tap the big **Fire!** button): BOOM, you fly through the air! But the cannons **swing left and right**: dots show where you will land, and they turn **gold** when the cannon points at the next island. Fire then and you land on it; fire too early or too late and you splash into the sea (the cannon then swings a bit slower). The cannons home to Sandy Start hold still. **Ropes** hang from log arches between the rocks: stand at a rope post and **hold Swing!** (the big button, E or the mouse): you hang on while the rope swings back and forth. The rope turns **gold** when you are over the other island: let go of the button then and you land there. Let go in the middle and... splash! (Every rope works both ways.) Fall in the sea? No problem: swim to a beach (jump to climb out) or swim into a **rope ladder** and it lifts you up. Nothing is ever lost. At the far end floats **Captain Squawk's ship**: a cannon on Lookout Isle shoots you onto its deck. Three pirates (Deckhand Dot, Lookout Lou and First Mate Fin) battle you on the way, and **Captain Squawk**, a giant parrot, guards his **treasure chest** on the back deck. But first his **12 rowboats** must go, three at a time: climb the little step behind a **swivel cannon** on the ship's rail and turn to aim. **Hold Fire!**: the longer you hold, the further the shot goes (then nearer again). The boats row fast figure-eights and the ball takes a moment to fly, so aim ahead of them; let go when the dots turn **gold**. Sink three and the next three row out, a bit faster each time; sink all 12 and beat his three pirates, and the Captain is angry enough to battle. The rowboats keep rowing after that, so you can sink them again just for fun. (After you beat him his crew rows new boats out, so a rematch starts with the boats again.) Beat him and the chest pops open in a shower of gold: the star was inside! Then take a **home cannon** back to Sandy Start and step into the picture to go back to the Swirl. Your star lights a stone of the star bridge, and Squawk offers rematches that grow.
- **Pirate creatures** (a parrot in a striped bandana, a crab with a golden hook, a bottle with a tiny ship inside and an octopus in a barrel with a spyglass) live only in the Pirate Seas: parrots and crabs on the islands, bottles and octopuses out at sea. Their moves are cannonballs, hook swipes and a swashbuckling quick attack; Featherbeard learns **Broadside** (up to four cannonballs in a row) and Anchorclaw learns **Anchor Drop**. Shipshape, the big bottle, can be ridden across the sea.
- **Jolly Jax's Surprise Party** (wave 15, a Picture World: step into the 🎁 gift-wrapped frame in the Swirl; Champions; it used to be the Room of Doors): a surprise birthday party inside an enormous present. There are balloons and bunting, a long party table with cups and plates, piles of presents, guests in party hats and a huge birthday cake. On the wall hangs the **Party Spinner**: stand on its gold pad and press **Spin!**, then **Stop!** when the game you want is under the arrow (good timing picks your game). It picks one of **four party games**, and the party floor builds the game in a whoosh of confetti. Each game has **three rounds** that get harder, and before round 2 and round 3 a party popper goes off: **Surprise!** Something changes (two piñatas, lights out, double gold, a pie party, a wobbly crane, tiny presents, a mirror...). Win all three rounds for a **golden bow**. A missed round simply starts again, and after a couple of misses it gets just a tiny bit slower. If the Spinner stops on a game you won already, it clicks on to the next game you still have to win. With all four bows it keeps spinning, and whatever it picks you play again, just for fun. When it picks a new game, press **Play!** on the party floor or at the Spinner and you pop onto that game's gold start pad. To stop, just walk away: the game folds away and keeps your round for later. Win all 3 rounds of a game for its golden bow; 4 bows (one per game) light the cake. **The Piñata Party**: press Play! and a piñata swings on a rope under a bunch of balloons. Press **Whack!** (E, a click, the big button, or tap the piñata) to swing your party stick the way you look. Whack it **when it swings AT you**, low down: **CRACK!** Any other hit is just a Bonk, and a whack at nothing makes the stick take a moment to come back. The balloons zip off after every hit, so run to meet it. Round 1 is a llama, round 2 a fish that sometimes dodges, round 3 a fast rocket. **Jack Catch**: the pop-up boxes fling jack-in-the-boxes high across the floor: run under a jack's shadow and it lands in your arms (a gold jack counts 2). Watch out for the **cream pies** that fly at where you are running (a pink ring shows where). Catch 6, then 9, then 12. **Gift Stack**: a toy crane swings a present back and forth over a table. - **Champion's Tower**: a final challenge after all 32 badges, with guards, a coach, Champion Solenne and the legendary Zenithar.
- **Rainbow creatures** are the rarest of all. They only appear in daytime, their colours shimmer, and a message tells you when one is nearby.
- **Evolution**: 67 creatures evolve when they reach a certain level, for example Ampup → Thunderwolf, Moonhop → Lunaroo and Drumbo → Bongorilla.
- **Starter choice**: Emberpup (fire), Sproutle (grass) or Bubbuck (water). Each one evolves at level 16.
- **Turn-based battles** that play out in the world. They include a type-effectiveness chart, same-type bonus, critical hits, priority moves, move effects (see **Moves** below), switching and running away. Your whole team shares the XP.
- **Moves**: 134 moves, at least 4 for every type, and every creature also learns moves from a second type. The buttons say what each move does. When a creature with 4 moves wants to learn a new one, you pick which move to forget (or say "Don't learn"). Older creatures get the new moves one at a time as they level up.
- **Catching**: throw Capture Cubes at wild creatures. Your odds are better when the target has low HP. You start with 10 cubes and find more by winning battles or breaking leaves.
- **Progression**: XP, level-ups, learning new moves and evolution. Your team holds up to 6 creatures; extra catches go to a storage box, where you can sort them (newest, level, name, type), show one type at a time, or let a creature go back to the wild (the Creature Book still remembers it).
- **Creature Book**: press **C** (or the Book button). Every creature you meet in a battle shows its picture, name and type; the ones you haven't met are black shapes. Catch one to fill in its whole page: where it lives, how it evolves, its moves and what it is strong or weak against. It works like a little wiki: tap a creature's name or picture on the team screen to open its page, tap a move to see what it does and who learns it, and tap a type to see all creatures of that type and what it beats. **‹ Back** goes back one page (or back to your team).
- **Attack animations**: every type has its own little animation (fire embers, water bubbles, sparks, music notes, rattling bones...), the sixteen signature moves have special ones, and moves that heal, boost, lower or put to sleep show it too. Turn them off with **Effects** on the pause screen if your device feels slow.
- **Sound effects**: soft, short sounds for building, battles, catching and badges. Sound-type creatures have their own tunes (Dinglet rings, Gongolin goes BONG, Drumbo drums), their moves sound special, and in the wild they sing every few seconds when you are near, with music notes floating up so you can spot them. Turn them off with the **Sound** button on the pause screen.
- **Weather** changes every few minutes and depends on where you are: rain, thunderstorms, snow in cold places, sandstorms in the desert (bones peek out of the sand), misty swamp mornings, shooting stars on clear nights, and a rainbow after rain. Each kind brings out more of some creatures: storms bring electric ones, rain water ones, snow ice ones, and rainbows make rainbow creatures much easier to find, and shooting stars bring aliens as well as cosmic creatures. Slime creatures love rain, paper creatures hide indoors when it rains or storms, sky creatures ride the storm winds, and light and sky creatures visit the rainbow.
- **Auto-save** to this device, plus **online saves** when you sign in (see below). This saves your world edits, team, badges, cubes and position.

Creatures farther from the starting point are higher level. Your team slowly regains HP outside of battle.

## Moves

Every move has one small effect at most. The battle buttons say what a move does.

### Big moves

Every type has 2 or 3 **big moves**. Creatures learn them from level 21 on (Lv 21 to 34), and only the late gym leaders use them, so you meet them as a surprise. Each one is a big decision: it helps a lot, but it costs something.

| Big move | What it does | The price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team boost (Go Team Go!, Hive Hum...) | Your whole team, also the friends waiting, gets +1 attack until the battle ends | Your defence goes down 2 |
| Team shield (Stand Together, Leafy Roof, Stone Fort...) | Your whole team takes 30% less damage for 3 turns | You rest on your next turn |
| Risky hit (Wild Blaze, Live Wire...) | Power 60. Half the time it hits 3 times as hard | The other half it hurts you instead |
| Goodbye (Wave Goodbye, Seed Gift...) | You faint, and the next friend comes in with full HP and +1 attack | You are out, and it only works if a friend can come in |
| Curse (Static Fuzz, Big Freeze...) | May slow every foe, also the ones waiting, until the battle ends | Works only 4 times out of 10 |
| Field (High Tide, Snow Day, Garden Bloom...) | One type is 50% stronger for 5 turns | The foe gets the boost too! |
| Last word (Never Give Up, Last Ember, Cliff Crash...) | Power 140 and never misses | Only works when you have less than 1/4 HP left |

Stat changes and sleep go away when a creature is switched out. A few creatures learn a **special move** (Blazehound, Tidalord, Grovebeast, Gongolin, Kitegale, Slimperor, Lumitower, Origrane, Clangalarm, Statuo, Pixbit 2.0, Dozeram, Crownguard, Teetertower, Socktacular, Topsyturvy (Head Over Heels), Scrawlbert (Crayon Storm) and Whirlwhisk (Leaf Whirlwind) at Lv 30, Zenithar at Lv 45). Player battles need both players to have the latest version of the game.

## Gyms and badges

## Champion's Tower

After you collect all 32 badges (the top-right corner shows them in 4 rows of 8), the **Champion's Tower** opens. It is a tall stone tower with a rainbow roof and a glowing crystal on top, near the middle of the map (the arrow in the top-right corner shows the way). Inside:

- **Nurse Poppy** heals your team. **Coach Dex** trains you with 3 creatures at your best level and gives **double XP** (and 1 Capture Cube) every time.
- **Guard Bram** (Lv 57–59) and **Guard Opal** (Lv 58–60) must be beaten first, one after the other.
- **Champion Solenne** has 6 creatures (Lv 59–62). Her last one is **Zenithar**, a one-of-a-kind rainbow guardian. It can't be caught, and it **powers up** when it is down to half its health!
- If you win, you become the **Champion**: you get 20 Capture Cubes, a gold "Champion" title on the screen, and the win is saved. You can challenge Solenne again any time (her creatures are 3 levels stronger).
- If you lose, you wake up at the start, with a hint. Heal with Nurse Poppy and train with Coach Dex, then try again.
- After 16 badges, wild creatures are also 10 levels stronger, so there is always something to train against.
- Champions keep their title, but the tower waits until they have every badge again.

## Champion missions

Once you are the **Champion**, talk to Champion Solenne again: she gives you **missions**, one at a time (M shows them any time; a small chip in the corner reminds you what you are doing). Finish one, go back to her for the next. Not in the mood? **Do a different one** swaps it. The first 12 are made by hand; **after that the missions never end**: she keeps making new ones, in rounds of 6 (two treasure maps, a build, a catch, a rematch or another build, and a giant). The panel counts them (*Mission 15 · 14 done*). Your list is the same on every device. The giants **grow a little every round**.

- **Treasure map**: you get a piece of an old map with a picture of the ground and a red **X**, and words that tell you where to start and how many steps to walk. The chip says *cold, warm, hot* as you get closer. Stand on the X and **dig 2 blocks down** (stand in the hole) to find the treasure. The X is the same for everyone in the same world.
- **Build challenge**: build something (a Lamp Tower, a Water Bridge, a Rainbow Wall, a Star Tower). A list shows what is still missing; the game checks your build by itself, or press **Check my build**. Only blocks you placed yourself count.
- **Giant battle**: two players against one giant creature (play together, stand close, ask your friend to help). Both of you battle at once; your creatures are copies, so nobody's real team gets hurt. The friend who helps gets a prize too. If you have no friend to play with and only giants are left, Solenne gives you other missions first.
- **Catch a creature** (after the first 12): "Catch a Fire creature". The card tells you which places to look in and links to the Creature Book. Only a creature you catch while the mission is on counts. Prize: 8 Capture Cubes and 2 Star Blocks.
- **Gym rematch** (after the first 12): "Beat the Boulder Gym again". Win a rematch there. Prize: 5 Capture Cubes and 2 Rainbow Bricks.
- The later treasure maps use new X spots (24 of them); builds get new shapes (towers with a Lamp, Crystal or wool colour on top, long walls, Glow Towers...). Maps and builds wait while you are in a dream: do them back in the normal world.

Prizes are Capture Cubes and three **rare mission blocks** you can only get this way: **Star Block** (treasure maps), **Rainbow Brick** (builds) and **Giant Trophy** (giant battles). They work like gems: placing uses one, breaking one gives it back. Missions are saved per player.

## Online saves

Sign in on the title screen to keep your progress online and continue on another device. Accounts and saves use [Supabase](https://supabase.com) (project `pokecraft`).

- **Create account** sends a confirmation email. After clicking the link, sign in with the same email and password. Each device only needs to sign in once.
- **Forgot password?** emails a link; opening it brings you back to the game to choose a new password.
- The game saves online every minute, after every battle, when you pause, and when you leave the page. It always saves on the device too, so it keeps working offline.
- When you sign in, the game loads whichever save is newer: the one online or the one on this device.
- Each player can only read and write their own save. This is enforced by Row Level Security in the database, so the project URL and publishable key in `src/cloud.js` are safe to be public.

If the save server can't be reached, the game still works and saves on the device only.

### Supabase setup

- **Authentication → URL Configuration**: Site URL and Redirect URLs must include the game's address (for example `https://gondiken.github.io/pokecraft/`), or email links won't return to the game.

## Play together

Two or more people can play in the same world at the same time, each on their own device.

1. The player whose world you want to use pauses the game, types their name and presses **Share my world**. A 5-letter code appears.
2. The other player pauses their game, types their name and the code, and presses **Join a world**.

You see each other walking around with name tags, with each player's lead creature following them. Blocks that anyone places or breaks show up for everyone. Each player keeps their own team, badges and Capture Cubes, and has their own battles and wild creatures. Building in the shared world is saved in the host's world.

**Battle each other**: look at (or tap) another player, click or press **E** and pick **Battle** to ask for a battle. They get a **Battle!** / **Not now** question (or press **Y** / **N**). Both teams fight at full HP, nobody's creatures get hurt or lost, and there is no XP. The winner gets **3 Capture Cubes**. **Give up** ends the battle early.

**Battle, trade or play hide and seek** (wave 17): look at a friend and press **E** (or tap them) to open the friend menu: **⚔️ Battle**, **🔁 Trade** or **🙈 Hide and seek** (stand close for that one). Pick Trade and your friend is asked "Dad wants to trade with you!". Say **Trade!** and you both get your own team screen in trade mode: the same cards, sorting and type chips as **P**, with a bar on top showing what you give and what your friend gives. Tap **Offer** on one of yours (or give nothing: then it's a present), see your friend's offer change live, and press **Ready**. When you are both ready, the trade happens (changing an offer un-readies you both). The creature keeps everything (level, moves, traits like "Can be ridden") and goes into the team, or the Storage Box when the team is full, and into the Creature Book. Your last team creature always stays with you. It works the same everywhere, in the Swirl and every Picture World too, and with more than two players: you pick who. If a game closes in the middle, nothing is lost and nothing is doubled: the creature waits safely ("on the way") and the trade finishes, or the creature comes home, the next time you play together.

**Follow your friend into the Dream or the Swirl**: when the host goes to sleep and dreams (or steps through the Portal Door into the Swirl), the visitor is asked "Dad is dreaming. Dream together?" and can say **Follow** or **Not now**. Not now is fine: later there is a **Follow ...** button in the pause screen's Play together box, or use a Bed (or the Portal Door) in the host's world. You arrive next to your friend, see each other, build together (what you build there is saved in the host's world) and can earn the realm's badge for yourself. You do not need your own cozy home, a night or the Champion title: your friend's trip is the key. When your friend wakes up or comes back, you do too (after any battle you are in). Duels, giant battles and hide and seek only work when you are in the same place.

A visitor's own world is untouched. **Go back to my world** returns them to it, and that happens automatically if the host stops sharing or leaves. The connection uses Supabase Realtime. Neither player needs to be signed in, but both need an internet connection.

For testing on one computer, add `?mp=local` to the address. Tabs in the same browser then connect to each other directly, without the server.

## Tests

Browser tests (Playwright) are in `tests/`: `cd tests && npm install && npm test`. See `CLAUDE.md` for details.
