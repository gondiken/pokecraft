# T40a.5 · The Giant House as a puzzle house

Owner 2026-10-26: getting to Nibbles was too easy ("I thought there'd be some puzzle, still possible for a smart 7 year old,
that'd force me to do two things at once"), too much plain walking, and the block cat and the grey spoon did not read as a
cat and a spoon. The house was covered in the Swirl while it was rebuilt.

## The way up (one route, four puzzles)

| # | Where | What you do | Why it is a puzzle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Floor | Ride the toy train from the arrival to the fan (13.5 s) | No long walk |
| 1 | Fan | Pull the tea towel off, wind the key (12 s), run back into the wind (to the short shelf) | Two things in the right order, then a run against the clock (an adult needs about 4 s of the 12) |
| 2 | Counter | Plug in the sink first, then the tap; float on the bottle cap | Order: without the plug the water runs away; the dry river is a dead end (steps back by the sink) |
| 3 | Counter -> table | The cat sleeps on the book stairs. Push the yarn off the counter: it jumps down after it and plays 14 s | Use what a cat would do; then hurry down |
| 4 | Table | Roll the marble down the ruler (8 s), stand in the spoon's bowl: the marble hits the handle and flings you into the sugar fort | Two things at once (the marble's stack is 20 blocks from the bowl, out of reach) |

Nibbles' crayon notes stand at each puzzle (pictures, no words). The guide line names the next step. Secret ways: the mouse
hole (from the floor to the shelf: skips the fan) and the keyhole (cans in the cupboard up to the counter, east of the river:
skips the fan and the river). The fort's back door opens only from inside (one way, like the Firewall Maze's exit).
All 12 crumbs: Pipsqueak's spawn condition `crumbs` is true (`world.allCrumbs`); the other six Tiny creatures stay on `nook`.

## Pieces

- `src/houseprops.js`: the models (cat sleeping / standing, yarn, plug, wind-up key, towel, spoon seesaw on a cotton reel,
  marble, ruler, notes). Screenshots for the owner: `node tests/houseprops-preview.mjs <dir>`.
- Block 250 `HOUSE_PROP` (`hidden`: solid, never drawn, see-through for neighbours, no outline): under the cat, the plug, the
  yarn and the key box.
- `giantland.js`: fan at (11.5, 53.5), shelf z 31-50, mouse tunnel 34 further south, the train on the floor only (no track on
  the ramp or bench; the books on the bench are gone), the river 4 deep (x 48-96, a wall stays before the cupboard room),
  book stairs at x 111-116 where the spoon was, the fort (r 10.2-11.7, 4 high) and the seesaw east of it.
  `REALMS.house.version` 2 (old house saves arrive at the start).
- `houselive.js`: the puzzle state machines; actions through the T40 `near` / `use` hooks (Pull! Wind! Plug in! Push!
  Roll!) and `hit` on the hidden cells. Everything per player, only the crumbs saved.
- Checks: `tests/housecheck.mjs` (the Boss with all four puzzles and with none left out, the mouse hole skips the fan, the
  dry river, nobody stuck, the fort door) and the `tiny` spec (every puzzle played with real physics, timings, shots).
