# T42 · 🌀 Portal type and the Portal House (in the Swirl itself)

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `plans/wave15.md` (shared rules: type `portal`, wool **215**, block ids **215-223**, tile prefix
`PO_`, only T42 changes the Swirl's terrain and bumps `REALMS.swirl.version` to **5**), `ROADMAP.md` "Wave 15",
`STORY.md` "The Swirl" / "The Picture Worlds", `plans/T40.md` (the Picture World base: B.5 travel, B.8 Bosses, B.9 live
parts, B.10 `Mover`), `plans/T38a.md` / `plans/T38d.md` (the Swirl and its looks), `plans/T41.md` (the style of this plan).

**Owner context (2026-10-27, through the orchestrator):** the son loved the **skill games** (football, basketball, race,
rowboats) and the puzzles began to feel the same. The Portal House must be a skill game, **different from the others**:
a held-and-let-go button is already used three times (Kick!, Shoot!, Fire! / Swing!) and T41 uses it again (Catch!), so
this one uses **no hold at all**: every action is one quick tap. Never a fail. Everything works on iPad with the one
action button. Teleports are cheap: the rooms are in the Swirl world itself, so a swirl is only "set the player's
position", with no loading.

**What the child does:** on the Swirl's hub a swirl glows on the floor: step on it and you pop out on the porch of the
**Portal House**, the home of the Portal creatures (the ones who built the Portal Door). Its rooms float apart in the
Swirl's sky, joined only by swirls. In the Hall of Doors **Keeper Kit** hands over a **Swirl Wand**: tap a white pad
and **your own swirl** (teal) appears there. Every room has an **orange swirl**; yours and the orange one are joined:
in one, out the other. **Speedy in, speedy out:** fall into a swirl from high up and you fly out of the other one fast.
Four swirl rooms, four tricks (aim far, jump from the right height, wait for the right moment, re-aim while flying),
then the last door opens and **Grandpa Arch**, the oldest Portal creature, battles for the `portal` star. A miss never
costs anything: you fall onto a soft cloud and pop back to the start of that room.

## 0. What makes it different from the other skill games

| Game | What your body does | What you judge | Button |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Football / basketball | run, kick / jump, throw | power / top of the jump | **hold** and let go |
| Race | run a track | speed, the line | stick only |
| Rowboats / cannons / ropes | stand, aim | where a boat will be / the swing | **hold** and let go |
| Atlantis (T41) | swim in 3D | the lunge distance | **hold** and let go |
| **Portal House** | **jump and fall through swirls, fly out the other side** | **where to put your swirl, from how high to fall, when to jump, quick aim while flying** | **one tap** (no hold) |

What is new:
1. **You build your own shortcut.** The only game where the child decides where the way goes: one tap puts your swirl
   on a white pad (snap to big pads, so a 7-year-old always hits one); tapping another pad moves it.
2. **Momentum through swirls.** Your speed going in is your speed coming out, turned to point out of the other swirl.
   A drop from 4 blocks throws you a little, from 16 blocks right across the room. The child learns it by doing it.
3. **Four rooms, four tricks**, each a different skill (section 3): a far aim, a height choice, a timing, and a
   **quick re-aim in mid-air** (the hardest, and a natural retry loop: miss the tap and you simply fly the same arc again).
4. **Seeing yourself pop out** somewhere else: rooms have windows onto the Swirl and onto the other rooms.

Never a fail: no timer, no health, nothing taken away. A fall lands on a soft Swirl Cloud floor and puffs you back to
the start of the room; a loop that goes round 4 times stops on a ledge with a hint.

## 1. Decisions (planned; the owner can change them, see the open questions)

- **The House is part of the Swirl world, not a hop-down realm.** Reasons: the story and the data say it is "in the
  Swirl itself" (`portal` has `door: false`, no frame); room-to-room teleports are position sets with no loading
  screen ("teleports are cheap"); realms together (T38b/T40) already work in the Swirl, so a guest who followed the host
  into the Swirl just walks over (no new entrance kind, no follow code); the guide, saving and `realmPos` need nothing
  new. A hop-down realm would need its own entrance kind (`followableAt`), its own travel screen and a door-less way in,
  for a world that is only about 64 x 32 blocks. Cost: the Swirl gains wild creatures (only in the House's calm rooms)
  and three realm fields (section 2.4).
- **Where:** the empty **north-east sky of the Swirl**, box **R = x 92..159, z 0..35, y 1..47**. Checked: in the version
  4 terrain every cell of R is air (only the cloud sea at y 0); no nebula puff, spark, floater or islet is in or near it
  (the nearest islet, (126, 40) r 3, starts at z 36; the spec keeps a 3-block gap). It lies outside all four T38d zones
  (haze only), far from the hub's view line to the Wizard's island.
- **Reached by:** (a) a **floor swirl on the hub** (the "Hub Swirl", x 50..52, z 72..74, on the deck top y 20, between
  the north-east lamp post and the spiral walkway): a mesh + a trigger box, **no block changes on the hub**; walking onto
  it pops you onto the porch. (b) A **new lift stream** from the cloud sea up to the porch (`HOUSE_LIFT`, appended to
  `world.lifts`), so a fall from the House is never a dead end (the guide already says "Ride the river up!" to the
  nearest lift). (c) The porch has a **Porch Swirl** back to the hub. Every old Swirl part stays where it is.
- **Swirl edits of version 4 are kept, not dropped:** the map size and every old cell are unchanged, so an old edit still
  means the same cell. Edits inside the House's keep boxes (and the Hub Swirl keep) are dropped by `applyEdits` through
  `editAllowed` -> `protectedAt` (keeps) as they load, and every House block is `fixed`. One generic realm field does it:
  `REALMS.swirl.sameCells = [4]` (versions whose cells line up with this one): `cleanRealmEdits` keeps those edits and
  `restore` keeps `realmPos` for them (two short lines in `realms.js`). Versions 1-3 are still dropped as before.
- **Rooms are cubes floating in R**, each with an underside that tapers like the Swirl's decks, a roof, and glass windows.
  From the porch you see the next room through a window.

## 3. The four swirl rooms

General rule, said once in the Hall: **"Your swirl and the orange swirl are joined: in one, out the other!"** Each
room has one orange swirl, a start ledge with a Hall swirl back (a small door swirl marked 🏠), an exit door swirl to
the next room (the room counts as done when you step into it), 3 **sparks** hanging where a good flight goes (+1
Capture Cube each, once per trip, not saved), and a Swirl Cloud floor at the bottom of every pit (`catch` box:
"Whoops! Back to the start of this room." with a puff).

1. **R2 Gap Room · aim far.** Three gaps, each wider than the last (6, 10, 16 blocks), the orange swirl in the wall of
   the near ledge. Across each gap a white pad on the far wall, the last one 26 blocks away and up high, so the child
   must aim at a small far target (the pad glows when the crosshair is on it). Walk into the orange swirl, step out of
   yours across the gap. The third pad is on the ceiling over a high ledge: you drop out of the ceiling onto it.
2. **R3 Fling Room · from how high?** A deep pit with the orange swirl in its floor. Three ledges above the pit (drops
   4, 9 and 16 to the swirl) and two white pads on the side wall (low, high). You come out of your pad flying sideways:
   from the low ledge you land on a near shelf (spark), from the middle on the middle shelf (spark), only from the top
   ledge with the high pad do you reach the exit shelf across the room. Stairs and a lift bring you back up.
3. **R4 Clock Room · wait for it.** The orange swirl slides up and down a rail on the side wall (`move`, period 5 s,
   triangle wave). Your pad is in the floor of a pit below the start ledge (drop 10). Where the orange swirl is when you
   arrive decides where you fly out: low = the bottom shelf (a lift back up), middle = a spark shelf, top = the exit
   shelf. Your fall takes about 0.8 s, so you must jump **before** it reaches the top: "Jump in when the orange swirl is
   going up!" The top window is about 1.2 s wide.
4. **R5 Skip Hall · quick, while you fly!** Four pits in a row, each with an orange swirl in its floor, and a white floor
   pad (with `lean`: out goes up and forward) on the platform after each pit. Put your swirl on pad 1, jump into pit 1:
   you shoot out of pad 1 in an arc that drops you into pit 2. **While you fly, tap pad 2** (the next white pad ahead)
   to move your swirl there, and you come out of pad 2 into pit 3, and so on. Miss the tap and you simply come out of
   the old pad again and fly the same arc (a retry loop that teaches by itself); after 4 loops on the same pad you land
   on its platform with the hint "Tap the next white pad while you fly!". After pit 4 the exit shelf.

Aim help (child-friendly, never a full auto-aim): the white pad nearest the crosshair inside a cone counts (8° on the
ground, 20° while flying or falling), only pads of your room, never the pad your swirl is on already, line of sight
needed, reach 32 blocks. The pad under the crosshair glows teal before you tap.

## 4. Swirl physics (`src/portalplay.js`, new, pure, no game imports, Node-runnable)

- `PORTAL = { reach: 32, cone: 8, coneAir: 20, step: 5, keep: 3.2, maxSpeed: 15, flingG: 14, ignore: 0.4, loops: 4, sample: 0.02 }`.
- **Going in:** the live part checks the player box against every swirl's trigger box (the layer of cells in front of
  its face) once per frame, also while carried by a fling. Wall swirls need you to move into the face; floor swirls
  take you as soon as your feet are in the trigger. A swirl you just came out of is ignored for `ignore` s.
- **Speed in, closed form (same at 60 and 20 fps):** a fall into a swirl uses the **drop height**, not `vel`
  (`exitSpeed(drop) = min(maxSpeed, keep * sqrt(drop))`: drop 4 -> 6.4, 9 -> 9.6, 16 -> 12.8). The live part tracks the
  highest feet y since you last stood on the ground. In a fling the speed is the closed-form `|v(t)|` of that fling.
  Walking into a wall swirl is below `step`.
- **Coming out:** below `step`: you step out 1 cell in front of the other swirl, facing out of it (wall) or keeping your
  yaw (floor; a floor exit lifts you 1). At `step` or more: a **fling**: a `Mover` (`canLeave: false`) on the new
  `PATHS.fling` (registered by `portalplay.js`): `p(t) = p0 + v t + (0, -flingG t² / 2, 0)`, `v` = the exit normal (tipped
  by `lean`) times the speed. `flingFlight(solid, p0, v, g)` samples the player box every `sample` s and ends the path
  at the last free sample (the Mover's `time`), so a fling never goes through a block; then normal physics take over
  (you drop the rest). `flingG` is half the normal gravity ("you float a little after a swirl"), which makes the
  distances readable: a sideways exit 6 blocks above the landing flies 5.9 / 8.4 / 10.3 / 11.9 blocks at speed 6.4 /
  9 / 11 / 12.8.
- **Loops:** a fling that ends in a swirl starts the next one at once (R5). 4 trips through the same pair without
  touching ground: the loop ends on that room's nearest ledge with the room's hint.
- Moving swirl: `swirlAt(swirl, clock)` (closed form), `slotAimed(eye, dir, slots, cone, solid)`, `roomState(game)`,
  `roomKey(n)` live here too, so the spec and `pictureboss.js` import them without three.js.
- `tests/portalcheck.mjs` (served to the page as well, like `housecheck.mjs`): a walk graph BFS over the House with
  swirl edges (each designed trick is an edge whose flight comes from `flingFlight`), for the spec.

## 5. The live part (`src/portalhouselive.js`, new: `PortalHouseLive` = `LIVE.portalhouse`, `part:portalhouse`)

- `update(dt)`: its clock is summed frame dt while `game.playing()`, out of battles and with no panel open (R4's
  swirl freezes then). Draws: the Hub Swirl and Porch Swirl (`DoorGlow` flat, full: 3 draw calls each, only one of
  them in view range at a time), and **only the current part's** swirls (lite, 1 call each, at most 6), your swirl
  (teal, lite), the aim glow on the pad you look at (1), the sparks (1 instanced mesh). Meshes removed in `dispose`.
- Hooks: `near(p)` -> 'Swirl!' when a pad of your room is aimed at (section 3) and you have the Wand; `use(p)` puts your
  swirl there (sound `swirlzip`, a little zip of sparkles from you to the pad). No `holds`: one tap. A **new tiny
  generic hook `clicks(p)`** in `gymlive.js` (`each(o => o.clicks?.(p))`) and one line in `main.js`'s mouse-click path
  (`if (target.near && game.gymLive.clicks(game.player) && game.gymLive.use(game.player)) return;`), so a desktop click
  places the swirl too (other live parts never say `clicks`, so their mouse clicks are unchanged).
- Going through: teleport, `swirlwarp` sound, a puff (`puff` from creatures.js) at both ends, the camera turned for wall
  exits; `game.riding.stop('quiet')` first (riding is fenced out of the House anyway: its keeps are protect boxes).
- `stand`: the catch boxes, room starts (the Wand is given in the Hall: toast + `game.toldOnce`), done rooms.
- Room done: `beaten` key `pw:portal:room<n>` (n 1-4) saved at once (`game.save()`), toast "Room 2 done! 🌀 2 / 4".
  The Hall's doors: door n+1 is drawn open (`PO_SHUT` -> air with `setLive`, a door swirl mesh) once room n is done, so
  a reload or a later visit starts at the furthest room; the study door opens at 4 / 4 (`LiveCells`, never inside you).
- Score chip (`ui.setScore`) inside the House: "🌀 Rooms 2 / 4 · ✨ 5"; green "To Grandpa Arch!" at 4 / 4.
- `aim(p)`: in a room, the exit door swirl (or "Put your swirl on the white pad" when the room needs one first); in the
  Hall, the next door; done: the study.
- `battleEnded(result, opts)`: a won battle against Grandpa Arch clears the four room keys (like the Captain's boats),
  so a rematch is a new run. `info()` for tests: `{ room, swirl, clock, trips, sparks, done }`; `manual` + `step(dt)` like `RaceLive`.
- Everything is per player and never sent: a friend sees you vanish and pop out (the avatar's state), not your swirl.

## 6. Saved progress and the gate

- `beaten` keys `pw:portal:room1..4` (strings, pass `loadKeys`-style checks like the other `pw:` keys). Your swirl, the
  Wand, sparks and the R4 clock are not saved (a new trip: the Wand comes back in the Hall with one toast).
- `roomState(game)` -> `{ done: [b, b, b, b], n, need: 4, ready }`. The Boss def's `gate(game)`: "Finish all four swirl
  rooms first! (🌀 n / 4)" (the study door keeps you out anyway; the gate covers a friend who walked in).

**Rules added to `portalplay.js` (pure, closed form, Node-runnable, checked at 60 and 20 steps a second by the spec):**
`headOf(sw, speed)`: a fling out of a **ceiling** swirl carries `dropOf(speed)` = (speed / keep)² blocks of fall into the next swirl
(`flightDrop(f, y, head)`), so a floor-ceiling loop speeds up; every other face carries 0, so rooms 1-4 fly exactly as before. A
moving swirl or pad is `move: { axis, from, to, period }` (the old `{ y0, y1, period }` still means the y axis); `centreAt` is a
triangle wave of the room clock. The Swirl Ball: `BALL` (a 0.5 box, one every 3 s), `ballStart` / `ballNext` (one closed-form flight
piece at a time, worked out with your swirls as they are when the ball gets there; a piece that bumps a wall drops straight down;
`ballRests`), `ballRun` (the whole run, for the checks), `inCup`. `touches`, `exitOf`, `boxHits` and `flingFlight` take a box (the
player's by default; a ball comes out of a wall swirl's middle). `slotAimed` takes the pads to skip (both your swirls) and their
current middles (the sliding pad).
