# T41 · 🔱 Atlantis type and the Sunken City (in the Pirate Seas)

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `plans/T40.md` (the Picture World base, B.0-B.16), `plans/T40b.md` (the Pirate Seas: this task grows
that world), `plans/T40d.md` (a skill game whose progress is saved as `beaten` keys and a Boss `gate`), `ROADMAP.md`
"Wave 15" and `STORY.md` "The Picture Worlds" first.

**Owner's decisions (2026-10-27):** Atlantis is its own type (7 creatures), its own star, wave 15, **open from the start**
(no need to beat Captain Squawk first). The challenge must be a **skill game** like the football, basketball, race and
rowboats (the owner's son loved those; the puzzles began to feel the same), and it must feel **different from them**,
not just another "press and let go at the right moment".

**What the child does:** swims from the Pirate Seas (or steps out of the Atlantis frame in the Swirl) down into a
glowing crater in the sea where a sunken city lies dark. The city's lights were its **glowfish**, and they swam away.
The child **swims freely in 3D** through the ruins, **chases and catches glowfish with a net**, and brings them to the
three district lanterns. Each lit district lights up its part of the city (lamps, currents, a beam of light to the
palace). With all three lit, the palace dome opens, and **Queen Coralia** battles for the star. Nothing is ever lost:
a fish that gets away swims home and can be chased again.

## 0. What makes it different from the other skill games

| Game | What your body does | What you judge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Football | run into / kick a rolling ball | power (the ring) |
| Basketball | jump and throw | the top of the jump |
| Race | run a track against a rival | speed and the racing line |
| Rowboats | stand still and aim where you look | where a moving boat will be |
| **Glowfish** | **swim in 3D (up, down, any way you look) with kicks** | **distance to a fleeing fish (the lunge), and each fish kind needs its own trick** |

Four things, together, make it its own game:
1. **Real 3D swimming** (new, only in Atlantis water): you swim where you look, up and down too, and **kick** for a
   burst of speed that glides out (momentum). No other place moves you like this.
2. **A lunge, not a shot:** hold **Catch!** to coil (you slow down, the net glows brighter), let go to **lunge forward
   with the net**: the longer you held, the further you lunge (1.5 to 4 blocks). You judge the distance to a fish that
   is moving away; too short and it slips off, too long and you overshoot.
3. **Three kinds of fish, three tricks** (one per district, each district a bit harder):
   - **Jar Market: shy fish hide in clay jars.** Bump a jar and the fish shoots out in a straight line: a **reaction
     catch** (lunge at the right moment and direction as it pops out). Jars wobble before the fish comes out.
   - **Arch Gardens: darters** flee in fast zigzags; in open water you can never catch one. **Chase it into a dead end**
     (a broken house, an arch closed by fallen stones) and catch it when it has nowhere to go: **herding**.
   - **Temple Steps: a golden school** of 8 swirls in a ball that breathes (tight, then spread out). One lunge catches
     every fish inside the net: **lunge when it is tight** to catch many at once (greedy and fun).
4. **Your catch is your light:** the net glows brighter with every fish in it, and the deep parts of the city are dark,
   so the fish you carry light your way deeper. Pouring them into a lantern lights a whole district at once
   (lamps come on, a current starts, a beam goes up to the palace dome): big visible progress.

Never a fail: no breath meter, no drowning, no enemy fish. A fish that escapes goes home and waits.

## 1. The world: the Pirate Seas grow east

- **Size:** `REALMS.pirate.size` goes from `[224, 224]` to **`[320, 224]`**: a new strip x 224..319. Growing **east**
  keeps every existing x / z, so the islands, the ship, the cannons, the ropes and the rowboats do not move, and
  `pirateLand` stays block for block the same inside x < 224 (the spec checks it, like `big-map` does for T32).
- **Old saves:** realm edits are indexed by size, so `REALMS.pirate.version` becomes 2 **and v1 edits are converted,
  not dropped** (a small `convertRealmEdits(list, [224, 224], [320, 224])` next to `cleanRealmEdits`, used when the
  saved version is 1 and the def says `convert: { 1: [224, 224] }`; same idea as `convertEdits`). A save made in
  the Pirate Seas keeps its position (`realmPos` is still right, since x / z did not move).
- **The crater:** centre about (264, 112), radius about 44, sea floor carved down to **y 1** (the rest of the sea floor
  is y 4-7, water to y 12, and `WORLD_H` is 48): about 11 blocks of water above the city, enough for swimming up and
  down. The city spreads wide instead of tall. A ring of rock and coral round the rim, glowing a little so the crater
  is found from the islands.
- **Getting there from the Pirate Seas:** open sea east of Palm Key; a **whirlpool ring** of bubbles over the rim (a
  lift box pushing down, the opposite of a ladder's lift) takes you down quickly. Bubble columns (normal lifts) take
  you back up. `pirateAim` points at it when the player is east of Palm Key or asks for Atlantis.
- **Its own frame:** `secret2` in `pictureworlds.js` becomes `{ id: 'atlantis', name: 'Atlantis', emoji: '🔱', realm:
  'pirate', place: 'Pirate Seas', ... }` (no save can hold `secret2`'s star yet, so the 12 stars stay 12; the frame
  already exists, so no Swirl version bump). Its arrival `world.arrivals.atlantis` is in a **diving bell** on the
  crater rim: a dry glass room with the way back door, then a step into the water.
- **Under water looks under water:** when the camera is in water inside a realm with `look.underwater`, the fog turns
  blue-green and close, and the light dims with depth (`updateSky` reads it). Main world, Dream and Swirl unchanged.
- **The city (all data, `src/atlantisland.js`, Node-runnable like `pirateland.js`):** three districts round a
  palace in the middle: **Jar Market** (west, nearest the bell: stalls, jars, low walls), **Arch Gardens** (north:
  arches, columns, broken houses with dead ends), **Temple Steps** (south: wide stairs, a temple front). The **palace**
  sits in the middle under a **glass dome** (dry inside, like a sky gym but under water): the throne room is where the
  battle happens, so no battle is ever under water.
- **Darkness:** the districts are dark until lit (`world.litCol` off in the crater, lamp blocks drawn as unlit with
  `setLive` until the district is lit). The net's glow is the player's own light (`caveLight.uLantern` grows with the
  fish carried).

## 2. Swimming in Atlantis (`Player`, one switch)

- A realm world may set `world.deepWater = box` (the crater). Inside it, `Player.update` uses **dive swimming**: the
  move direction follows the look (pitch too), no sinking (neutral, a slow drift down when idle), speed about 3.2.
- **Kick:** Jump (Space, the touch Jump button, which says **Swim** there) gives a burst of 7 forward that eases out
  over 0.8 s; at most one kick every 0.5 s. Holding Jump with no stick still swims up, as now (old habit works).
- Closed-form easing, checked at 60 and 20 steps a second (as the pirate gadgets were).
- Outside `deepWater` nothing changes (the rest of the Pirate Seas swims as before).

## 3. The glowfish (`src/glowfish.js`, pure, no imports, Node-runnable)

- Every fish is a **closed form of the part's own clock** (summed dt, frozen in battles / asks / panels / pause) plus
  its state, never `Math.random` (a hash of the fish index): `home`, `kind`, `state` (`home` / `out` / `flee` /
  `stuck` / `caught`).
- **Shy (Jar Market, 4 fish):** sits in a jar (a `JAR` block). Bump or hit the jar: it wobbles 0.6 s, then the fish
  shoots out in a straight line (speed 6, 4 blocks, direction away from you plus a fixed turn from the hash), slows,
  and swims back into its jar after 3 s. Catchable while out.
- **Darter (Arch Gardens, 4 fish):** wanders slowly; within 6 blocks it flees at 7 (faster than your kick) in zigzags
  that never go into walls. When its flee direction has no open water (a dead end, checked on the walk grid), it is
  `stuck` for 2.5 s, trembling: catch it then. Dead ends are data (`DEAD_ENDS`), at least 6 in the gardens.
- **School (Temple Steps, 8 fish = 4 lights):** a ball of 8 round a point that drifts slowly along the steps; its
  radius breathes between 0.6 and 2.2 (a 3 s cycle, slower in the first round). A lunge catches every fish inside the
  net's sphere (radius 1.1) at that moment. The school counts as caught when all 8 are in (two fish = one light).
- **The net:** hold **Catch!** (`gymLive.holds` / `release`, like Kick! and Swing!) to coil (speed x 0.4, the net
  glows); let go = lunge `1.5 + 2.5 x min(1, held / 0.9 s)` blocks along the look over 0.25 s (`moveAxis`, stops at
  walls), the net's sphere sweeps the lunge path. A quick tap is a short swipe (1.5).
- **Carrying:** up to 6 fish in the net (they show as glowing dots round it). Swim into a district's **lantern** (a big
  glass lamp in the district's middle) to pour them in. A district needs 4 lights.

## 4. Saved progress and the gate

- Every light poured in is a `beaten` key saved at once: `pw:atlantis:light1..12` (district 0: 1-4, 1: 5-8, 2: 9-12),
  like the stadium's goals. Fish in the net but not yet poured are not saved (leaving the crater lets them swim home).
- `lightState(game)` -> `{ lit: [n0, n1, n2], done }`. The Boss def's `gate(game)` waits for all 12 (the palace door is
  drawn shut with `LiveCells` until then; the guide says which district is next).
- A won Boss battle clears the 12 keys (`battleEnded`, as the Captain clears his boats), so a rematch is a new catch.
- Score chip (`ui.setScore`): "Lights 5 / 12" in the crater; green "To the palace!" at 12.

## 5. Queen Coralia and her guards

- A person Boss (`look: 'crown'` + a trident), on a `BOSS_STAND` before the throne, top level 64, Atlantis team
  ending with Guardlion; rematches grow (`bossWins`, `REMATCH_CAP` 70). Two optional crew: **Guard Pelos** (Jar
  Market) and **Guard Talia** (Temple Steps), bonus battles.
- Winning: the star, `ui.celebrate`, the Wizard's note; the palace dome glows and all city lamps stay lit for the trip.
- Losing: `lose: 'stay'` (nearest rest spot, healed). Rest spots: the diving bell, the palace hall.

## 6. The Atlantis type (7 creatures: 3 families of 2 + 1)

The creatures are **the city's old guardians**, woken by its glow: statues, jars and tiles that came alive. **None is a
fish, a crab or a sailor** (Water and Pirate already have those). Every model carries **teal glowing rune lines**
(`gear: 'rune'`), the way Pirate creatures all wear the bandana.

| # | Base | Evolved | Shape (new) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Jarling** | **Grand Urnling** | `jar`: a clay jar with eyes peeking out → a tall painted vase, arms from the handles | lives in the Jar Market |
| 2 | **Statuecub** | **Guardlion** | `stonelion`: a little stone lion → a big guardian lion, a mane of stone waves, glowing eyes | rideable (land), Coralia's ace |
| 3 | **Tilesnek** | **Mosaicoil** | `mosaic`: a snake of loose tiles with gaps between them → a long one whose tiles rattle and shimmer | tiles drift a little apart, like Dream's `drift` but tight |
| 7 | **Runestone** | (none) | `runestone`: a floating stone with a glowing mark, spinning slowly | rare, Temple Steps |

- **Chart (proposal):** strong against Pirate (guardians chase treasure hunters off), Treasure and Time (it is older
  than clocks); weak to **Cyber** and **Ninja** only (Water stays neutral both ways, so it never feels like a water
  type). Resists Pirate and Ball.
- **Moves:** Jar Bonk, Tile Toss, Stone Roar, Rune Glow, Ancient Echo (raise), sig **Sunken Bell** (Guardlion) and
  **Mosaic Storm** (Mosaicoil, multi 2-5); big moves the usual 7 kinds via `BIG_MOVES.atlantis`. Check no id is a
  sound name.
- **Usual per-type cost:** `TYPE_COLORS`, `EFFECTIVENESS`, wool colour, `TRAVEL` / `IMPACT` / `FX_COLORS`, `fx:atlantis`
  sound, Book, README tables. Spawns only in `REALMS.pirate.spawns.atlantis` (`world.realmBiome` returns `atlantis`
  inside the crater), on the city floor.

## 9. Spec `atlantis`

- The Pirate Seas inside x < 224 are block for block the same as before (three seeds); v1 edits convert; an old save
  in the Pirate Seas keeps its spot.
- Terrain for three seeds: the crater, every district reachable by swimming (a 3D BFS), every dead end really traps a
  darter, the whirlpool and bubble columns, the bell and the palace dry, nobody stuck.
- Swimming: dive swimming and kicks with real Player physics at 60 and 20 steps a second; outside `deepWater`
  unchanged.
- Fish: each kind caught by the intended trick and **not** by the wrong one (a darter in open water is never caught;
  a lunge into a spread school catches few, a tight one many); 60 / 20 fps give the same result.
- Saved lights through a reload, the gate, Queen Coralia, the star, keys cleared after the win, losing stays.
- iPad: Catch! held on the action button, Swim on the Jump button; two browsers (each player has their own fish).
- Screenshots `t41-*.png` for the owner (the dark city, a district lighting up, the school, the palace).
