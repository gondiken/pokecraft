# T44 · 🎁 Surprise type and the Room of Doors

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `plans/wave15.md` (the shared rules: type id `surprise`, wool **235**, block ids **235-243**, tile
prefix `SU_`, realm id `doors`), `ROADMAP.md` "Wave 15", `STORY.md` "The Picture Worlds" (the Room of Doors),
`plans/T40.md` (the Picture World base, B.0-B.16), `plans/T40a.md` (a realm of its own, live parts, the `peek` gait),
`plans/T40d.md` (`beaten` keys and the Boss `gate`) and `plans/T41.md` (the house style) first.

**Owner context (2026-10-27):** the son loved the **skill games** (football, basketball, race, rowboats); the puzzles
began to feel the same. So the Room of Doors mixes **fun surprises** (open a door: what is behind it?) with **two skill
games** that are new for the game: **tracking** (the shuffled doors) and **quick reaction** (the pop-up boxes). Neither is
"hold and let go" (Kick!, Swing!, Fire! already use that). Fun, never scary, never a fail, iPad with the one action
button.

**What the child does:** steps out of the gift-wrapped frame in the Swirl into a big, bright toy-box hall with
**twelve doors** round its walls and a **Big Gift** behind the gold door in the north. Every door opens on a surprise: a
spiral slide, a ball pit, a confetti shower, a room of springs, a door inside a door inside a door, a shop of gift boxes
that are really creatures, Healer Stella, and two trainers who jump out "Surprise! Battle!". In the middle of the hall
is the **Shuffle Stage**: a golden bow hides behind one of three doors, the doors whizz round and swap places, and
the child picks the one with the bow. Behind the east gold door is the **Pop-Up Room**: jacks spring out of boxes and
the child boops them before they duck back. **Four golden bows** untie the four ribbons of the Big Gift; it bursts open
and **Jolly Jax**, a giant jack-in-the-box, battles for the star.

Nothing in the main world, the Dream or the Swirl changes (no `WORLD_VERSION` bump, no Swirl version bump: the `doors`
frame already exists). Battle rules do not change (duel `VERSION` stays 11).

## 0. What makes it different

| Game | What your body does | What you judge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Football / basketball | run into a ball, hold and let go | power, the top of the jump |
| Race | run a track | speed, the racing line |
| Rowboats / cannons / ropes | stand and aim, hold and let go | where a moving thing will be, the swing |
| Glowfish (T41) | swim in 3D, lunge | distance to a fleeing fish |
| **Shuffle Stage** | **stand still and watch, then look at a door and press once** | **where the prize went: keep your eyes on one door while the others swap** |
| **Pop-Up Room** | **turn and press fast** | **which box popped: react and aim before it ducks** |

- **One press, no hold.** Both games use a single press of the action button (E, click, a tap on the screen or the
  big button). No power ring, no timing a release.
- **Two new skills.** Tracking (attention, like the old cup game) and reaction (like whack-a-mole, but kind: nobody
  is hit, a jack is *booped* on the nose and giggles).
- **The surprise is the reward and the "miss".** A wrong door never says "wrong": out pops a silly surprise (a
  rubber duck, bubbles, a sock on a spring) and the right door opens too ("It was here!"). Then "Again!".
- **Exploring is part of it.** The twelve doors are a seeded shuffle per world: nobody knows which room is behind
  which door until it is opened (but the four doors that matter are gold and always in the same place).

## 2. The Shuffle Stage (`src/shuffle.js`, pure, no imports, Node-runnable)

**How it plays:** stand on the watch pad, the big button says **Play!**. The doors stand **open** in a row on the stage
and a golden bow sits behind one (1.6 s, the bow bobs and sparkles). The doors **close** (0.5 s) and **shuffle**: two
doors swap places, one gliding round the front, the other round the back (half ellipses, so they never pass through
each other), again and again. Then they stop and wiggle. The button says **This one!**: the door you look at glows
(an outline); press to open it.
- **Right:** the bow flies to you, confetti, `rightdoor` fanfare, "You found it! 🎀 2 / 4", the bow key is saved.
- **Not right:** that door opens on a silly surprise (rubber duck squeak, bubbles, a sock on a spring, a little
  cloud; from a fixed list by the attempt hash), and the bow's door opens too: "It was here! Again?" -> **Again!**
  (a new shuffle). Never "wrong", never a loss.

**Three rounds, one bow each** (`SHUFFLE.rounds`; round n = the first bow not won yet):

| Round | Doors | Door look | Swaps | One swap | Extra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 | three colours (red, blue, green) | 4 | 0.9 s | - |
| 2 | 4 | two colours, alternating | 6 | 0.7 s | - |
| 3 | 5 | all the same colour | 8 | 0.55 s | two swaps at once now and then (`doubles`) |

- **Help (rubber band):** after 2 misses in a row in a round every swap is x 1.35 slower and one swap shorter
  (`SHUFFLE.help`); after 4, x 1.7 and the prize door gets a tiny twinkle while it moves. The help goes away after a win.
- **Rematch** (`hard`, from `bossWins.doors > 0`): +2 swaps per round, swap times x 0.9 (never faster than 0.45 s).
- **Pure functions** (all closed form of a time `t` in seconds since the shuffle started):
  - `shufflePlan(seed, round, attempt, { help, hard })` -> `{ n, prize, swaps: [{ a, b, t0, dur }], end, look }`:
    `hash32(\`${seed}|${round}|${attempt}\`)` drives every choice (no `Math.random`). Rules: the prize door takes part in
    at least `ceil(swaps / 2)` swaps; never the same pair three times in a row; for rounds 2-3 the prize never ends where
    it started; `doubles` only uses disjoint pairs.
  - `doorsAt(plan, t)` -> `[{ slot, x, z, open }]` for every door (slot offsets `SPACING` 3 along the stage, front/back
    ellipse `depth` 1.4), `slotOf(plan, door)` at the end.
  - `pickDoor(doors, eye, dir, reach = 14)` -> the door index the look ray hits (door boxes 2.4 wide x 3.2 high), or
    the nearest within 8 degrees (a child's aim is wobbly), or null.
  - `SHUFFLE` numbers in one object (the spec reads them).
- **The seed** is the player's own: `game.missionSeed()`-style main-world seed mixed with `realmSeed`, so two players
  get different shuffles and a reload with the same attempt counter replays the same one (tests).

## 3. The Pop-Up Room (`src/popup.js`, pure)

**How it plays:** behind the east gold door, a 3 x 3 grid of `POP_BOX` blocks (1 apart) on a low stage at the back of
the room; a pad 4 blocks in front of it. On the pad the button says **Play!**. Then jacks pop up, one at a time (later
two): the lid flips (`POP_BOX_OPEN` with `setLive`) and a grinning jack on a spring bounces out (one `InstancedMesh`, 9
instances). Look at it and press **Boop!** (or tap it on the screen) before it ducks back: it giggles, its nose
flashes, +1. Some boxes only **wobble** and nothing comes out ("cheeky box!"); booping one does nothing bad.

- **A round = 20 pops, 10 boops win the bow** (`POPUP.round`, `POPUP.need`). The score chip says "🤡 6 / 10 · 14 left".
- Up time per pop: 1.4 s at the start down to 0.75 s near the end (`POPUP.up`), 0.5 s gap; from pop 12 on, every
  fourth pop is a double. A round lasts about 40 s.
- **Not 10?** "You booped 7! Again? They will be a bit slower." The next round's up times are x 1.25 (x 1.5 after two),
  like the shuffle's help. Rematch: x 0.9, 12 to win.
- **Pure functions:** `popPlan(seed, attempt, { help, hard })` -> `[{ box, t0, up, wobble }]` (hashed, no box twice in a
  row, doubles never the same box), `popAt(plan, t)` -> which boxes are up / wobbling at `t`, `boopOk(plan, t, box)`.
- **Aim** is the normal block target: the crosshair (or a tap) on a `POP_BOX` / `POP_BOX_OPEN` cell -> `gymLive.hit`,
  `action(block)` = 'Boop!', `prompt` "Boop the jack!". A boop also counts for the 0.15 s after the jack ducks (a
  child's tap at 1-2 fps in a test, and on a slow iPad, lands a frame late).

## 4. The live part: `DoorsLive` = `LIVE.doors` (`src/doorslive.js`, new)

- Made from `world.parts` (`part:doors`), like `HouseLive`. Its clock is summed `dt`, frozen while `!game.playing()`,
  in a battle, an ask or a panel (`game.playing()`).
- **Doors:** `GIFT_DOOR` / `GOLD_DOOR` cells drawn as `AIR` with `LiveCells` when opened (never solid inside you).
  `prompt(block)` "Open the door!", `action(block)` 'Open!', `hit(block)` opens it with a little wrapping-paper rip
  (`giftopen`) and the room's surprise (a toast, a sound, confetti). Open doors stay open for the trip (not saved; a
  new trip closes them again, but the gift chests remember: `pw:doors:gift*`). The trainers jump forward 1 block
  when their door opens (`npc.steer` like the Dojo's people, 0.3 s).
- **Shuffle and Pop-Up:** `near(p)` gives 'Play!' / 'This one!' / 'Again!' on the watch pad, 'Play!' on the pop-up pad,
  'Slide!' at the top of the slide; `use(p)` acts. **No `holds` / `release`.** Meshes: the stage doors (one merged
  geometry with vertex colours, an `InstancedMesh` of 5, instance colours for the look), the bow, the jacks
  (`InstancedMesh` 9), confetti (camera-facing quads in one `InstancedMesh`, 160, closed-form fall from the burst time),
  the pit balls (`InstancedMesh` 120). All removed in `dispose`.
- **The Big Gift:** draws its four ribbons from the saved bows (`RIBBON` -> `GIFT_WALL` with `setLive` per bow);
  `hit` on it says the gate words; at 4 bows the front (5 x 4 cells) opens with `LiveCells`, confetti, `jackpop`, and
  the Boss is inside. The Boss's model bounces up once (a scale-y spring, 0.6 s) the first time the front opens in a
  trip.
- `talk(npc)` / `talkPrompt` only for the crew's "Surprise!" line; `battleEnded(result, opts)` clears the bows after a
  won Boss battle; `aim(p)` = `doorsAim`; `info()` for tests (`{ round, attempt, phase, plan, doors, popup, score }`);
  `manual` + `step(dt)` for tests (like `RaceLive`).
- Score chip (`ui.setScore`): "🎀 2 / 4" in the hall; "🤡 6 / 10" in the Pop-Up Room; green "Open the Big Gift!" at 4.
- One-time hints via `game.toldOnce`: "Keep your eyes on the bow!" (first shuffle), "Boop the jacks before they hide!"

## 5. Wild Surprise creatures start as gift boxes (`creatures.js` T44 section, `GAITS.gift`)

- Every Surprise species has `gait: 'gift'` (the T37 hand-over line style: `if (w.species.gait === 'gift') return
  this.updateGift(w, dt);`). On spawn the model's `root` is hidden and a **gift box** (from `buildGiftWrap(paper)` in
  the T44 section of `shapes.js`: a 0.9 cube, a ribbon cross, a bow, one merged mesh with vertex colours, shared
  geometry per paper) is added as a child: `w.wrapped = true`. **All seven species use the same box**, and the paper is
  picked from `hash32(uid)` (`PAPERS`, 6 papers), so you cannot tell what is inside... except that **gold paper**
  (1 box in 8) always holds a rarer one (an evolved form or Partypop): a happy little tell (open question 3).
- **The box wobbles** now and then (closed form in `w.t`, phase from the uid), and when you are within 3 blocks it
  hops and giggles (`giftgiggle`, quiet). It never moves away. Bumps are noticed (`bump: true`) but never battle
  (owner 2026-10-26 rule): E, a click or a tap opens it.
- **Opening = the battle start:** `startBattle({ wild })` -> `Battle.start` calls `wild.reveal?.()`. For a wrapped
  creature `Wild.reveal()` (one marked branch) removes the box with a lid-pop (the lid flies up, confetti, `giftopen`),

  \`Surprise! The gift was a wild ${name}!\` : was ? ...` (Camo text unchanged).
- **The name tag:** `main.js` line ~1107: `target.wild.wrapped ? \`A gift box! 🎁 · ${how} to open\`` (one marked line)
  and the button word 'Open!'. The Book marks a species seen only when it is opened (the battle's `refresh()` does that

- **Spawns:** only in `REALMS.doors.spawns.doors`, on the `nook` condition (reused: `place` = `creatures.nookNear`):

  ['bowtower', 0.12, 'nook'], ['jestergrin', 0.1, 'nook'], ['fiestallama', 0.1, 'nook']]`. The Book's row: "In the Room

### Bows 1-3: the Piñata Party (`src/pinata.js`, pure)

**What the child does.** In the middle of the hall is a round party rug. Stand on it, press **Play!**: a bunch of balloons floats
down holding a piñata on a rope (a llama, then a star, then a rocket). The balloons drift round the rug and the piñata **swings**
under them like a pendulum: low in the middle of a swing (in reach of your party stick), high at the ends. Press **Whack!** (E, a
click, the big button, or tap the piñata on the iPad): the stick swings the way you look.
- Hit it while it swings **at you**, low = **CRACK!** (counts). Hit it any other way = **Bonk!** (it only flies off). Miss = a
  whoosh and the stick takes a moment to come back (2 s: whacking all the time does not work).
- Every hit knocks the piñata away **the way you hit it**, so it swings back at you half a swing later (a rhythm, like
  tetherball) ... but the balloons **zip** on 2-5 blocks along their path after every hit, so it comes back somewhere else: run to
  meet it, face it, wait for it, CRACK. From the star on it sometimes **dodges** (the knock turns 70 degrees), the rocket's
  balloons sometimes zip back.
- Enough CRACKs and it bursts: candy rains, a surprise falls out (a rubber duck, a sock on a spring, bubbles, a little cloud) and
  the golden bow flies to you. Time up = "The piñata went up for a nap. Again? It will swing slower." (never a loss).

**Why it is skill, not a puzzle:** timing (the moment it comes at you, low), angle (face it: the stick hits where you look, and
where you hit it is where it swings back from), and directional tactics (read where the balloons go and stand on the far side of
the swing). Mashing loses: a whiff costs 2 s, a bonk costs a swing.

**Unlike every other world:** a melee hit on a moving pendulum. The rowboats are a held-power cannon at a target far away, the
Stadium a held kick / jump timing on a ball you push, the race running a track, Atlantis a lunge net on a fleeing fish, the Funfair
bumpers / bouncing / rings from a ride, the Portal House placing swirls, Doodle tracing lines. Nothing else swings at you.

| Piñata | CRACKs | swing (s) | balloons (b/s) | path | zip (blocks) | dodge | time (s) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Llama | 5 | 2.8 | 0.8 | circle r 2.5 | 2.4-3.6 | - | 40 |
| 2 Star | 7 | 2.35 | 1.4 | figure eight 4.2 x 2.6 | 2.8-4.2 | 25% | 45 |
| 3 Rocket | 9 | 1.95 | 2.1 | wander 4.2 x 3.8 (doubles back) | 3.2-5 (30% back) | 35% | 55 |

Stick: reach 2.1 sideways from your eyes, 50 degrees either side of the look (closer than 0.9 always), the piñata between 1.45 below
and 0.95 above your eyes; a press counts for 0.04 s before to 0.16 s after (the stick is in the air; slow iPads). CRACK = coming
at you at 1.5+ blocks a second and within 0.4 of the bottom of the swing. Rope 4.6, the bottom of the swing at y 12.9 (eyes 12.6).
**Help** after each time-up (per piñata): swing x1.2 / x1.4 slower, balloons x0.7 / x0.45, zips x0.75 / x0.5, one / two CRACKs
fewer; it goes away with the bow. **Rematch** (Jax beaten before): +2 CRACKs, swing x0.9, balloons x1.2, zips x1.15.
**Randomness:** the path's turn and phase, its direction, the first swing, every zip and dodge come from a hash of
seed | piñata | attempt | hit number (`seeded`), never `Math.random`: each try is different, a replay of the same presses is the same.

### Bow 4: Jack Catch in the Pop-Up Room (`src/jackcatch.js`, pure)

**What the child does.** Stand on the pad, **Play!**: the nine pop-up boxes start to fling jacks-in-the-box high across the room
(the lid flips, *pop*). Each jack's **shadow** runs over the floor and grows as it comes down: run under it and it lands in your arms

**Why it is skill:** reading an arc and running to where it will come down (like a fly ball), choosing between two jacks at once,
and dodging a pie that leads you. **Unlike every other world:** you catch with your body, the throws come at you, you run a small
room with boxes in the way.

## Kind help (owner 2026-10-28)

"Making it easier a bit each time something goes wrong, but only ever so slightly; with the shield game, after failing 6 times it became pretty easy. We should ask a bit of grit / patience." Every skill game now takes its help from the one shared rule in `src/kindhelp.js` (`KIND_HELP = { after: 2, step: 0.02, max: 0.10 }`: nothing for 0-1 misses, then 2 / 6 / 10 % easier after 2 / 4 / 6 misses, never more); this game only maps `kindHelp(misses).slow` / `.more` onto its own levers (the table is in the module comment). The older, stronger help numbers here are replaced by it; a rematch's `hard` is unchanged. Spec `kindhelp`.
