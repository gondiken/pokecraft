# T40a · 🐜 Tiny type + the Giant House (plan)

Status: ready to build after the T40 base is on the branch. Every open question has a default ("Decided", section 13),
so no builder waits on the owner.

Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, `plans/T40.md` (Step 0 and **the whole "Base" section**: this plan uses its APIs by name),
`ROADMAP.md` "Wave 14", `STORY.md` "The Picture Worlds" (the Giant House). Models: `plans/T35.md` (a type + a realm
terrain as data), `plans/T37.md` (type + gait), `src/dreamland.js` / `src/swirlland.js` (terrain as data, Node-runnable),
`src/sneak.js` (`Watcher`), `src/livecells.js`, `src/creatures.js` (`GAITS`, the T37 `blink` hand-over).

**In one line:** seven tiny creatures that hide in cups, flowers and under leaves and peek out; and the **Giant House**,
the Wizard's kitchen seen by his pet mouse: you ride a toy train, float down a tap river on a bottle cap, get blown up
to a shelf by a fan, tiptoe past a sleeping cat, find two secret ways (a mouse hole, a keyhole) and twelve crumbs, and
battle **Nibbles, the Wizard's Mouse**, on a giant cookie on the table for the first star.

Nothing in the main world, the Dream or the Swirl changes (no `WORLD_VERSION` bump, no realm `version` bump but the
house's own `version: 1`). No battle rule changes (duel `VERSION` stays 11).

## 1. Step A: the Tiny type

Base data already on the branch: `TYPE_COLORS.tiny = '#e6c08a'`, wool `WOOL.tiny` = 140, chart row
`tiny: { racer: 2, food: 2, cyber: 2, tiny: 0.5, fire: 0.5, sky: 0.5 }` (base correction: `food`, not `metal`), and
`fire.tiny 2`, `sky.tiny 2`, `ball.tiny 2`. Tiny is weak to fire, sky and ball. Do not touch the chart.

### 1.1 The type look: "tiny, with big shiny eyes"

Every Tiny creature is small (scale 0.4-0.85, so a whole one is 0.35-1.1 high) and has **oversized round eyes with a
white glint** (a helper `tnEyes(g, x, y, z, r, color)` in the T40a section of `shapes.js`: a dark box, a smaller white
box up-left in front of it; used by all four shapes). Each family has its own body shape (owner: no recolours).

### 1.6 The `peek` gait ("you find them by looking")

`GAITS.peek = { notice: 4.5, noticeUp: 3, every: 4.5, show: 1.4, keep: 3, bump: true }` in the T40a section of
`creatures.js`; `updateGait`'s first lines get `if (w.species.gait === 'peek') return this.updatePeek(w, dt); // T40a`.
`updatePeek(w, dt)` (closed form in `w.t`, no `Math.random`):
- It never moves (stays in its nook cell).
- **Hiding** (you are further than `notice` sideways or `noticeUp` up/down): the model is visible only while it peeks,
  `((w.t + w.phase) % every) < show`, where `w.phase = (hash32(w.data.uid) % 1000) / 1000 * every` (`traits.js`
  `hash32`). During a peek the root rises from -0.25 to 0 in the first 0.25 s and sinks back in the last 0.25 s, and
  the model turns to face you. Otherwise `model.visible = false` (so it cannot be picked: `pick` already skips hidden
  parts).
- **Out** (you come within `notice`): visible, faces you, idle animation, `w.keep = keep`; when you step away it stays
  out for `keep` seconds, then hides again.
- Battles: tap / click it while visible, or walk into it (the T36 edge-triggered bump, `bump: true`).
- `frozen` (in a battle): always visible, as today (`updateOne` handles `frozen` before gaits).
- One-time tip (main.js `onSpawn` is not needed): `HouseLive` toasts once per visit when a wild creature first comes
  within 12 blocks: "Tiny creatures hide in cups, flowers and under leaves. Look closely!"

## 2. Step B: the Giant House realm

### 2.1 The scale

**1 block = 3 cm of real house.** You (and Nibbles, and the Wizard's real mouse) are 1.8 blocks = 5.5 cm. Then:
table top 75 cm = y 25, kitchen counter 90 cm = y 30, the shelf 1 m = y 33, a desk fan 12 cm housing, the sleeping cat

## 3. The gadgets: `HouseLive` (`src/houselive.js`, `LIVE.house`)

### 3.0 Wiring

`gymlive.js`: `import { HouseLive } from './houselive.js'; // T40a` and `LIVE.house = HouseLive; // T40a`. The base
makes it from `world.parts` (`{ id: 'house', live: 'house' }`): `new HouseLive(game, part)`; it reads
`game.world.house`. Interface: `update(dt)`, `hit(block)`, `prompt(block, how)`, `action(block)`, `dispose()`. It
imports `LiveCells` from `livecells.js`, `Watcher` from `sneak.js`, `Mover` / `PATHS` from `mover.js`, `THREE`, `B`.

**Clock:** `this.clock += dt` only while `!game.inBattle` (every timing below is a closed form of it or of a Mover's own
clock). Block writes go through `LiveCells` (`this.cells = new LiveCells(world, () => game.player)`; `flush()` every
update) and only when a cell's wanted state changes. Meshes (train, cap, fan blades, wind, zzz) are made in the
constructor and removed in `dispose()`. Draw calls added: **9** (train 3, cap 1, blades 1, wind 1, zzz 3).

**Per visit:** gadget states start fresh with every new world (fan off, tap off, train at S, cat asleep); only the
crumbs are saved (3.5). Everything is per player (like gym live parts): a friend sees their own train.

### 3.1 The fan (wind lifts you to the shelf)

- The switch (`FAN_SWITCH` / `FAN_SWITCH_ON`) toggles `fanOn`. On: `world.lifts.push(FAN.lift)` (the T38a lift rule in
  `Player.update`: you rise at up to 6 blocks/s until your feet are at `y1 + 1` = 34, then bob there; walk north onto
  the shelf at z 124); off: remove it from `world.lifts` (you fall back to the grill, no harm). Sound `whoosh` on.
- Blades: one flat 4-blade mesh at y 4.05 over the grill, `rotation.y += dt * (fanOn ? 14 : 0.5)`. Wind: one
  additive box over the lift box with a scrolling streak texture (`offset.y = -clock * 1.2`), opacity 0.35, only while on.
- Words: prompt `Fan switch · ${how} to turn it ${fanOn ? 'off' : 'on'}`, action `fanOn ? 'Fan off' : 'Fan on!'`.
- First time within 10 blocks per visit: toast "A giant fan! Turn it on and ride the wind up to the shelf."

### 3.2 The toy train (press the giant remote)

Two `Mover`s (base B.10), `run: 'ride'`, `canLeave: true`, `mesh: null` (the live part draws the cars itself):
`toN` with `PATHS.line({ points: <TRACK.points cut from stop S to stop N>, speed: 9 })` and `toS` (the same points
reversed). The full `TRACK.points` polyline (with its stubs past both stops) is `this.trackLine = PATHS.line({ points:
TRACK.points, speed: 1 })` (so `at(s).x/y/z` is the point at arc length `s`) and only draws the cars.

State: `train = { at: 'S' | 'N', run: null | { dir: 'N' | 'S', mover: toN | toS | null, t0 } }`.
- **Ride:** press a remote while the train stands at that station and you are within 4 blocks of its stop:
  `game.riding.stop('quiet')`, toast "All aboard! Choo choo!", sound `choochoo`, `mover.board(game.player)`.
  The Mover carries you (base: `player.carry`, position = `where()`, no collision; jump with a fresh press to get off).
  At the path end the Mover puts you at the other stop and calls `onEnd` -> `train.at` flips, toast "Here we are!".
- **Call:** press a remote while the train is at the other station: an empty run (no Mover; `s(t) = sFrom + dir *
  (clock - t0) * 9`), toast "Here it comes!"; on arrival `train.at` flips.
- **Busy:** pressing while it runs: toast "The train is on its way!". Pressing a remote far from the train at its
  station: "Stand by the train, then press the button!" (still within REACH of the button means you are close: the
  remotes sit 3 blocks from the stops).
- **Cars:** engine + 2 wagons (each one merged geometry with vertex colours, built from boxes by a small local helper:
  red engine with a chimney and a yellow cab, blue and green wagons, black wheels). The rider sits in the first wagon:
  wagon 1 at the rider's arc length `s`, the engine at `s + 5 * dir`, wagon 2 at `s - 5 * dir`, each turned to the
  line's yaw at that point. Parked at a station: `s` = that stop.
- Times (speed 9): S -> N is about 260 blocks, ~29 s. The ramp line is
  `feet y = 1.5 + (165 - x) / 2` from x 165 to x 137 (above every step: the spec samples it), then 15.5 on the bench.
- Words on a `REMOTE_BUTTON`: here -> prompt `Toy train remote · ${how} to ride`, action `'Ride!'`; there ->

## 13. Decided (planner's recommendations; the orchestrator may overrule)

1. **Species:** ant (Antsy -> Antlas), thimble mouse (Thimbit -> Spoolmouse), dust bunny (Dustbun -> Fluffnado), and a
   seed as the single-stage 7th (Pipsqueak). **No ladybug:** it would look too much like Bug's Beetlet (owner: unique
   shapes); it can come back later as a swap.
2. **4 new shapes** (`ant`, `thimblemouse`, `dustbunny`, `seedling`) with a shared type look: big shiny eyes, small size.
3. **Moves:** `teenytap`, `mightymite`, `tinysteps` (1-5 hits), `tuckaway` (defence up); sigs `sugarlift`,
   `fluffstorm`; big moves shield / rally / field. No battle rule change, no duel bump.
4. **Hiding in the wild = a `peek` gait + a `nook` spawn place** (not the Camo disguise: a disguise hides a creature as
   a block, a Tiny one should be seen peeking out of a real cup). Creatures appear only in `world.nooks` cells.
5. **Scale 1 block = 3 cm**, realm **192 x 192**, gravity 1, no ceiling (cream sky), bright everywhere (`light 0.95`).
6. **Routes:** train + cat (guided), fan + shelf + river + spoon (found by looking), walk the ramp (always works); the
   **mouse hole** (floor -> shelf) and the **keyhole** (floor -> counter) are secret shortcuts, never guided.
7. **The cat** = a `Watcher` that never walks; running on the bench wakes it for 5 s and its tail blocks the strip:
   you wait. Never a puff, never sent back.
8. **Train and bottle cap are base `Mover`s** (per player); the train boards with the remote ("Ride!" / "Call
   train!"); the cap boards when you stand on it (it is a mesh, so no button).
9. **Tap water** is drawn with `setLive` by a closed form of the clock (fills in ~7 s, drains the same way); the dry
   groove is always walkable.
10. **Fan and tap toggle** (on / off, per visit); everything resets on each visit except the crumbs.
11. **Crumbs: 12, saved once each** as `pw:house:crumb<i>` in `beaten` (no new save field); +1 cube each, +5 for all 12.
12. **Boss Nibbles, the Wizard's Mouse** (a person with a mouse look), 4 creatures 59-62, no crew.
13. **Keeps** over every gadget and way; the open floor stays free to build on; the floor track is not a keep.
14. The guide's default route is **the train** (riding was the big hit).
