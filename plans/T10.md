## 2. Placement (`world.js`)
### 2.1 Rules (old worlds)
1. `extraGymSpots()` loops `k < EXTRA_GYMS_END` (not `GYMS.length`), so it keeps returning exactly the two
   legacy spots (reserved bone spot, Saucer). The legacy `fillCavesNear` calls and T8's cave-gym mask keep
   using only these two. **The Fossil cave spot must not change** (fixture test, §5).
2. Every new spot depends only on the terrain and on the spots of gyms before it in `GYMS` (plus the two legacy
   extras and the tower). Nothing T10 adds is ever fed back into gyms 1–18 or the tower.
3. Outside the **new gym boxes** (`world.newGymBoxes`, §2.6) every block is identical to a T8 world.

### 2.2 `newGymSpots()` (cached; terrain only, identical with or without caves)
Loop `GYMS.slice(EXTRA_GYMS_END)` in order, keeping a `taken` list (start: gyms 1–16, Saucer, reserved bone
spot as points; tower as Chebyshev; Fossil cave spot and its stair columns as "cave" items). Use a cached
`Int8Array` of `min(terrainHeight, WORLD_H - 10)` (build once, or reuse `this.ground` if filled).
- **surface** (`pickSurfaceSpot(def, taken)`): candidates `x, z` from 20 to `W-20` step 2, `hypot` to the middle
  ≥ 40, `dry(x, z)` (same 5-point test as `extraGymSpots`), centre biome in `def.spot.biomes` and ground ≥
  `def.spot.minGround` (if given). Hard limits: Chebyshev ≥ 32 from every cave gym centre and ≥ 18 from every
  cave stair column (the slope reshaping reaches 15). Score = min(Chebyshev to the tower, Euclid to every taken
  point); take the highest score, ties by x then z. No candidate → retry without the biome/minGround filter,
  then without `dry`. Result `{ def, place: 'surface', x, z }`.
- **cave**: T8's cave-spot search for this one def (refactor T8's loop into `caveGymSpot(def, extraMask)`) with
  the **same mask and order as T8**, plus `extraMask` = every earlier T10 surface spot ±18 and every earlier cave
  spot ±26 (T8's rule). Calling it for Fossil with an empty `extraMask` must give T8's spot. `null` → surface
  fallback with `def.spot`. Result `{ def, place: 'cave', ...T8 spot }`.
- **sky** (`pickSkySpot`): as surface (no biome filter) plus `maxG(x, z) ≤ 22` over ±12 (measured: always
  found); hard limit Chebyshev ≥ 24 from cave gym centres (just for clarity). `fy = min(36, maxG + 12)`.
  Result `{ def, place: 'sky', x, z, fy }`.
Front for all: towards the middle (same rule as `placeGym`); for cave gyms T8's stair direction.

### 2.3 `generate()` order (T8's order + 3 T10 steps, marked ★)
terrain → `solid` → `carveCaves` → legacy fills → `carveBigCaves` → ★ `fillCavesNear(solid, x, z, 18)` for
each T10 **surface** spot (no caves under or opening into them; before the cave rooms so it never touches
them) → `carveGymRooms()` (Fossil **and Goo**) → `carved` → plants → `placeGyms` → `placeTower` →
`placeExtraGyms` (Saucer only) → `placeCaveGyms()` (Fossil and Goo) → ★ `placeNewGyms()` (surface + sky;
`markLit` each) → `fillCavesNearStart` → sort `this.gyms` into `GYMS` order. With `caves: false` the fills are
skipped like today. ★ `newGymBoxes` is filled while placing.
