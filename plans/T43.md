# T43 · 🎡 Funfair type and the Funfair in Playland

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `plans/wave15.md` (the shared rules: type id `funfair`, wool **224**, block ids **224-234**, tile
prefix `FF_`, write only inside `ZONES.fair.box`), `ROADMAP.md` "Wave 15", `STORY.md` "The Picture Worlds" (Playland:
"beat the racer, the football captain and the fair leader, and Playland starts up again"), `plans/T40.md` (B.7-B.11:
the kit, Bosses, live parts, `Mover`, Playland), `plans/T40c.md` (a game in Playland, a vehicle mesh, `forceSprint`),
`plans/T40d.md` (a pure physics module with fixed sub-steps, `beaten` keys and the Boss `gate`) and `plans/T41.md`
(the house style for a wave 15 plan) first.

**Owner context (2026-10-27):** the son loved the **skill games** (football, basketball, the race, the rowboats); the
puzzles began to feel the same. So the way to the Boss is a **set of three fair games**, each a skill game, and none of
them is "hold and let go at the right moment" (Kick!, Shoot! and Swing! / Fire! already use that). The roller coaster
is the **reward ride** to the Boss (a `Mover`), not a game. Nothing is ever a fail. Everything works on an iPad with the
joystick, Jump and the one action button.

**What the child does:** steps out of the Funfair frame's way-back door in Playland's hub (or walks along the
promenade from the Race Track and the Stadium) and through the striped arch into a fair that stands still, waiting for
the festival. A board by the arch shows three empty ticket slots. The child **drives a bumper car** and bumps the
balloons off the back of six bumper cars, **bounces on the big trampoline** higher and higher to pop the balloons that
float above it, and **rides the carousel**, hopping from horse to horse to grab the gold rings. Each game gives a
**golden ticket**. With three tickets the **roller coaster** opens: a long, swooping ride that ends on the **Star Stage**
high above the fair, where **Merry Mae, the Fairkeeper**, battles for the star. A slide takes you back down.

## 0. What makes it different from the other skill games

| Game | What your body does | What you judge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Football / basketball | run, kick, jump and throw | power (the ring), the top of the jump |
| Race | run a track against a rival | speed and the racing line |
| Rowboats / cannons / ropes | stand (or hang) still, hold and let go | where a moving boat will be, a swing's phase |
| **Bumper Cars** | **drive a car that slides and bounces** (momentum) | **how to get behind a moving car** and hit its back bumper |
| **Balloon Bounce** | **bounce higher and higher** and **steer in the air** | how high to go (hold or let go of Jump) and where to land |
| **Gold Ring Ride** | **ride a turning carousel, hop between horses** (lanes) and up | which horse to be on when a ring comes round (reaction, like a runner game) |

- None of the three uses a hold-and-let-go button. Bumper Cars is the joystick only (a new way to move: a vehicle
  with momentum); Balloon Bounce is the joystick in the air plus holding Jump to pump; Gold Ring Ride is a quick stick
  push in or out plus a tap of Jump. The action button only gets you in and out (Drive! / Get out, Ride! / Get off).
- **Never a fail:** no timers, no lives. Bumper cars only bounce, a missed balloon stays, a missed ring comes round
  again on the next lap. Points stay for the whole trip to Playland.
- Each game is short (about 1-2 minutes for a 7-year-old) and gives a ticket at the end, so progress is big and visible
  (the ticket board by the arch lights a slot, the coaster's lights come on one third at a time).

## 3. The three fair games (`src/fairgames.js`: pure, no imports, Node-runnable)

All clocks are the part's own summed frame dt (frozen while `!game.playing()`, in a battle, an ask or a panel), every
moving thing is a closed form of that clock or a fixed 1/120 s sub-step simulation (as `ballphysics.js`), so 60 fps
and 1-2 fps software rendering give the same results. No `Math.random` (hashes of indices only).
`hard` = the player already holds the `fair` star (a rematch): a little faster, a few more targets.

### 3.1 Bumper Cars (ticket 1)

- **Getting in:** six free cars are parked along the arena's east rail. Within 2 blocks of one: the action button says
  **Drive!** (`near` / `use`). `game.riding.stop('quiet')`, then the car becomes the player's `carry` (a duck-typed
  rider object: `where`, `wantsLeave` (always false: Jump is the horn), `leave`, `steer`). **Get out** (the action
  button again, or walking the car out through the gate gap) parks it where it is and puts you beside it.
- **Driving (`BumperCar`, 2D disc physics):** radius 0.85; the stick (or WASD, from where you look, like walking) is a
  wish; the car accelerates 9 b/s² towards it, top speed 6.5 (hard 7), rolling drag 1.5 b/s², the car's nose turns
  towards its velocity (drawn only). Off the stick it glides to a stop in about 4 s: **momentum** is the game. Rails
  bounce it back (`e` 0.65, the snap like the ball's `moveAxis`, never inside a wall). Jump = **Honk!** (sound, the
  car hops a tiny bit: pure fun). Mouse look stays free while driving.
- **The balloon cars:** six cars driven by Bumper Bots (round, smiling robot heads, decor) on **two closed-form loops**
  that never meet: three on an inner ellipse (12 x 6, clockwise, 3.4 b/s), three on an outer ellipse (22 x 15,
  anti-clockwise, 4.0 b/s; hard + 0.8), evenly spaced. Each car has a **balloon on a stick at its back**. Their path is
  never changed by a bump (closed form); a bump only gives them a short wobble (drawn).
- **The rule:** contact (centre distance < 1.7) **from behind**: the angle between the target's backward direction and
  the direction target -> you is below 55°, and your speed towards it is at least 2. **Pop!** (sound `balloonpop`,
  confetti puff, the car drives on without a balloon). Any other contact is a bounce: you bounce off it like off a
  moving wall (relative velocity reflected with `e` 0.8, plus its own velocity). So you must **drive round behind a car
  that is going away from you**: slower ones on the inner loop are easier, faster ones outside are harder.
- **Never stuck:** after every sub-step the car is pushed out of rails and bot cars; NaN or outside the arena -> back to
  its parking spot.
- 6 pops -> **golden ticket 1** (`pw:fair:ticket1`, saved at once; toast + the board lights). Pops before that are kept
  for the trip (`live.pops`), not saved. With the ticket the bots keep driving (play on for fun; pops then give no more).

### 3.2 Balloon Bounce (ticket 2)

- **The block flag `trampoline` (player.js, generic):** landing on a block with `trampoline: { min, max, step, drop }`
  (falling, `vel.y < -1`) launches you again at once (no Jump needed):
  `h = clamp(p.trampH + (Space held ? step : -drop), min, max)`, `vel.y = sqrt(2 * 28 * g * h)`, `p.trampH = h`.
  `TRAMPOLINE` = `{ min: 2, max: 13, step: 2, drop: 1.5 }`. Standing on anything else sets `trampH = 0`. So **hold Jump
  to go higher** (2, 4, 6 ... 13 blocks: about 7 bounces), **let go to come down** step by step; landing off the
  trampoline stops the bouncing. Air steering is the normal walk in the air (4.3 / 6.2 b/s), so the skill is
  **where you land and when you go high**. Max apex 13 above y 11 = y 24, under `WORLD_H` 48.
  (Jump rules are a block flag, not the live part, so they run inside `Player.update` at the same step as gravity; the
  spec compares 60 and 20 steps a second.)
- **The balloons:** `BALLOONS` = 6 (hard 8) at heights above the mat 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 (the top two gold; hard adds 9
  and 12). Each drifts on its own small closed-form circle (radius 1.2-2.5, period 7-11 s, hard x 0.7) and bobs 0.3.
  All circles lie inside the trampoline's air column shrunk by 2, so you can always land back on the mat.
- **Pop** = the player's box (0.6 x 1.8) touches the balloon's sphere (r 0.7). Sound `balloonpop`, confetti.
- 6 pops (hard 8) -> **golden ticket 2** (`pw:fair:ticket2`). The popped ones float back after the ticket (for fun).

### 3.3 Gold Ring Ride (ticket 3)

- **The carousel** turns all the time (ω 0.45 rad/s anti-clockwise from above: one lap in 14 s; hard 0.52). Its
  canopy, pole and 24 horses (8 per lane) are meshes turning together; the horses bob up and down (closed form,
  ±0.35, each a different phase).
- **Getting on:** within 2 blocks of the deck's edge: **Ride!**. You are put on the nearest outer-lane horse
  (`CarouselSeat`, a `Mover` on `PATHS.carousel({ c, y, omega, radius: lane => ... })` whose `seat` is the lane; run
  `'always'`, so everybody's carousel is at the same angle for a given clock, but each player has their own).
  **Get off** (action button) puts you on the deck beside your horse.
- **Lanes:** three (radius 4.5, 6.5, 8.5). `steer`: a stick push **outwards** or **inwards** (the stick's direction
  projected on the radius, beyond 0.5; A / D on a keyboard when looking along the ride) moves you one lane in 0.25 s
  (one move per push: it waits for the stick to come back). **Jump** = a hop (0.6 s, 1.3 up, closed form), it does not
  throw you off (the seat's `wantsLeave` is always false).
- **The rings:** `RINGS` = 10 gold rings (hard 12) hanging from the canopy's ring rail at fixed angles (they do not turn;
  you turn past them), each with a lane and a height: `low` (caught just by being in that lane when you pass under it)
  or `high` (only during a hop). Window: your angle within ±0.09 rad of the ring's. A caught ring vanishes with a
  `ringding` and a sparkle; missed rings come round again next lap. The ring table is made so two rings in a row are
  at least 1.8 s apart and need a lane change or a hop (the spec checks a perfect schedule catches all 10 in 3 laps and
  that staying in one lane catches at most 4).
- Silver rings between the gold ones are just for fun (a sparkle and a note, no count).
- All gold rings caught (the trip keeps the caught ones) -> **golden ticket 3** (`pw:fair:ticket3`).

## 4. The roller coaster and the Star Stage

- **The station:** the coaster car (a mesh: two seats, a lap bar, a light string) waits at `(166, 11, 173)`. Before three
  tickets its lights are dim and the action button near it says **Ride!** greyed with the toast "Win 3 golden tickets
  first! 🎟️ 1 / 3". The coaster track's light string shows the tickets: one third lit per ticket.
- **With three tickets: Ride!** -> the car is a `Mover` with `canLeave: false` on `PATHS.coaster` (`run: 'ride'`).
  Path: Catmull-Rom through `COASTER.points`, sampled every 0.25 blocks; the speed comes from height
  (`v = max(vMin 4, sqrt(v0² + 2 * 12 * (yTop - y)))`, a chain lift at 3 b/s on the lift hill), precomputed into a
  table so `at(t)` is a closed form. Route: west from the station, the **lift hill** up to y 34 at the west end (click
  click click: `coasterclack`), a **big drop** south-east to y 13, a long curve along z 208, a **camel hump** (a little
  floaty moment), a banked turn north at x 210, and a gentle climb that stops at the east edge of the Star Stage
  (y 24). About 35 s. The camera keeps the player's look, but the yaw is eased towards the way the car goes (so the
  ride looks right without taking the mouse away; the touch drag-look still works).
- **The Star Stage** (y 23, 11 x 11) high above the fair, bunting, the `BOSS_STAND` in the middle with Merry Mae. The way
  down is the **slide**: **Slide!** at the north-west corner, a `Mover` on `PATHS.helix` (2.5 turns, radius 3.5, 6 s) to
  `(193.5, 11, 192.5)`. There are no stairs, so the only way up is the coaster (the Boss `gate` still checks the tickets,
  for a reload on the stage or a lost battle).
- Never a fail: the coaster cannot be left, the stage has a rail, a fall from the stage (only possible at the slide gap)
  lands on the floor without harm (Playland has no fall damage).

## Harder games (owner 2026-10-27)

> "The challenges to reach the boss should always be fun and interesting and unique. We really knocked it out of the park with the
> rowboat sinking ... the kind of challenge that seems more fun are those that need aim / dexterity / timing / directional tactics more
> than the puzzle kind. That doesn't mean repeat the SAME kind of challenge." And: the race "ended up still too simple / easy".

The first Funfair games were good ideas but a careful child could not lose them: the bots never reacted, the balloons waited, and every
missed ring came round again with no limit. So each game keeps its own character (a car with momentum, bouncing and steering in the air,
a turning ride with lanes and hops) and gets **three rounds that grow from "learn it" to "needs real skill"**, a small clock or lap per
round and a score on the chip. **Never a fail:** a missed round simply starts again (the bots and balloons keep moving, nothing is lost but
that round); after **two missed tries of the same round** the game turns a little slower (`fairHelp(misses)`: x0.9, x0.8, at most x0.7, and a
timed round gets 10 s more), so a child who keeps trying always gets there. A rematch (you hold the star) is quicker and needs a bit more
(`HARD`). Everything is pure and closed-form or fixed-step in `fairgames.js` (`BumperGame`, `BounceGame`, `Seat`), so 60 and 20 frames a
second give the same game.

What makes them different from each other and from the other worlds: Bumper Cars is **chasing with momentum and reading a rival's
reaction** (wait for the tired puff, line up and commit to a fast run); Balloon Bounce is **height control plus air steering against
moving targets** (and a rhythm: one pop on every bounce for the combo); the Gold Ring Ride is **reaction and rhythm on a fixed beat**
(swap and hop on time, the beat gets faster). None is hold-and-let-go (the stadium, the rowboats), none is a race against a rival on a
track (the race).

### Bumper Cars: three rounds against bots that notice you

| Round | Balloons on | Bot speeds (b/s) | Tricks | Need | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Warm-up | the 3 inner bots | inner 3.0 (outer 3.4, no balloon) | none | 3 | 45 s |
| 2 Zoomers | the 3 outer bots | outer 4.2 (inner 3.0, no balloon) | **zoom** | 3 | 50 s |
| 3 Spinners | all 6 | inner zoomers 3.4, outer spinners 3.6 | **zoom** + **spin** | 6 | 75 s |

- **Zoom** (`BOT_TRICKS`): a zoomer notices a car within 4.5 blocks that is behind it (within about 78 degrees of its back) and closing at
  1+ b/s, and zooms off at +3.4 b/s (faster than your 6.5) for 1.1 s, then **puffs along tired** at 40 % for 1.8 s (it nods: that is the
  moment), then rests 0.8 s before it can notice again. Chasing straight works only if you keep after it through the zoom. On the small
  inner loop you can also cut across and meet it.
- **Spin**: a spinner turns its nose (and its balloon away) towards you when you are within 3 blocks, at 1.0 rad/s, and back to the road
  further away. A slow sneak never pops it: line up behind it and come in fast (the last 1.3 blocks at full speed take 0.4 s: a turn of
  23 degrees, inside the 55-degree "from behind" cone). Once it has turned to face you, ramming it only bounces: back off and come again.
- Bots without a balloon this round still drive (moving obstacles). The round clock runs only while you drive; getting out pauses it.
- A rematch: bots +0.4 b/s, every round 5 s shorter.
- The words: "Round 2: Zoomers! They zoom off when they see you. Bump them when they get tired!", "Round 3: Spinners turn to look at
  you! Line up behind and come in fast!", "Time is up! Round 3 again. You can do it! (A bit slower now.)"; the chip
  "🚗 Round 2/3 · 🎈 1 / 3 · ⏱ 32"; sounds `botzoom`, `fairround`, `fairagain`.

### Balloon Bounce: patterns, star balloons and combos

| Round | Balloons | Rule | Need | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Warm-up | 5 slow circles, 5-9 high (a lazy 2-block bounce never reaches them) | touch | all 5 | 60 s |
| 2 Wobblers | 2 figures of eight (6, 9 high), 2 zigzags (7, 10), 2 **star balloons** (6, 8) | stars pop only from above | all 6 | 70 s |
| 3 Combo | 7 that come back 2.5 s after a pop (4.5-11.5 high: a circle, a zigzag, a figure of eight, a riser, 2 stars, 1 gold) | **combo** | 30 points | 60 s |

- Patterns are closed forms of the game clock (`balloonPath`: `circle`, `eight`, `zig`, `rise`), all inside the mat's air column shrunk by 2.
- A **star balloon** (pearly white with a gold star on top) pops only when you come DOWN onto it (falling, your feet at its middle or
  higher); touched from below or the side it just boops (`fairboop`, a hint once). So you must bounce higher than it and steer onto it.
- **Points and combo** (round 3): a plain balloon 1, a star 2, a gold one 3, times the combo: every pop in a row adds one (x1, x2, x3 at
  most); a bounce without a pop is allowed, **two bounces in a row without a pop end the combo**. The chip: "🎈 Round 3/3 · ⭐ 14 / 30 · x3 · ⏱ 41".
- The clock runs while you are on or above the mat (`player.trampN` counts the launches, a small T43 line in player.js).
- A rematch: patterns x1.33 quicker, 5 s shorter, round 3 needs 34.

### Gold Ring Ride: one lap per round, faster each round, gold rings

| Round | Carousel | Rings | Points (need / all) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Warm-up | 0.45 rad/s (a lap in 14 s) | 8 low rings 1.75 s apart, the 2 gold ones on the inner lane | 9 / 12 |
| 2 Hop and swap | 0.55 (11.4 s) | 10 rings 1.1 s apart, 4 high gold ones, two-lane swaps | 13 / 18 |
| 3 Gold rush | 0.65 (9.7 s) | 12 rings in quick pairs (0.6-0.9 s), 5 high gold + 1 gold right after a two-lane swap | 19 / 24 |

- A round is **one lap** from the moment you get on (or the last round ended): enough points before the lap is over and the next round
  starts at once (the carousel speeds up); not enough and the lap starts again ("You got 9 of 13 points! Round 2 again."). Every ring can be
  caught once a lap; a ring you pass in the wrong lane (or under a high one without a hop) is gone for that lap.
- Gold = 3 points (all high ones and the hard-to-reach low ones), silver = 1. No lane alone and no play without hops can reach a round's
  need (lane 0 / 1 / 2 give 6 / 4 / 2 of round 1's 9; without hops round 2 gives 6 of 13).
- A rematch: +0.07 rad/s each round and one point more. The help: the carousel x0.9 ... x0.7 after two missed laps.
