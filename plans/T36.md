## A4. The run-away walk ("gait"), `creatures.js`

**Generic, not a type check:** a species field `gait: 'hopaway'` and a table `GAITS` in `creatures.js` (exported). A
gait owns the creature's movement instead of the wander walk. Racer can later add `run: { ... }` (straight dashes)
to the same table.

```js
export const GAITS = {
  // Parkour: sits still until you come near, then hops a few blocks away from you; tired after 3 hops.
  hopaway: { notice: 6, noticeUp: 8, hops: [3, 2], time: 0.45, arc: 0.9, pause: 0.35, tiredAfter: 3, tired: 8,
    up: 1, down: 2, leap: 8, leash: 14, angles: [0, 35, -35, 70, -70, 110, -110], bump: true },
};
```

**Behaviour (kid-friendly defaults from the roadmap):**

- **Idle:** never wanders (so a creature on a treetop stays there). Idle animation, slowly turns to look at the player.
- **Notice:** player within `notice` 6 blocks horizontally and `noticeUp` 8 vertically, not tired, not frozen ->
  hop. Between hops a `pause` of 0.35 s, facing the player (it looks back at you, then hops again).
- **Choosing a hop:** away = the direction from the player to the creature. For each angle in `angles` (degrees off
  away) and each distance in `hops` (3, then 2): the cell `floor(pos + dir * dist)`; take the first that:
  - has a landing height: scan from `y0 + up` down to `y0 - down` (y0 = the creature's feet): the first `y` with air
    at `y` and `y + 1` and a solid block at `y - 1`; **if a WATER (or other liquid) cell is met first, the column is
    refused** ("never into water");
  - **"never off a cliff":** at most 1 up and 2 down; one exception: a creature that stands on a perch (A5 `isPerch` of its own
    column) may leap down at most `leap` 8 blocks **to the natural ground** (the landing block `y - 1 <= world.ground[col]`),
    so approaching a tree or a roof makes it jump down and run (trees are 5-7 high), but never off a tall tower;
  - not `world.fenced(x, y, z)` (never into a gym or the tower), inside the map (1..W-2, 1..D-2), within `leash` 14 of
    where it spawned (`w.home`), not closer to the player than before, and the arc is free: the 1-2 columns between start
    and landing have air at `max(y0, y) + 1` and `+ 2`.
  - **No hop possible (cornered) = tired at once** (it gives up; kid-friendly).
- **The hop is closed form (time-based):** `w.hop = { from, to, t }`; `k = min(1, t / time)`; position =
  `lerp(from, to, k)` with `y += 4 * h * k * (1 - k)`, `h = arc + max(0, to.y - from.y)`. Any `dt` (also 0.5 s) lands
  exactly on the target cell. Heading = towards the target. `animateModel(model, t, true)` while hopping.
- **Tired:** after `tiredAfter` 3 hops (or cornered) `w.tired = 8` seconds of game time: no hops, it pants
  (`w.model.scale` squashes `1 + 0.03 s, 1 - 0.06 s, 1 + 0.03 s`, `s = |sin(t * 10)|`; the scale is set back to 1 in
  every other state and in the frozen branch). When it runs out: `hops = 0`, it can run again. Hook
  `this.onTired?.(w)` once per tired phase when the player is within 12 blocks; `main.js` (one line):
  `game.creatures.onTired = w => { if (game.started && !game.inBattle) game.ui.toast(`${w.species.name} is out of breath! Walk into it to battle.`); };`
- **Touching it starts the battle (like Camo):** the bump check in `update()` becomes generic:
  `w.disguise || GAITS[w.species.gait]?.bump`; for a gait creature `half = 0.9`, `dy = player.y - w.pos.y` in
  `[-1, 1.5)`. **Edge-triggered** (`w.touching`): `onBump` fires only when the player starts touching it; otherwise a fled
  battle would restart at once because you are still standing in it. Clicking/tapping it battles as before.
- **Code shape:** in `updateOne`, after the `disguise` and `frozen || still` branches: `if (sp.gait) return
  this.updateGait(w, dt);` (uses `this.viewer` for the player). `updateGait` does not use `groundY`/`caveGroundY` (it
  stands on leaves, roofs, pillars); y is the landing cell. State on the Wild: `home`, `hop`, `hops`, `tired`, `pause`,
  `touching`. Nothing is saved or sent (wild creatures are local, as now).
- Removal stays as it is (`life`, distance, `condOk(w.when)`): see A5 for why a creature from `high` stays while you are
  above ground.

## A5. The "high places" spawn condition (`spawnrules.js` + `creatures.js`)

**Perch rule** (a method `isPerch(x, z)` on `CreatureManager`, also used by A4; returns `{ x, y, z }` = the air cell to
stand in, or null):

- `s = world.surface(x, z)`, `c = x + z * W`; **`s.y - world.ground[c] >= 3`** (`PERCH_UP`; `world.ground` = the natural
  top block): something stands on the land (treetops, tall cacti, roofs, pillars), natural hills never count;
- `s.id` is solid and not water; **if `s.id` is a ground block (the `GROUND` set: grass, dirt, sand, stone, snow, ice...)
  it must be a player edit** (`world.edits.has(world.index(x, s.y, z))`): a dirt pillar you built counts, but ground that
  a gym builder raised (`levelSite` slopes) and frozen lakes (ice over deep water) do not;
- `s.y + 2 < WORLD_H`, `!world.fenced(x, s.y + 1, z)` (no gym or tower roofs), not in a realm (`world.realm`).

**`perchNear(pos)`:** up to 16 random columns at 8-24 blocks from the player (close enough to see, not in your face);
the first perch, or null.

**Context:** `context()` gets one field: `perch: under || !this.env || this.world?.realm ? null : () => this.perchNear(playerPos)`
(a function: the search only runs when the condition is actually rolled).

**Condition** (one `CONDITIONS` entry, data only):

```js
high: { ok: c => !!c.perch, share: 1 / 30, max: 2, outdoor: true,
  place: c => c.perch(),
  text: 'up high: on treetops, roofs and tall pillars',
  toast: 'Look up! A sporty creature is on a high spot nearby.' },
```

- `share` 1/30 of spawn attempts (one every 0.6 s, so one roll about every 18 s above ground), at most 2 out at once; the
  roll often finds no perch in open land (no spawn then), so near trees and houses one shows up within half a minute or
  so, on bare plains rarely until you build a pillar. `ok` needs `c.perch`, so hand-made contexts in older specs (no
  `perch`) never pick Parkour creatures, and underground (`under`) they leave like day/night ones.
- `spawnPlaced` passes `indoor: !condOf(when).outdoor` instead of `indoor: true` (home visitors keep `indoor`; a
  perched creature is not an indoor walker). One-word change in an existing line.
- **Spawn table** (`species.js`, after `SPAWN_HOME`, merged in the existing `for (const extra of [...])` line by adding
  `SPAWN_PARKOUR` at its end: a one-line edit):

```js
const PARKOUR = [['teetail', 1, 'high'], ['zippet', 1, 'high'], ['vaultling', 1, 'high'], ['zoomstick', 0.6, 'high'],
  ['tightropa', 0.12, 'high'], ['ferrocket', 0.12, 'high'], ['highvault', 0.1, 'high']];
const SPAWN_PARKOUR = Object.fromEntries(Object.keys(SPAWNS).filter(b => b !== 'cave').map(b => [b, PARKOUR]));
```

- **Book:** reads it from data: the condition has a `place`, so the Book shows one row "Up high: on treetops, roofs and
  tall pillars" (`spawnInfo`, no Book change). Check the page of one creature in the spec.
- **How a spec forces it:** build a pillar (or use the high `platform` helper: every cell of a y-42 platform is a
  perch, its natural ground is far below) and call `cm.spawnPlaced('teetail', 'high', cm.context(pos), g.spawnPoint)`,
  or call `pickEntry(biome, { perch: () => spot })` for the share.

## B3. Player physics: what a child can jump (block-exact)

From `player.js`: jump speed 8.6, gravity 28 (apex 1.32 blocks, 0.61 s in the air on flat ground), walk 4.3, sprint 6.2
(Shift, or the touch stick pushed all the way, which iPad kids do anyway), half-width 0.3 (you may stand 0.3 past an
edge, so a gap of n air columns needs n + 0.6 of flight). Capacity in air columns:

| Jump | Walking | Sprinting | **Course rule** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| flat | 2.0 (too tight) | 3.2 | gap 1 anywhere; **gap 2 only flat, max 3 times, landing >= 3 wide** |
| up 1 | 1.4 | 2.2 | **gap 1** (or adjacent) |
| down 1 | 2.5 | 3.8 | gap <= 1 |
| down 3 | about 3 | about 5 | gap 1 (only the exit) |
| up 2 | impossible | impossible | used to keep people out |

Beams are 1 wide (you are supported up to 0.3 past each side, so it feels 1.6 wide), straight, 4-8 long, and always
start right next to the platform you stand on (never a jump onto a beam's end). At 20 fps (`dt` 0.05, the clamp in
`main.js`) up 1 still works (apex about 1.1): the spec checks both 60 and 20 fps steps.

## B5. `CourseLive` (`def.live: 'course'`, in `obstaclecourse.js`; `LIVE.course` in `gymlive.js`)

Imports `LiveCells` from `livecells.js` if needed (never from `gymlive.js`: the import cycle). State per player, in
memory only (never saved or sent; a new world or reload starts fresh):

- `reached` (highest checkpoint stood on this session, starts 0) and `falls` (for tests).
- **Send-back target** = the furthest reached checkpoint, but **never past a landing whose trainer is not beaten yet**
  (`game.beaten.includes(trainerKey(def, i))`): you can't hop off the start and land beyond a trainer you skipped.
- `stand(x, y, z, id)` (called every frame on the ground by `main.js`, already wired):
  - `CHECKPOINT` / `CHECKPOINT_ON` at a checkpoint cell: if its index is new: `reached = k`, redraw flags `0..k` as
    `CHECKPOINT_ON` (`world.setLive`), sound `checkpoint`, toast `Checkpoint! If you fall, you come back here.` (+ ` Runner Remy wants
    to battle you!` when that landing's trainer is not beaten). Standing on CP0 when `reached > 0`: once per visit the
    toast `Your flag is waiting! Hop onto the soft mat to go back to it.` (the way back after a lost battle, which sends
    you to the start of the map).
  - `SOFT_MAT` inside `gym.course`: `player.pos` = the target's `stand`, `vel` 0, `yaw` = its yaw, pitch 0, sound
    `boing`, toast `Boing! Back to the start.` / `Boing! Back to your last flag.`; `falls++`.
- `update()`: on the first call draws CP0 lit. `prompt(block)`: on a checkpoint `'Checkpoint flag'`, else null. No `hit`.
  No other methods (the `gymLive` hook names).
- Moving platforms: **none** (moving collision is not cheap). See open question 3.
