# T8 · Bigger caves + a cave gym (plan)

Owner: "bones should be only found in caves and also their gym would also be in the cave." / "I couldn't
really find caves ... we need to make them more sizeable and explorable. Maybe even one more gym can be in a
cave." / "if you need to expand the map for these, so be it. let's not lose existing games though."

Result: the old caves stay exactly as they are and a **second, much bigger cave layer** is added on top of them
(big halls, wide tunnels, walk-in stairways with lit arches). The **Fossil Gym moves into its own lit
cavern** with a stairway down from the surface. **Bone creatures only live in caves.** The map stays 224×224.
A second cave gym is **not** built here: T10 gets a ready API (`place: 'cave'`) for its Slime gym (section 4).

Read first: `CLAUDE.md` (caves, lighting, old saves), `plans/T3.md` §1 (cave rules), `plans/T4a.md` §3 (extra
gym spots). **T9 runs at the same time**: it adds `SPAWN_SKY/SLIME/LIGHT/PAPER` and edits the `rain/storm/
rainbow` lines of `BOOSTS`. T8 only edits `SPAWNS` itself, `SEVENTH_C`, and the `sandstorm/fog` lines.

## 0. Measured before planning (node on a copy of `src/`, seeds 1, 4242, 99, 123456, 777)

- **Today's caves are small and broken up**: 7.5k–13k cave air per world, split into **75–110 separate
  pockets**; the largest connected one is only 950–3,700 blocks. Tunnels are ~3 wide × 3–4 tall (worm radius
  1.5±0.6). 7–8 entrances per world, but each is a small worm hole (49–147 "opened" columns in total), with
  nothing marking it. That matches "I didn't think it was a cave".
- **Vertical room**: `WORLD_H` 48, `WATER_LEVEL` 12, natural ground p10 = 10–11, median 13–17, p90 19–21,
  max 28–29. Caves may only use `y = 3 .. ground-3` (y ≤ 2 carved cells turn to water; y = 0 is the bottom),
  so normal land has **8–12 layers** of rock to work with, hills up to ~20. There is no room for "several
  levels". `WORLD_H` doesn't help (nothing is gained above) and shifting the terrain up for a deeper layer would
  need every saved edit, player position and gym fixture moved by y; **rejected**. `index()` is
  `x + W*(z + D*y)` and `convertEdits` only cares about W and D, so nothing here touches the save format.
- **Growing the map is not needed** (and would move every gym: `gymSpots` scales with `WORLD_W`).
- **Prototype of the new layer** (8 halls rx/rz 10–15 under ground ≥ 17, hall tunnels radius 2.6, 40 wide
  steered tunnels radius 2.3, y ≤ 2 → water): cave air **22k–37k** (×2.9–4.2 of today), largest connected
  cave **8.9k–15.4k**, halls hold 1.4k–4.6k air each (a few small ones where the hill is low). Carving
  15–50 ms. Triangles vs `caves:false`: today +7–13 %, with the new layer **+11–18 %** (vs today +3–6 %);
  `buildAll` time unchanged within noise (400–700 ms in node).
- **Cave gym room** (prototype, 200 seeds): a spot whose 17×17 building box is dry (ground > WATER_LEVEL+2,
  dilated by 2), ≥ 16 (Chebyshev, box edge) from gyms 1–16, the tower and the Saucer gym, ≥ 12 from the start,
  ≥ 28 from the middle, plus a straight stairway (rise 1 per 2 forward) from the room floor (y = 2) up to the
  surface that never crosses a keep-out column: **found on 188/200 seeds; with a 1-per-1 stair fallback on
  198/200** (seeds 122 and 1253 have no spot: they use the surface fallback, section 3.6). Stairways are
  24–39 blocks long; the box's lowest ground is 15–23, so the building (roof at y = 9) always has ≥ 2 rows of
  rock above it under the 3-block roof rule. Spots for the test seeds: 1 → (62,154), 4242 → (130,30),
  99 → (160,20), 123456 → (48,62), 777 → (170,116), 7 → (58,168) (prototype; the builder re-measures).

## 1. Hard rules (old saves)

1. **The old caves are kept byte for byte.** `carveCaves` (v1) is not changed at all (same rand stream, same
   decoration, same chests), and the `fillCavesNear` at both legacy extra spots stays. The new layer is a
   separate function that runs **after** it with its own rand (`mulberry32(seed ^ 0xb16ca7e)`) and only adds
   air. Old saves' opened chests (`CHEST_OPEN` edits) therefore still sit on their chests.
2. Terrain heights (`ground`), gyms 1–16, the tower and the **Saucer gym never move**. `extraGymSpots()` keeps
   computing the **bone spot first, as a reserved spot** (nothing is built there any more), so the Saucer spot
   and every later spot stay the same.
3. The surface only changes at: new stairway mouths and their arches (±3), lamp posts beside old entrances,
   the cave gym's stairway mouth, and the legacy Fossil box (±18: the old building is gone, natural ground and
   plants come back). Everything else on the surface is identical.
4. New carving never breaks the surface except at its stairways (3-block roof rule, same `keep` mask as v1
   plus the two legacy extra spots ±18). Gyms added later are protected by `fillCavesNear` after carving,
   **never by changing the carve mask** (that would shift the rand stream and move every new cave).
5. Badges are gym ids, `beaten` keys are `bone:trainer` etc.: nothing changes; old bone badges stay.

## 2. The new cave layer (`src/caves.js`: `export function carveBigCaves(world)`)

Called from `generate()` right after the v1 legacy fills (section 3.1). Uses `keepOutMask(world)` (now
exported, unchanged) plus the legacy extra spots ±18. Same `carve()` rules as v1 (roof rule `y <= ground-3`,
never water/air, never keep columns, never `y < 3` except lake rooms) and the same flood rule (carved `y <= 2`
→ water). Keep a `carved` index list (no full-world scans).

1. **Halls** (up to 8): random columns with `ground >= 17`, not keep, ≥ 34 apart (4000 tries). Ellipse
   `rx, rz = 10 + rand*5`; per column at normalised distance `d²`: floor stays solid at y = 2, air from y = 3
   to `min(ground-3, 3 + floor(9*sqrt(1-d²)))`. Theme by index: `['crystal', 'lake', 'glow', 'fossil'][i % 4]`.
2. **Hall tunnels**: join halls in nearest-neighbour order with a steered tunnel (yaw turns 15 % towards the
   target + noise ±0.25, y random walk ±0.3 clamped to `[4, ground-5]`, ball radius `2.6 + 0.5 sin(s*0.2)`,
   stop within 2 of the target or after 400 steps).
3. **Wide tunnels** (40): start at a random non-keep column with `ground >= WATER_LEVEL+5`, y = 5, steer
   towards a point 60 blocks away in a random direction, radius `2.3 + 0.5 sin(s*0.2)`.
4. **Themes** (after flooding): `lake`: inside 0.6 of the ellipse carve y = 1..2 and fill with water (edges
   stay dry, 2 deep, swimmable); `crystal`: ceiling crystals at 6 % and 3–5 crystal clusters 2–3 tall;
   `glow`: glowcaps on 12 % of the floor; `fossil`: a ribcage of 5 arches of bone wool (`WOOL.bone`) across the
   hall plus a 3×3×3 bone-wool skull with two dark (`COAL_ORE`) eye holes. Kid-friendly, no gore.
5. **Decoration** of the new cells: same rules as v1 (ore on walls, crystals, glowcaps) but with hash seeds
   `seed + 605..608` so nothing in v1 is re-decided. Copy the loop into a helper; **don't refactor v1**.
6. **Stairways** (`stairsToSurface(world, x, z, fy, keep)`, also used by the gym, section 3.3): try the 4
   directions out from the hall centre, first with rise 1 per 2 forward, then 1 per 1; walk `d` outwards
   from the hall edge, tread `y = fy + floor((d - d0) / rise)`; fail if any of the 5 columns across
   (`s = -2..2`) is keep or off the map; succeed at the first `d` where `tread >= ground - 1`. Pick the
   shortest. Carving (ignores the roof rule, only along the path): 3 wide, tread set to `COBBLE`, air from
   tread+1 to tread+4 (room to jump), a `LAMP` in the side wall (s = ±2, tread+2) every 6 blocks where the
   wall is solid. **Mouth** (the first `d` where tread+4 ≥ ground): two `STONE_BRICKS` pillars (s = ±2,
   tread+1..tread+5), a lintel at tread+5 (s = -2..2), a `LAMP` on top of each pillar; record the mouth in
   `entrances`, its columns in `opened` and the arch columns in a new `marks` set. Up to 6 halls get one.
7. **Old entrances get a marker**: for each v1 entrance, a lamp post (2 `COBBLE` + `LAMP`) on the highest dry
   non-hole column within 3 blocks (add to `marks`). It makes the owner's "small hole" visible at night.
8. **Chests**: up to 6 more, in new floors, ≥ 24 from each other and from v1 chests.
9. Merge into `world.caves`: `air += `, `entrances.push(...)`, `floors` (cap 300 total, new ones first so tests
   find big caves), `crystals`, `chests`, `glow`, plus new `halls: [{ x, z, rx, rz, theme, mouth }]`, `marks`.

Budget (builder reports numbers in the PR): v1 + new carving ≤ 60 ms per world in node; triangles ≤ +22 % over
`caves:false` and ≤ +10 % over today; `buildAll` ≤ 1.3× `caves:false`; a cave chunk rebuild ≤ 8 ms desktop.
Tune hall count/radii down if over budget (halls first, they are the cheapest per block).

## 3. The Fossil Gym in a cavern (`world.js`, `gyms.js`, `main.js`)

### 3.1 `generate()` order
terrain → `solid` copy → `carveCaves` (v1) → `fillCavesNear` at both legacy extra spots (as now) →
**`carveBigCaves`** → **`carveGymRooms()`** (rooms + stairways, also when `caves:false`) → `carved` snapshot →
plants → `placeGyms` → `placeTower` → `placeExtraGyms` (skips `place: 'cave'`) → **`placeCaveGyms()`** →
`fillCavesNearStart`. Afterwards `this.gyms` is **sorted into `GYMS` order** (`gyms[i].def === GYMS[i]`,
the guide arrow relies on it). Options for tests: `generate({ caves, bigCaves = caves, caveGyms = true })`
(`caveGyms: false` = no cave gym at all, only for the checksum test). With `caves: false` there is no
`this.caves`: the gym room code keeps its own data (`gym.cave`, `litRooms`) and never touches `this.caves`.
Lamp posts and arches belong to `carveBigCaves`, so `bigCaves: false` has none of them.

### 3.2 `gyms.js`
Fossil def gets `place: 'cave'` (default/absent = `'surface'`; T10 may add more values). New intro first
line: `'Welcome to the Fossil Gym, deep under the hill! Mind the bones, they are very old.'`. Teams unchanged.

### 3.3 `world.caveGymSpots()` (cached, terrain only, the same with or without caves)
For every def with `place === 'cave'`, in `GYMS` order: candidates `x, z` from 20 to `W-20` step 2,
`hypot` to the middle ≥ 28, the 17×17 box (R = 8) clear of the **cave-gym mask** (edges 6; columns with
`ground <= WATER_LEVEL+2` dilated by 2; gyms 1–16, tower and every `extraGymSpots()` spot except the reserved
bone one ±16; start column ±12; middle ±14; earlier cave gym spots ±26). `m` = lowest ground in the box.
Order: `min(m, 18)` desc, then stair length asc, then x, then z. Take the first candidate with a rise-2
stairway (section 2.6, against the mask, `fy = 2`, `d0 = 9`); if none, the first with rise 1; if none, return
`null` for that gym (surface fallback, 3.6). Result `{ def, x, z, fy: 2, front: [fx, fz], rise, len }`.
The door faces the stairway (`front` = its direction). ≤ 40 ms per world (measured 1–33 ms).

### 3.4 `carveGymRoom(spot)`
- Cavern: ellipse half-size 13 around (x, z); per column air from y = 3 to
  `min(ground-3, 2 + 4 + floor(8*sqrt(1-d²)))`, but at least y = 10 inside the building box (always fits,
  0 §); floor y = 2 forced solid `STONE` (dries any worm water), y = 1 stays as is.
- Stairway with `stairsToSurface` along `front` (rise from the spot), arch as 2.6 but the lintel's middle
  block is `WOOL[def.type]` and two extra bone-wool blocks on the pillars, so kids can tell it's the gym.
- Lamp ring: 8 `STONE_BRICKS` posts 2 tall with a `LAMP` on top at radius 10 (skip the stair side), and
  glowcaps on 5 % of the cavern floor. Four bone-wool pillars from floor to ceiling at (±10, ±10).
- `this.litRooms.push({ x0: x-8, x1: x+8, z0: z-8, z1: z+8, y0: 2, y1: 10 })`. `skyAt()` returns 1 inside a
  lit room (check after `litCol`; ≤ 3 rooms, a tiny loop). Like surface gyms: bright by day, dim at night;
  the cavern around it is dark and lit by the lamps.

### 3.5 `placeCaveGyms()` and `buildGym(..., { cave: true })`
`placeGym(def, x, z, { cave: spot })` → floor `fy = 2`, `buildGym(x, 2, z, front, type, { cave: true })`.
Cave mode: **no** plant clearing, **no** slope reshaping, clear air only `fy+1 .. fy+5` inside the walls (not
up to `WORLD_H`), roof and walls as usual, solid foundation `y = 1 .. fy-1` under `R+2`, door/path/pillars as
usual. `decorateFossilGym` in cave mode: the 4 roof bone pillars grow from fy+8 **up to the rock** (they hold
the cave up). Return the usual gym object plus `cave: { mouth: [mx, my, mz], spot }`. No `markLit` (the lit
room does it). `gymAt(x, z, y)`: cave gyms only match when `y` is undefined or `g.y - 1 <= y <= g.y + 6`;
surface gyms ignore `y`. `main.js` passes `p.y`.

### 3.6 Surface fallback
If `caveGymSpots()` gives `null` (≈ 1 % of seeds), the gym is built exactly as today on its reserved
extra spot (`placeGym` surface). Test with seed 122 (builder confirms it still has no spot).

### 3.7 `main.js`
- **Guide arrow**: for `next.cave`, aim at the mouth unless the player is "on the way in": within 3 blocks
  (x/z) of the segment mouth→door and below `ground + 1`, or inside the cavern (Chebyshev ≤ 14, `y < g.y + 9`);
  then aim at the gym. Texts: `Next: Fossil Gym (in a cave) ↗ 40m`, near the mouth (< 6)
  `The Fossil Gym is down these stairs!`, on the way in `Next: Fossil Gym ↓ 20m`, then the usual
  `Fossil Gym is here!`. Expose `game.guideTarget = [x, z]` for tests.
- `updateGuide`: `gymAt(p.x, p.z, p.y)` (no "Welcome to the Fossil Gym!" while walking on the hill above).
- `continueGame`: once, if the save has no `bone` badge and no `toldCaveGym`: toast
  `The Fossil Gym moved into a big cave! Follow the arrow to the stairs.`; new optional save boolean
  `toldCaveGym` (true for new games). The existing "New gyms" toast stays.
- `creatures.js` `update`: `under = world.underground(pos) || world.inLitRoom(pos)` so standing in the gym
  never spawns surface creatures above you (`trySpawnCave` already skips lit cells, so they come from the dark
  cavern and tunnels). `world.inLitRoom(pos)` is the same box test as `skyAt`.

### 3.8 Multiplayer
Host and visitor build the world from the seed, so both need the new code. Add `wv: 2` (world version) to the
`world` message; a visitor whose own `WORLD_VERSION` differs shows `Your friend has a newer game. Please
reload the page!` and leaves. (Small; skip if the orchestrator prefers.)

## 4. API for T10 (a second cave gym, e.g. Slime)
- Add a def to `GYMS` (appended) with `place: 'cave'`. That's all: `caveGymSpots()` gives it a spot ≥ 26 from
  earlier cave gyms, `carveGymRoom` + `placeCaveGyms` build it, the arrow/stairs/lit room work, Fossil stays put
  (spots are chosen in `GYMS` order).
- Optional `def.cave = { theme: 'slime' }`: `carveGymRoom` calls `decorateCaveRoom[theme]` (T8 ships `bone`:
  pillars + ribs; T10 adds e.g. slime puddles) and `decorate<Type>Gym` for the building.
- T10 **surface** extras: `extraGymSpots()` for defs after index 17 must add the cave gym spots to `taken`
  (T8 adds this line already, it is a no-op today). Sky/night gyms are T10's own `place` values.
- Never change the cave-gym mask, the candidate order or `fy` afterwards: it would move the Fossil Gym in
  existing worlds (harmless for badges, but players' edits around it would no longer line up).

## 8. Decisions for the owner (defaults in bold)
1. **Map stays 224×224**: the measured room is enough and growing it would move every gym.
2. **Old caves kept, a second bigger layer added** (old chests and digs line up). Alternative: replace the old
   caves (simpler code, but old opened chests and underground builds would no longer match).
3. Entrances: **stone-brick arch with two lamps and walk-down stairs (1 up per 2)**, plus a lamp post at each
   old hole. Alternative: plain holes in hillsides (today's style), just more of them.
4. Old surface Fossil Gym: **removed** (natural ground returns). Alternative: leave a small bone arch with a
   lamp there as a "the gym moved underground" sign.
5. Second cave gym: **not now; T10's Slime gym can use `place: 'cave'`** (no extra work for T10).
6. Bone share in caves **about a quarter**; bone creatures **nowhere else** (desert, beach, swamp lose them).
7. Cave gym light: **bright inside the building like other gyms, the cavern lit by lamps**.
8. Players who haven't beaten the Fossil Gym get **one toast** about the move; those with the badge see
   nothing new.
