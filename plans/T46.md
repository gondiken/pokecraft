# T46 · Owner's improvement list after wave 14 (2026-10-27)

What the owner asked for, and what was done.

1. **Picture World creatures were too easy to collect.** Every Picture World (and the Portal House in the Swirl) now has
   at most 5 wild creatures out at once (4 in the Swirl), with a spawn try every 2 s (the main world: 16, 0.6 s): realm
   def `wild: { max, every }`. And, like Pipsqueak and the 12 crumbs, the big evolved / rare ones of each world only come
   out once you got a bit further there: a spawn entry's 4th element names the world, `wildafter.js` checks it
   (house 6 crumbs, race a won race, stadium 2 goals, pirate 6 rowboats, atlantis 6 lights, fair a golden ticket,
   doors 2 bows, doodle 4 lines, portal 4 swirl rooms; or the world's star). The Book says when.
2. **The Race Track (v2).** "The road is too short ... think Hot Wheels ... no real car mechanics":
   - you drive your own kart (`kart.js`): it speeds up (no instant top speed), brakes, turns only so fast at speed
     (a corner of radius r at up to sqrt(12 r) blocks a second: the dip's hairpin needs slowing to 9.8), kerbs and
     grass slow it to 4.5, walls and rails bounce it, boost pads kick it to 17, a jump ramp launches it, a drop
     makes it faster; the camera follows it (stick forward = go, left / right = steer);
   - the lap is 2.5 times as long (about 470 blocks over the whole race zone): main straight, a snake of S-bends,
     a jump over the pond (too slow: splash, back before the ramp), the orange flyover (climb to 6, a high bend with
     rails, the big drop), a tunnel through a hill, a dip of hairpins, a chicane. Still 3 laps;
   - the boost strips boost Rio too; the rubber band waits for you only a little (it used to slow Rio to 85% whenever
     he led, which was the real reason the race was easy); every lost race makes him 4% gentler, up to 8 losses.
   - Calibrated with a driver that steers ahead and brakes for the corners (`autoRace` in the racer spec, run in
     Node on a stand-in world): a clean drive wins by about 6 blocks, a wobbly one loses the first race and wins
     after one loss, a very sloppy one needs several losses.
3. **Rowboats after the twelfth:** they keep rowing (wave 3's speed); a hit sinks one just for fun (nothing saved),
   it comes back 4 s later.
4. **5 goals and 5 baskets** (was 3 and 3). Keys `goal1..5` / `basket1..5`, so goals already scored still count.
   The difficulty climb (green part, keepers, Dee, the shot window) is spread over the 5 (`stageOf`).
5. **Bosses wait for their crew:** Captain Kickley (5 + 5 and Wes and Hazel: already so), Racer Rio (a won race and
   Pit Crew Pia and Max), Captain Squawk (the 12 rowboats and Dot, Lou and Fin). The guides lead to the crew.
   Nothing saved is taken away: a star already won stays won; a rematch only asks for crew not beaten yet.

Not changed: saved progress, the Playland realm version (the race zone is a keep, so no edits of old saves sat in it).

## Part 2: the owner's notes on the five wave 15 worlds (2026-10-27, the same night)

- **Doodle, the pencil "gets stuck near the edge / can't grab it back":** a real bug, two of them. (1) A blocked pencil (an
  edge, a page, an island's side) could only turn toward points *beyond* it, so facing the edge it could never turn round;
  now a stuck pencil follows any point you look at (`aimPoint` ignores the "short of the pencil" rule while `st.blocked`)
  and turns faster (`DRAW.stuckTurn` 3). (2) On a climbing line the tip can be above your eyes; the view ray then never met
  its floor and no look steered it; now the aim plane is at most just below your eyes. The blocked words say what to do.
  The careless test players still fail every line, so it is not easier, only fair.
- **Atlantis, "I didn't know which ones were glowfish and what the point was":** the game now shows it. Glowfish wear a
  glow; each lantern has 4 glass bulbs that light up one by one; fish in your net glow round you and a trail of light leads
  from you to the lantern they belong to; poured fish fly into their bulb; and until your first catch a ghost glowfish swims
  from the district's fish into the lantern and lights a bulb, again and again. Dark districts are much darker (blocks and
  water), so a lit one is a big change.
- **Funfair:** Bumper Cars need 4, 5 and 6 balloons in its three rounds (was 3, 3, 6), with a little more time.
- **Room of Doors, "why all those rooms?":** they matter now. Jolly Jax battles only after his two helpers (who hide behind
  two of the surprise doors) are beaten, and every opened door gets a sign with a picture of what is inside (a green tick when
  it is done: a helper beaten, a gift box opened), so the hall becomes your own map. The arrival words say so.
- **All Bosses wait for their helpers** (the owner's "all bosses should have previous battle conditions"): generic in
  `pictureboss.js` (`crewText` after the Boss's own gate; `bosses.crewAim` leads the guide to the nearest helper once the
  Boss's own gate is passed). Nibbles has no helpers.
- **Portal House:** not changed. The two-colour wand and "anywhere" need new puzzles, so it is a proposal for the owner:
  `plans/T47-portal-lab.md`.

## Part 3: the owner's second look (2026-10-28)

- **Funfair open for everyone** ("we can switch on the Funfair in the real game, just that one"): `comingsoon.js` `OPEN_NOW = ['fair']`,
  `worldOpen(id)`; the other four wave 15 worlds still need `?preview`.
- **Doodle, "it again got stuck at the end quoting ink blot even though the ink blot was far behind":** the real cause this time. Line 3
  has four ink blots and the live part only had meshes for three (`MAX_BLOTS = 3`), so the fourth, right by the Pond Page's landing
  strip, stopped the pencil and was never drawn. The mesh counts now come from the courses (`MAX_OF`), and the spec checks every star,
  blot and bee of every line has a mesh. (Part 2's turning fixes stay: they were real too, but not this one.)
- **Atlantis, "what are these circles? drawn so poorly":** the see-through balls are gone. Every glow (round the glowfish, the lantern
  bulbs, the trail to the lantern, the fish flying into a bulb) is a soft camera-facing sprite (`glowSprites` in atlantislive.js: one
  instanced quad, a radial falloff, additive, fading with distance); an empty bulb is a thin glass ring with a highlight, a lit one a
  warm glow. Smaller than before.
- **Atlantis, "I caught all of them in 90 seconds":** every kind gets away better (`FISH` in glowfish.js): a shy fish shoots out at 8
  (was 6) blocks a second, 5 blocks (was 4), drifts faster and hides again after 2.2 s (was 3); a darter notices you from 9 (was 7),
  flees at 11.5 (was 10) and trembles in a dead end 1.6 s (was 2.5); the school breathes every 2.2 s (was 3) and spreads to 2.4; the net
  is a little smaller (1.0) and rests 1.0 s after a lunge (was 0.8). The spec's skilled players still catch each kind with its trick, and
  a darter in open water still never.
- **Room of Doors, "is that supposed to be a slide? what's the purpose of it? ... the ball pit?":** the **Piñata Hunt**
  (`pinatahunt.js`). The rug has no piñata until you find it: the three piñatas hide in the slide room, the springs room and the ball
  pit (shuffled from your seed), one skill each: the slide piñata swings in and out over the slide (start sliding at the right moment
  to grab it on the way down), the springs piñata floats high and drifts (jump on the spring right under it), the ball-pit piñata pops
  up out of the balls away from you for a moment (the balls shake first: run to it). Found piñatas fly to the rug; a room that still
  hides one shows a piñata on its door sign; the guide points at the next unopened door, then at the room. Found is per trip; a saved
  bow needs no hunt. The confetti room and the nested doors keep their gift chests, the helpers still hide behind two doors.
- **The Room of Doors is gone** (owner, the same day: "I don't like this world even as a concept ... come up with a better version"):
  it became **Jolly Jax's Surprise Party** (`plans/T48-surprise-party.md`): one party room, the Party Spinner, four party games with
  three rounds and party surprises each, party crashers and the cake finale. Old bows are kept (the same number), the Piñata Hunt,
  the doors, the slide, the ball pit and the springs are gone.
- **The Sketchbook open for everyone** (owner, the same day: "Sketch / doodle world is now great! It can be switched on"): `OPEN_NOW` gains `'doodle'`.
- **Atlantis open for everyone** (owner later that day: "great work on the Atlantis redesign, we can switch it on"):
  `OPEN_NOW = ['fair', 'atlantis']`; the Pirate Seas use `worldOpen('atlantis')` for their size (320 wide) and version 2, so old
  version 1 Pirate Seas saves convert as the spec already checks.
- **Doodle, "the pencil's sensitivity got worse ... it got stuck near an edge (not an ink blot)":** both true, both found with a new
  test player (`wobblyKid` in `tests/doodlecheck.mjs`: follows the route roughly and, like a real kid, every other moment looks
  down at their own line to walk). With last night's rules it had the pencil wedged at the Pond Page for 72 s. Three causes:
  1. last night's "a stuck pencil follows any point you look at" also took looks along your own line, so a pencil blocked for a
     moment swung back at you. Now a stuck pencil follows only a look off to the side (`DRAW.stuckSide`, 0.6 rad);
  2. the line is 3 wide and every one of its samples had to be free, so a pencil running beside a page or a wall wedged itself on
     the smallest turn. Now only the line's centre must be free (`DRAW.core` 0); the rest is drawn where there is room;
  3. at an edge, a page or a wall (not an ink blot) the pencil now **glides along it** (`DRAW.glide`: it tries turns up to about
     105 degrees, the side you look first), so aiming at the dotted strip from beside the page slides the line onto it.
  The aim height is the line's floor again unless that is not well below your eyes. The check: the wobbly kid never has the pencil
  stuck at an edge for more than 2 s on any line (not counting the Curly Tower, where "go round to climb" is the rule); skilled
  players still win every line cleanly, careless ones still fail lines 2-8 (line 1, the easy first line, now lands by just looking at
  the strip).
