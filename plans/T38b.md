# T38b · Realms together

Wave 13 (`plans/T38.md`). **Built after T38a is merged** (it needs `REALMS.swirl` and the Portal Door, and touches the
same realm code). Read `CLAUDE.md` (Realms T31, Dream realm T35, play together, hide and seek), `plans/T31.md` sections
5 and 9, and `src/realms.js`, `src/playtogether.js`, `src/multiplayer.js` first.

**Goal:** a visiting friend can follow the host into any realm (the Dream, the Swirl, later Picture Worlds), see them,
build with them, battle there and earn that realm's badge, so father and son can play every place together. Today a
guest only gets a toast ("... is asleep in the Dream realm. Look around until they wake up!").

Everything below is written for "any realm" (`realmOf(id)`); the Dream and the Swirl are the two cases to name in text
and tests. No new blocks (step 0), no main-world block changes.

## 0. Today (what the builder must know)

- Only one place is loaded per browser: `game.place` (`'main'` or a realm id). A realm is `createWorld(realmSeed(mainSeed,
  def.seedSalt), edits, def.size, { realm: def })`. The realm terrain comes from the **main seed**, so a guest whose
  `game.world` is the host's world gets the host's realm terrain for free (`stash.seed` = host seed).
- `realms.enter(id, { bed })` stashes the main world (`stash = { seed, edits, homes, owner, ret }`), loads the realm with
  `game.realmEdits[id]`; `leave(reason)` writes `game.realmEdits = self.edits()` and rebuilds main from the stash.
- Messages: `state`, `block` and `world` carry `place` (T31). `Avatar.place` / `here`; an avatar elsewhere is hidden, not
  pickable, not a `friends` position, not in hide-and-seek `nearby()`. A `block` for another place is dropped, except a
  main-world block, which `realms.stashBlock` keeps for later.
- The guest's own world is `game.homeWorld` (set in `loadWorld`); `snapshot()` saves `homeWorld` instead of the host's
  world and leaves `place` / `realmPos` out while visiting. **But** `snapshot()` saves `realmEdits: game.realms.edits()`,
  and `edits()` writes the *current* realm's `world.edits` into the list: if a guest were in the host's realm, the
  host's Dream edits would land in the guest's own save. `leave()` does the same (`game.realmEdits = self.edits()`).
  This is the main bug to prevent.
- `useBed` for a guest: toast "You can dream in your own world. Sleep tight!". `WHERE.realm` for a guest:
  "Next: Upside-Down House · it is in a dream (dream in your own world)".
- `WORLD_VERSION` is 18 after T38a.

## 1. Decisions (recommended; the orchestrator writes "Decided" next to each)

1. **Who owns a realm's edits: the host.** Like the main world. The realm a guest walks in is the host's realm with the
   host's `realmEdits[id]`; everything built there is saved in the **host's** save only. A guest's own Dream / Swirl
   edits stay in their own save, untouched (never sent, never loaded while visiting, never overwritten).
2. **How a guest follows: an ask, no entrance needed.** When the host goes into a realm, every guest gets
   `ui.ask({ text: 'Dad is dreaming. Dream together?', yes: 'Follow', no: 'Not now' })` (Swirl: "Dad went into the
   Swirl. Go too?"). Saying no is fine; the guest can follow later with a **"Follow Dad"** button in the pause screen's
   play-together box, or by using the realm's entrance in the host's world (any Bed for the Dream, the Portal Door for
   the Swirl), which asks the same question. No distance limit (father and son may be far apart).
   - **Dream: no Bed or cozy home of the guest's own, no night check of its own.** The host is only in the Dream at
     night, and the guest's clock already follows the host's (`state.time`).
   - **Swirl: the guest does not need the Champion title.** "Your friend is a Champion, so the door opens for you too!"
     The title stays the host's (`game.champion` of the guest is never changed). A guest Champion whose host is not in
     the Swirl gets a toast: "You can go to the Swirl in your own world, or follow Dad when he goes."
   - **A guest never enters a realm on their own** (only to where the host is). Keeps one owner per place in play.
3. **Where the guest arrives:** beside the host (host's last spot on the ground, `realms.lastGround`), or at the
   realm's `arrive` spot when that cell is blocked / the host never stood. The guest's way back (`stash.ret`) is the
   **host's** `ret` (beside the host's Bed / in front of the Portal Door), so they wake up together.
4. **Host leaves the realm (Wake Stone, morning, lost battle, Portal Door back): guests come too.** A guest whose
   host's avatar is no longer in the guest's place calls `leave('friend')` ("Dad woke up, so you wake up too!" / Swirl:
   "Dad went back through the Portal Door, so you go too!"). It waits like `realms.update` already does: never during a
   battle, a duel invite or while travelling; it happens right after the battle ends.
5. **Guest leaves on their own** (Wake Stone, losing a battle, their own morning check): back to the host's main world at
   the host's `ret`, healed after a loss, exactly like the host. They may follow again (button / entrance).
6. **Host disconnects or stops sharing while the guest is in a realm:** unchanged T31 path, `ended('host-left')` ->
   `goHome()` (after a battle if in one) -> reload into the guest's own world. The guest's save holds only their own
   world (`homeWorld`) and their own `realmEdits`.
7. **Joining while the host is in a realm:** the guest loads the host's main world beside the host's return spot (today)
   and gets the follow ask at once (instead of the 500 ms "Look around until they wake up!" toast).
8. **Host in main, guest still in the realm** (only for a moment: the guest is finishing a battle, or is travelling):
   the guest's avatar is hidden for the host (today). The host's pause box says "Kid is still in the Dream realm".
   A `block` the guest sends from the realm in that window is kept by the host in `game.realmEdits[place]`
   (`realms.stashRealmBlock`, the realm twin of `stashBlock`), so nothing the guest built is lost.
9. **Battles:** wild and gym battles in a realm stay per player (as everywhere). **Duels and co-op bosses only between
   players in the same place**: `mp.pick` already needs `here`; add the same check on the receiving side of a duel ask
   (`busy` when the asker's avatar is not `here`) and in `coopFor` ("Your friend is in another place."). Both are
   allowed inside a realm. **Duel `VERSION` unchanged** (no battle rule changes).
10. **Realm gyms: a guest can earn the badge in the host's realm** (the Dream's Wonder Badge). Badges, `beaten`,
    `rematchWins`, cubes, catches, the Book are per player and saved in the guest's save as always. Healer Stella heals
    the guest too.
11. **Hide and seek:** may be played inside a realm (both players `here`, the circle works there). Any change of place by
    either player ends a running game (`hideSeek.stop()` in `enter` / `leave`, which tells the other side), because
    morning or a lost battle can move a player without asking. Starting a trip is still refused during hide and seek
    (`why()` today). The follow ask is not shown during a game (it waits until the game ends).
12. **Guide:** in the realm the guest's arrow is the realm's own (route to the realm gym, then the Wake Stone / way
    back), same as the host. In the host's main world, `WHERE.realm` for a guest says: host in that realm ->
    "Next: Upside-Down House · in Dad's dream: Follow Dad (pause -> Play together)"; else "... · it is in a dream:
    follow Dad when he sleeps". (No `tips`, the access golden fixture is the host's view and must stay the same.)
13. **`WORLD_VERSION` 19.** The `world` / `state` messages keep their fields, but a guest now needs a host that answers
    the new `realm` request, so mixed versions are refused at join ("Your friend has a newer game. Please reload the
    page!").

## 2. Messages (`src/multiplayer.js`)

- `EVENTS` += `'realm'` (Supabase channel listens to it; `?mp=local` needs nothing).
- Guest -> host: `realm { type: 'ask', id: <request id>, from, to: <host id>, realm: 'dream' }`.
- Host -> guest, when `game.place === realm` and not `realms.busy`: one or more
  `realm { type: 'part', id, to, realm, rv: def.version ?? 1, part, parts, edits: [...world.edits] sliced by
  EDITS_PER_MESSAGE, ret: stash.ret, spot: realms.lastGround ?? null }`. Otherwise `realm { type: 'no', id, to, why:
  'gone' }` (the host is already back / travelling).
- `mp.askRealm(id) -> Promise<{ edits, ret, spot } | { error }>`: assembles the parts like `join()` does, 12 s timeout.
  Errors (child words): `rv` different -> "Your friend has a newer game. Please reload the page!"; `no` -> "Dad is not
  there any more."; timeout -> "Couldn't reach Dad's dream. Try again from the pause screen."
- `hooks.realm(p)` (playtogether.js) routes `ask` to `game.realms.answer(p)` on the host; parts / no go to the pending
  promise on the guest. Only messages with `p.to === this.id` are read.
- `block` handler: a block for another place than mine -> main: `stashBlock` (today); a realm **and I am the host**:
  `game.realms.stashRealmBlock(p)`; otherwise dropped.
- `state` handler: the "fell asleep / woke up" toasts come from realm words (below): going in uses the new place's
  `words.went`, coming back uses the old place's `words.back`. Keep the Dream strings exactly
  (`fell asleep and is in the Dream realm.` / `woke up!`: the realms spec matches `/Dad fell asleep/`, `/Dad woke up/`).
- `mp.hostAvatar` getter (the `isHost` avatar) for realms.js / the pause box.

## 3. `src/realms.js`

- **Realm words** on each def (data, no `if` per realm):
  - dream: `words: { went: 'fell asleep and is in the Dream realm', back: 'woke up', ask: n => `${n} is dreaming. Dream
    together?`, follow: 'Follow', joined: n => `You followed ${n} into the dream!`, too: n => `${n} woke up, so you
    wake up too!`, guide: n => `in ${n}'s dream` }`.
  - swirl (T38a's def): `went: 'went through the Portal Door into the Swirl'`, `back: 'came back from the Swirl'`,
    `ask: n => `${n} went into the Swirl. Go too?``, `joined: n => `Your friend ${n} is a Champion, so the door opens
    for you too!``, `too: n => `${n} went back through the Portal Door, so you go too!``.
  - a missing `words` falls back to generic text ("went to the {name}", "came back") so Picture Worlds work unchanged.
- `stash.guest = true` when a guest follows. Everywhere the guest's own `game.realmEdits` would be touched, skip it:
  - `edits()`: `if (stash?.guest) return { ...game.realmEdits }` (the guest's own lists only; this is what `snapshot()`
    saves).
  - `leave()`: `if (!s.guest) game.realmEdits = self.edits()`.
  - `enter()` takes `{ bed, follow }`; `follow = { edits, ret, spot, host }`: load `createWorld(realmSeed(stash.seed,
    def.seedSalt), follow.edits, def.size, { realm: def })` (never `game.realmEdits[id]`), `stash.ret = follow.ret`
    (fall back to the guest's own spot when `ret` is not 5 finite numbers), put the player on `follow.spot` + 1.5 in x/z,
    lift up to 20 cells out of blocks (like `loadWorld`), else `game.spawnPoint`. Toast `words.joined(host)` instead of
    `enterText`; the realm-gym hint toast as for the host. **No Bed / night / Champion checks in `enter`** (they live in
    `useBed` and T38a's door function); if T38a put a Champion check inside `enter`, move it out or skip it when
    `follow` is set.
- `follow()` (guest): `why()` + "not during hide and seek"; the host's avatar must be in a realm (`hostPlace()`), else
  a toast; `await game.mp.askRealm(place)` (overlay "Dreaming..." / "Travelling..." shown while waiting), error ->
  toast; ok -> `enter(place, { follow })`.
- `offerFollow()` (guest): the ask box from decision 2 (`asked` flag like `useBed`; asking again = yes). Shown once per
  host trip (`offered = hostPlace + trip counter`, reset when the host's place changes) and on demand.
- `update()` additions, guest only (`game.mp?.role === 'guest'`), with the same "not in a battle / duel invite / busy /
  not started" guard it already has, plus `!game.hideSeek?.active && !game.ui.asking` for the ask:
  - in a realm and `hostPlace() !== game.place` -> `leave('friend')` (toast `words.too(host)`).
  - in main, `hostPlace()` is a realm and not yet offered -> `offerFollow()`.
  - (the morning check stays for everyone: the clocks are shared, so both wake together anyway.)
- `answer(p)` (host): reply as section 2. `lastGround`: set in `update()` whenever the host is in a realm and
  `player.onGround` (copy of `pos`), cleared on `leave`.
- `stashRealmBlock({ x, y, z, b, place })` (host in main or another place): bounds from `realmOf(place).size`, y < 48,
  integers; replace the index in `game.realmEdits[place]` (create the list). `applyEdits` drops cells on `fixed`
  blocks and gyms later, so no `editAllowed` here.
- `useBed(hit)` for a guest: host in a realm -> `offerFollow()`; else today's toast. T38a's Portal Door function gets
  the same first line for guests (host in the Swirl -> `offerFollow()`; else "You can go to the Swirl in your own world,
  or follow Dad when he goes." for a Champion guest, the normal locked text for others).
- `enter` / `leave` call `game.hideSeek?.active && game.hideSeek.stop()` before travelling (decision 11).
- `reset()` also clears `offered`, `lastGround`, the pending ask.

## 7. Open questions (recommended answer; orchestrator decides)

1. Must a guest follow the host, or may a guest visit the host's realm alone? **Recommended: only follow** (one owner
   per place in play, no realm without its owner; the guest wakes when the host does).
2. Does the guest need their own entrance (Bed / cozy home, night, Champion title)? **Recommended: no**; the host's
   trip is the key. The Swirl guest needs no title.
3. Ask automatically, or only a button? **Recommended: ask once per host trip + a pause-box button + the entrance**
   (a 7-year-old won't find a menu button on their own; the ask is skippable).
4. Where does the guest arrive / wake? **Recommended: beside the host on arrival, at the host's return spot on waking.**
5. When the host wakes, does the guest wake too? **Recommended: yes**, after any battle the guest is in.
6. Can the guest earn the Dream badge in the host's dream? **Recommended: yes** (badges are per player).
7. Duels / co-op / hide and seek in a realm? **Recommended: yes when both are in the same place**; hide and seek ends
   when either player changes place.
8. `WORLD_VERSION` 19? **Recommended: yes** (new request a guest depends on); duel `VERSION` stays.


## Decided (orchestrator, 2026-10-24)

Every recommended answer above is taken: follow only; the host's trip is the key (no bed, night or Champion title needed for a guest); ask once per trip plus the pause-box button and the entrance; arrive beside the host, wake at the host's return spot; the guest wakes with the host (after any battle); badges per player; duels / co-op / hide and seek only in the same place; `WORLD_VERSION` 19, duel `VERSION` unchanged by T38b. Built after T38a and T38c are merged (T38c sets duel `VERSION` 11).
