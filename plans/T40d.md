# T40d · ⚽ Ball type and Playland's Ball Stadium

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `plans/T40.md` (wave 14 shared decisions **and its "Base" section B.0-B.16**), `ROADMAP.md` "Wave 14",
`STORY.md` "The Picture Worlds" (Playland: "beat the racer, the football captain and the fair leader, and Playland
starts up again") first. Models to copy from: `plans/T37.md` (a type plan), `src/dancefloor.js` (a small live game whose
progress is saved), `src/obstaclecourse.js` (summed-dt clock, closed forms).

**What the child does:** steps out of the Stadium's way-back door in Playland's hub, walks through the gap in the hedge
and the players' tunnel into a big football stadium, runs into a big ball (or taps **Kick!**) and scores **2 goals**
past two slow goalkeepers. Captain Kickley then battles them for the Stadium's star. Behind the stadium is a basketball
court: tap **Shoot!** next to the ball and it flies in a high arc into the hoop (bonus cubes). Nothing is ever lost: a
miss, a save or an out just puts the ball back in play.

This task writes **only** inside the stadium zone box (`ZONES.stadium.box`, B.11) and its own files. T40c owns the race
zone and may restyle Playland's ground and hub; the hub, its hedge gap and the Stadium's way-back door are the base's.

## 2. Ball physics (`src/ballphysics.js`)

### 2.1 Model
- The ball is drawn as a sphere but **collides as an axis-aligned cube of half-size `r`** against the voxel grid.
  Axis-separated moves (x, then z, then y), like `Player.moveAxis`: stable, never tunnels, never gets stuck in a corner,
  trivially deterministic. With `r = 0.4` the difference to a sphere is invisible at this scale.
- State: `pos {x, y, z}` (centre), `vel {x, y, z}`, `onGround`, `acc` (time not yet simulated), `time` (summed
  sub-steps: **the stadium clock**, keepers read it), `restFor` (s at rest), `spot` (reset point), `opts` (constants).
- Fixed sub-step `h = 1/120 s`. `update(dt, obstaclesAt)`: `acc += dt`; while `acc >= h` and fewer than `maxSteps`
  (12) steps: `step(h, obstaclesAt(time))`, `time += h`, `acc -= h`; leftover above `maxSteps * h` is dropped. main.js
  already clamps a frame's dt to 0.05 s, so 1-2 fps software rendering (dt 0.05 every frame) and 60 fps (dt 1/60)
  run the same sub-steps: the same total time gives the same path (the spec checks 1e-6).
- `solid(x, y, z)` is `(x, y, z) => world.isSolid(x, y, z)` (integer cells). The physics never reads anything else of the world.

### 2.2 Constants (`BALL`, one object; the court ball overrides a few)

| Name | Football | Basketball | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `r` | 0.4 | 0.4 | half size (the hoop hole is 2 x 2) |
| `g` | 20 | 20 | gravity, blocks/s² (the player has 28: the ball floats a little longer, easier to follow) |
| `h`, `maxSteps` | 1/120, 12 | same | sub-step |
| `eGround` | 0.55 | 0.72 | bounce off the floor (basketballs bounce more) |
| `eWall` | 0.6 | 0.6 | off walls, posts, rims, nets |
| `rest` | 1.2 | 1.2 | a floor bounce slower than this stops (vy = 0, `onGround`) |
| `roll` | 2.5 | 3.0 | rolling slow-down on the ground, blocks/s² (linear) |
| `air` | 0.1 | 0.1 | drag per second (`v *= 1 - air * h`) |
| `maxSpeed` | 24 | 24 | clamp of |vel| after a kick (shots need ~20) |
| `wallStop` | 0.3 | 0.3 | a wall bounce slower than this stops that axis |

So a 10 blocks/s kick rolls about 10² / (2 x 2.5) = 20 blocks: half a pitch.

### 2.3 One sub-step `step(h, obstacles)`
```
vel.y -= g * h
moveAxis('x', vel.x * h, obstacles); moveAxis('z', vel.z * h, obstacles); moveAxis('y', vel.y * h, obstacles)
onGround = overlaps(pos.x, pos.y - 0.02, pos.z)            // touching a floor
if onGround and vel.y <= 0: s = hypot(vel.x, vel.z); s2 = max(0, s - roll * h); scale vel.x/z by s2 / s (s > 0)
vel *= (1 - air * h)
```
`overlaps(x, y, z)`: any `solid(cx, cy, cz)` for `cx` in `floor(x - r) .. floor(x + r - 1e-6)` (same for y, z), or any
obstacle box overlapping the ball box.

`moveAxis(axis, d)` (d = 0: nothing):
- `next = pos + d` on that axis. If `!overlaps(next)`: move, done.
- Hit a **cell**: snap like the player: `d > 0`: `pos = floor(next + r) - r - 0.001`; `d < 0`: `pos = floor(next - r) + 1 + r + 0.001`.
  Then the velocity on that axis: y-down: `vel.y = -vel.y * eGround`, if `|vel.y| < rest` then `vel.y = 0`; any other:
  `vel = -vel * eWall`, if `|vel| < wallStop` then 0. Return a `bounce` event `{ axis, speed: |old vel| }`.
- Hit an **obstacle** (a keeper box, 2.6): snap to the obstacle's face the same way, and
  `vel[axis] = obs.v[axis] + (obs.v[axis] - vel[axis]) * eWall` (a moving keeper knocks it away), event `{ kind: 'keeper' }`.
- A step is at most 24 / 120 = 0.2 blocks, smaller than a post (1) and the ball (0.8): nothing can be skipped.

### 2.4 Never stuck, never lost (start of every `update`)
- `NaN` anywhere -> `reset()`.
- Ball box inside a solid cell or obstacle (a keeper walked into a ball at rest, a live block): try `pos.y + 1`, then
  `+ 2`; if still inside, `reset()`. For a keeper: push the ball out along the keeper's moving axis (z) to its face; if
  that spot is solid (ball against a post), push along x out of the goal instead.
- `reset(spot)`: `pos = spot`, `vel = 0`, `restFor = 0`.
- The outer rules (out of the pitch, resting high on a roof or rim, a goal) are in `StadiumLive` (section 5), so the
  physics module stays world-agnostic.

### 2.5 The player and the ball (`touchKick`, `canBoot`, `boot`, `aimShot`)
The ball **never moves the player** (it is not solid for the player; nothing in `player.js` changes), so it can never
push the player into a wall. The player is passed as `pl = { x, y, z, vx, vz, yaw, halfW: 0.3, height: 1.8 }`
(feet position, `Player.vel`).

`touchKick(ball, pl, now)` (once per frame, before `ball.update`; returns `'kick' | 'nudge' | null`):
- Touch = the player's box grown by 0.05 overlaps the ball box (`|dx| < 0.75`, `|dz| < 0.75`, `ball.y - r < pl.y + 1.8`,
  `ball.y + r > pl.y`), and `now - ball.kickedAt >= 0.25` s.
- `s = hypot(pl.vx, pl.vz)`, `f = (-sin yaw, -cos yaw)` (facing, horizontal), `d` = unit vector player -> ball (horizontal).
- **Kick** (`s >= 0.5`): `dir = dot(f, d) > 0.3 ? f : d` (in front: where you look; a strafe or back-step: away from you);
  `speed = clamp(1.7 * s, 3, 10.5)` (walking 7.3, sprinting 10.5); `vel = dir * speed`, `vel.y = max(vel.y, 1.2)` (a hop).
  Move the ball to `pl + dir * (0.3 + r + 0.06)` (same y) if that box is free, so it does not touch again next frame.
- **Nudge** (`s < 0.5`, the ball rolled into a standing player): if the ball moves towards the player, reflect its
  horizontal velocity on `d` with factor 0.5 and move it out along `d` if free.
- `ball.kickedAt = now` (now = `ball.time`, so tests are deterministic).

`canBoot(ball, pl)`: horizontal centre distance <= 2.3, `|ball.y - (pl.y + 0.4)| <= 1.5`, `dot(f, d) >= 0.5` (in front,
60°), or distance <= 1.0 (right at your feet counts from any side). This is what makes **Kick!** / **Shoot!** appear.

`boot(ball, v)`: `ball.vel = v` clamped to `maxSpeed` (shots bypass the clamp only via `shotTo`'s numbers, all < 24),
`kickedAt = time`, `restFor = 0`.

**Kick!** (the pitch): `aimShot(ballPos, f, mouths)`: for each goal mouth (centre `m`, half width `hw`), the direction
to the nearest point of the mouth shrunk by 1 block from each post. If the facing is within 25° of that direction and
the mouth is within 28 blocks, use that direction (**gentle aim help** for a child), else `f`. `v = dir * 13`,
`v.y = 3` (a low drive under the crossbar; the keeper is 1.8 tall, so a shot can still be saved).

**Shoot!** (the court): `shotTo(from, target, g, slope = 2)` gives the arc that reaches `target` while falling at
least `slope` times faster than it moves sideways (so it drops cleanly through a 2 x 2 hole):
```
d = horizontal distance, dy = target.y - from.y
T = sqrt(2 * (dy + slope * d) / g)          // flight time: the smallest that is steep enough at the end
vh = d / T (towards the target), vy = dy / T + g * T / 2
```
`target` = the hole centre at `rimMid = G + 5.5` (middle of the rim layer). From 2 blocks: T 0.9 s, peak 4.6 above the
ball; from 12 blocks: T 1.7 s, peak 9 (the realm is tall enough; the spec checks the peak against `WORLD_H`). The shot
picks the hoop you face (`dot(f, toHoop) > 0.5`, horizontal distance 1.5..15), else the nearer hoop in front
(`dot > 0`), else a **lob** forward (`f * 6`, `vy 9`). Every shot from the court scores (checked in Node for every court
cell); that is on purpose: the court is the "always works" bonus.

### 2.6 Keeper motion (closed form) `keeperAt(k, t, hard)`
`z(t) = c + A * sin(2π t / P + φk)`, `vz(t) = A * (2π / P) * cos(...)`; `x` fixed just in front of the goal line.
`A = 2.6`, `P = 6 s` (peak speed 2.7 blocks/s, slower than a walking child), `φ0 = 0`, `φ1 = π / 2` (the two keepers
are out of step). `hard` (any rematch, i.e. the star is already won): `A = 2.9`, `P = 4.5`. The keeper box is
`x ± 0.35`, `z ± 0.7` (arms out), `y = G+1 .. G+2.9`. The mouth is 8 wide, so a keeper covers 1.4 of it: most shots go
in, a shot straight at him is saved. `t` = `ball.time` of the football (the same clock moves keepers and ball).

## 5. `StadiumLive` (`src/stadiumlive.js`, `LIVE.stadium`)

### 5.1 Construction (`new StadiumLive(game, part)`; made by `gymLive.reset` for `world.parts`, B.9)
- Two `Ball` objects: `this.foot = new Ball(solid, part.ballSpot, BALL)`, `this.hoop = new Ball(solid, part.courtSpot, { ...BALL, eGround: 0.72, roll: 3 })`.
- Meshes (all `userData.ball = true`, added to `game.world.scene`, removed and disposed in `dispose()`):
  - Football: `SphereGeometry(0.4, 16, 12)` with **vertex colours**: black where the vertex direction is within
    `dot > 0.92` of one of the 12 icosahedron vertex directions (patches), white elsewhere; `MeshLambertMaterial({ vertexColors: true })`.
  - Basketball: orange (`0xf07a28`), black where `abs(n.x) < 0.05`, `abs(n.y) < 0.05`, or `abs(abs(n.z) - 0.7) < 0.05` (seams).
  - One shadow each: `CircleGeometry(0.42, 16)`, black, `opacity 0.3`, `depthWrite: false`, lying on the first solid
    top under the ball (`+0.02`), scaled `1 / (1 + 0.15 * height)`. Helps a child judge where a high ball lands.
  - **4 draw calls** for balls and shadows.
- **Keepers** are the live part's own meshes, not NPCs (B.8 places only Bosses and crew): `buildPerson(colors, { look: 'sport' })`
  from `npc.js`, green shirts (Keeper Gus west `0x3aa84a`, Keeper Gail east `0x2a9a8a`), two big orange glove boxes added
  to the arm groups (the stadium's own touch: no `npc.js` change). Moved and turned by `update`, legs swing while moving
  (`parts.legs` rotation like `npc.steer`), `disposeModel` + removed in `dispose()`. They are not pickable and never battle;
  their words come as toasts (section 8).
- `this.playing = () => (game.playing ? game.playing() : true)` (tests replace it, like `DojoLive`).
- `this.bonus = 0` (cubes from baskets this trip), `this.goalAt = null`, `this.outAt = { foot: null, hoop: null }`, `this.told = false`.

### 5.2 `update(dt)` (every frame)
1. If `game.inBattle`, `game.ui.asking`, `game.ui.panelOpen` or `!this.playing()`: draw only, return (balls and keepers
   stand still; B.9: clocks only out of battle).
2. Sleep: a ball at rest more than 60 blocks from the player skips its physics.
3. The player as `pl` (from `game.player.pos`, `vel`, `yaw`). For each ball: `touchKick(ball, pl)`; `ballkick` on a kick.
4. `foot.update(dt, t => this.keeperBoxes(t))`, `hoop.update(dt, () => [])`. Bounce events faster than 3 play a quiet
   `ballkick` (at most 6 a second). A `keeper` event while the ball moved towards that goal faster than 2 = **a save**:
   toast `Saved by Keeper Gus! Try again!` (or Gail; at most every 4 s).
5. Keepers: `keeperAt(k, foot.time, hard)` (hard = `game.stars.includes('stadium')`) -> mesh position (y 11), facing the pitch.
6. **Goal** (`goalOf(part, foot.pos)` -> goal index or -1), while not already celebrating: `goalAt = foot.time`, sound
   `goalcheer`, push the next missing key `pw:stadium:goal1..3` to `game.beaten` while fewer than `need()` are there
   (`need()` = `GOALS.first` 2, or `GOALS.rematch` 3 once the star is won), `game.save()`, toast (section 8). 1.5 s
   later (`foot.time`): `foot.reset()`, sound `whistle`.
7. **Out**: football centre outside `pitchBox` (through a tunnel, over a stand), or at rest (`onGround`, speed < 0.1)
   higher than y 12.5 for 1 s (on a goal roof, a stand step): 0.8 s later `foot.reset()`, toast
   `Out! The ball goes back to the middle.` Same for the court ball with `courtBox` / `courtSpot` (a rim or backboard top
   counts as high).
8. **Basket**: `basketCross(part, prevY, hoop.pos)` -> sound `netswish`; while `this.bonus < 3`: `game.cubes++`,
   `this.bonus++`, `ui.renderHud()`, toast `Swish! 🏀 +1 Capture Cube`; then `Swish! 🏀`. No reset (it drops through).
9. First time inside `part.area` this trip (`told`): toast `The Ball Stadium! Run into the ball, or tap Kick!`
   (desktop: `... or press E to kick!`).
10. Draw: mesh positions, rolling (horizontal move since last frame `dx, dz`, axis `(dz, 0, -dx)`, angle `len / r`,
    `quaternion.premultiply`), shadows.

Keeper boxes in the physics use `keeperAt(k, t)` at the sub-step's own time, so the keeper the ball meets is exactly
where the keeper is drawn.

### 5.3 `near(how)` and `use()` (new generic hooks, section 7)
- `near(how)`: the nearest ball with `canBoot`. Football: `{ prompt: \`Ball · ${how} to kick\`, action: 'Kick!' }`;
  court ball: `{ prompt: \`Ball · ${how} to shoot\`, action: 'Shoot!' }`; else `null`. Never while step 1 says no.
- `use()`: the same check; Kick: `boot(foot, aimShot(...))`; Shoot: `boot(hoop, shotTo(...))`; sound `ballkick`; `true`.

### 5.4 `talkPrompt(npc)` / `battleEnded(result, opts)`
- `talkPrompt(npc)` (asked before `game.bosses.talkPrompt`, B.8): the captain while goals are missing ->
  `{ verb: 'talk', action: 'Talk' }`; otherwise `null` (the base's "Challenge!" / "Rematch!").
- No `talk` hook: boss and crew talks go straight to `game.bosses.talk`; the "score first" answer is the def's `gate`
  (sections 6, 7).
- `battleEnded('won', opts)` with `opts.picture?.id === 'stadium'` and `opts.picture.crew == null` (the Boss, first star
  or rematch): remove the three `pw:stadium:goal*` keys from `game.beaten`, so the next match starts at 0 (a rematch then
  needs 3). This runs after `bosses.won` (main.js calls `gymLive.battleEnded` last). A lost Boss battle keeps the goals.

### 5.5 `aim(p)` (new generic hook, section 7), guide words
Only inside the zone box (else `null`, and the base's `pictureAim` aims at the captain or the way back). Returns
`{ x, z, text: (a, m) => ... }` like `pictureAim`:

| State | Arrow at | Text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| goals < need, more than 8 from the football | the football | `⚽ Kick the ball into a goal! (${n} / ${need}) ${a} ${m}m` |
| goals < need, near the football | the nearer goal's middle | `⚽ Kick it into the goal! (${n} / ${need}) ${a} ${m}m` |
| goals done | `null` (the base aims at the captain: "⭐ ⚽ Ball Stadium Boss ...") | |
| star won, no rematch match started (`n = 0`) | `null` (the base aims at the way back) | |

### 5.6 Playing together (decided: **the ball is local per player**)
Each browser has its own two balls and keepers, exactly like gym live parts: nothing is sent, nothing new in
`multiplayer.js`, no `WORLD_VERSION` change. A friend's avatar never touches your ball, and **a friend's goal does not
count for you** (you only see your own ball). Goals and stars are per player (`beaten` keys, `game.stars`). A guest who
followed the host into Playland plays the same match on their own. A shared ball (host authority, like backlog S1/S2)
is a later idea, not this task.

## 10. Spec `ball` (`tests/specs/ball.mjs`)

   never below that at any sub-step.
2. Roll: a 10 blocks/s kick on flat ground stops after 18-22 blocks, straight (z unchanged).
3. Walls: 200 seeded (`mulberry32`) shots at 5-22 blocks/s into a wall, a post (1 x 3), a corner and under a crossbar:
   the ball box is never inside a solid cell after any sub-step; it comes back out; no NaN.

   touching the rim; peak below `WORLD_H - 2`.
8. Keepers: `keeperAt` matches the formula, the keeper box stays 0.4 inside each post; a drive straight at the keeper is
   saved (a `keeper` event, no goal), a drive at the open side scores; `hard` is faster.
9. Aim help: facing 20° off a goal gives a shot inside the mouth; 40° off does not change the direction.

## Decided (planner's recommendations and the orchestrator's decisions)
- The ball collides as a cube of half-size 0.4 with axis-separated moves and fixed 1/120 s sub-steps: simple, stable,
  deterministic, no tunnelling. A true sphere-vs-voxel solver was rejected (corner cases, no visible gain).
- The ball never moves the player and is not solid for the player: no change to `player.js`.
- **Orchestrator: the first win needs 2 goals** (either goal; two slow keepers). A rematch needs 3. Each goal is saved at
  once (`beaten` key), so leaving never loses one; the keys are cleared when the captain is beaten.
- **Kick!** has gentle aim help (25°) towards a goal; **Shoot!** always scores from the court (no launch pad needed).
- Baskets are a bonus only: 1 Capture Cube each, the first 3 per trip. They never gate anything.
- **Orchestrator: the ball is local per player; a friend's goal does not count for you.** Nothing is sent.
- Wes and Hazel are crew (bonus battles, never a gate); the keepers are the part's own meshes and never battle.
- The stadium is the same in every world (no seed), `fixed` blocks, one keep box over the zone; it is centred on the
  gate (x 58), so hedge gap, tunnels and court gap line up.
- Wild Ball creatures hop with the existing `parts.bounce` animation; no new gait.
- New generic hooks: `gymLive.near / use / aim`, Boss `gate(game)`; no realm or part ids in shared code.

## Built (the builder's notes, 2026-10-25: where the code differs from the plan above)

- **Hooks:** the base already had `gymLive.near(p)` / `use(p)` / `aim(p)` and the main.js lines; `near` returns the button word as a plain string (`'Kick!'` / `'Shoot!'`), main.js builds the prompt. `PLAYLAND_PARTS.stadium` carries its own `walk: [PITCH_DARK, PITCH_LIGHT, PITCH_LINE]` (no change to `REALMS.playland.walk`). The Boss `gate(game)` is the base's (reads `goalState(game)` from `stadium.js`). The generic `talk` order is the base's: `StadiumLive` has no `talk` at all.
- **Shoot! geometry:** the plan's slope-2 arc alone clipped the hoop's rim from short range and the backboard from behind. Final rules (`SHOT` / `shootable` in `stadium.js`, `shotTo(..., over)` in `ballphysics.js`): shots from 3.6 to 15 blocks away, not more than 1 behind the hole's middle (under the backboard) and not within 3.6 (Chebyshev) of it (under the rim) lob forward instead; the arc is made high enough to clear the 3.4-wide rim square by 1 block; the court ball has no air drag (`BALL_COURT.air` 0) and `shotTo` pays back the `g h t / 2` the sub-steps lose. The spec shoots from every court cell in range: 608 of 608 score without touching a block.
- **Rolling:** air drag only applies while the ball is in the air, so a 10 blocks/s kick rolls 20 blocks as the plan says.
- **Keepers:** the hard keeper tops out at 4.05 blocks/s (the plan's numbers); the spec allows < 4.3 (a walk). Keeper boxes carry `id` for the save toast.
- **Look:** the stand's side tile is a blue seat back over grey concrete; the court boards are two long boards a tile (calmer from far away); the football's patches are 12 black dots (cos 0.945).
- **Species:** four shapes with a `shine` L on every model; Smashbird's rackets stick out sideways and it wears a white sweatband; Sealbop / Beachboomer's body is a white cube-ball with six coloured stripes on every side.
- **Spec:** `ball` (BALL_ONLY=node|browser); the Node part checks the stadium build, the browser part drives the live part with `L.update(dt)` loops inside one `evaluate` (no pointer lock needed, deterministic).
- **Other files touched:** `tests/specs/picture-worlds.mjs` (the "none ready" check now allows the wave 14 worlds), `tests/pending-types.mjs` (`ball` removed), the fx / sound fallback loops lose `'ball'`, `tests/perf.mjs` (`--realm=playland` adds the stadium views and an fps run with the football kicked every 2 s).
