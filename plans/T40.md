### Shared rules for every Picture World
- A Picture World is **not a gym**: no badge, not in `GYMS` (the Tower needs exactly `GYMS.length` = 32 badges).
  Exploring is free; there is **no fail state that loses progress** (a fall or a miss puts you back a little, never
  at the start of the world; nothing is ever taken away).
- **Do things, don't solve puzzles** (owner: riding was the big hit). Short, child-friendly text; iPad first.
- It ends with a **Boss** (base: `PictureBoss`), who gives the world's **star** and a short note from the Wizard.
- The way back to the Swirl is always easy to find (the base's way-back door + the guide).
- Realms together (T38b) must keep working: a guest follows the host from the Swirl into a Picture World and back
  (generic realm fields, no realm ids in follow code). Stars are per player like badges.
- Every new realm terrain is data + a builder in its own file (like `dreamland.js` / `swirlland.js`), Node-runnable
  where possible so the spec can check it for three seeds. Live parts (fans, trains, cannons, races, balls...) follow
  `gymlive.js` / `LiveCells` / `setLive` rules (never edits, never inside the player) or are their own meshes
  removed on world reset; summed frame dt clocks, closed forms, no `Math.random` in anything two players must agree on.
- Reuse: `world.lifts` (T38a lift streams) for wind/updrafts, `bounce` blocks (T35) for springs, `Watcher` /
  `Hideout` (sneak.js, T37b) for a sleeping cat, `GAITS` (creatures.js) for wild walks (T36 `hopaway`, T37 `blink`).
