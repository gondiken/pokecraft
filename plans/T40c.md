   Playland's own `spawns` table.
2. **The `dash` gait** (creatures.js): a Racer revs for a moment, then zooms away from you in a straight line; after 3

   pads pulse and the start lights cycle.
6. Spec `racer`, README / CLAUDE.md lines.

## 4. The `dash` gait (creatures.js, `/* ==== T40c racer ==== */` after the T37 section)

A Racer sits still, watching you. Come within `notice` and it **revs** (shakes for `rev` s, a little dust, turns to its
way out), then **dashes** in a straight line away from you, fast (`speed` 13 blocks/s, faster than you can sprint),
stops, looks back (`pause`). After `tiredAfter` 3 dashes (or when it has no straight way out) it is out of breath for
`tired` 9 s (the existing `onTired` toast: "... is out of breath! Walk into it to battle."). Bumps start battles (the
existing edge-triggered T36 touch, `bump: true`).

```js
Object.assign(GAITS, {
  dash: { notice: 7, noticeUp: 4, rev: 0.3, dists: [9, 7, 5], speed: 13, pause: 0.5, tiredAfter: 3, tired: 9,
    up: 1, down: 1, drop: 2, leash: 20, angles: [0, 25, -25, 50, -50, 80, -80, 120, -120], bump: true },
});
```
- `updateGait`: first line becomes two lines: `if (w.species.gait === 'dash') return this.updateDash(w, dt); // T40c`.
- **`dashLine(g, w, ax, az, dist)`** -> `[{ x, y, z }, ...]` (cell centres, one per block along the line) or null. From
  the creature's cell, step `i = 1..dist` to column `(floor(x0 + ax * i), floor(z0 + az * i))`. For each column: a
  standing y within `prev.y - g.down .. prev.y + g.up`, and within `y0 - g.drop .. y0 + g.up` overall (so never off a
  ledge); here and below never `B.WATER`; air for body and head; solid below; inside the map (1..W-2); not
  `world.fenced`. When a step changes both x and z, one of the two side cells must also be air at body height (no
  squeezing through a diagonal gap). The whole line must be valid (a short line is tried with the next `dists`).
- **`chooseDash(w, g, p)`**: direction = away from the player (fallback: its heading), angles tried in order (mirrored
  every other dash, like `chooseBlink`), for each angle the `dists` in order; skip ends outside `leash` from `w.home` and
  ends that are not at least 2 further from the player than now. Returns `{ cells, to }` or null. No `Math.random`.
- **`updateDash(w, dt)`**: states in `w.dash` = `{ phase: 'rev' | 'go', t, cells }`.
  - `rev`: `t += dt`; model jitters (`model.position.x += Math.sin(w.t * 60) * 0.03`), heading eases to the way out;
    at `t >= g.rev` -> `go` (sound `dashzoom` if the player is within 20, a small dust puff behind it).
  - `go`: closed form: `k = min(1, t / (n / g.speed))` with n = cells, eased `e = 1 - (1 - k)^2`; x/z lerp from the
    start to the last cell, y = the height of cell `floor(e * n)` eased towards the next (no stairs pop); model leans
    forward (`rotation.x = -0.2 * (1 - e)`), `animateModel(model, w.t, true)` (wheels spin). At `k >= 1`: land exactly
    on the last cell, `w.dash = null`, `w.pause = g.pause`, `++w.hops >= g.tiredAfter` -> `getTired`.
  - Not dashing: like `hopaway` (tired: panting; near and `pause <= 0`: `chooseDash`, null -> `getTired`; else turn
    slowly to watch you).
  - Frozen (battle): the existing `w.frozen` branch already resets scale; also clear `w.dash` there if set.
- `puff(at, opts)`: give the T37 `puff` an optional second argument `{ color, sound }` (defaults = today's smoke and
  `'smokepuff'`), so the dust is a pale sand colour and plays no sound. One-line signature change, behaviour unchanged.
- A Racer on the race track during a race: nothing special (it dashes away; bumps are possible but rare; the race
  pauses in any battle, section 6.6).

## 6. The race

### 6.1 Rules (Decided)

- **The race is the gate to the Boss battle.** Talk to Racer Rio at the start line -> the race starts at once (no
  question box; the action button says **Race!**). Win -> Rio says his line and the button says **Battle!**: talking
  to him starts the base's Boss battle. Lose the race -> "Race again!", nothing lost.
- **2 laps** (`RACE.laps`). One lap is 167 blocks: a sprinting child (6.2 blocks/s) needs about 54 s for the race.
- **Start lights 3-2-1-GO**: you and Rio are put on the grid; three red lamps light one per second (`racebeep`), then
  all three turn green (`racego`, toast "GO!"). Moving more than 1.2 blocks off your grid spot before GO puts you
  back ("Wait for GO!", once per countdown).
- **Flags** (checkpoints): the start line plus 3 flag arches. Pass them in order. Corners may be cut over the grass,
  and the grass behind the chicane is a real **shortcut** (5 blocks shorter), but a missed flag is not counted: the
  guide points back at it ("Missed a flag! Follow the arrow back to the 🚩"). Going the wrong way through a flag:
  "Wrong way! Turn around." (at most every 5 s). Nothing is ever taken away.
- **Run or ride**: running, sprinting and riding any creature (land, water on land, sky) all count. The track is a keep
  that riders may enter (`rideIn`, section 7.3).
- **Boost pads** (3): standing on one zooms you 5 blocks along the track in 0.36 s (like the Obstacle Course's pusher:
  `p.moveAxis` steps, walls stop it). Rio's speed profile is faster on them too.
- **Easy on purpose (rubber band)**: Rio slows down when he is ahead and speeds up a little when he is far behind;
  every lost race makes him gentler at the end. Sprinting wins the first race; walking wins the second (table 6.4).
- **A won race is kept for the session** (`live.won`): losing the Boss battle does not mean racing again; talk to Rio
  and battle. After the star, a rematch (base) = race again, then battle (racing is the fun part).
- **Stopping**: walking out of the zone box or more than 40 blocks from the track ends the race ("Race stopped. Talk to
  Rio to race again."); not a loss. Any battle (a wild bump, a friend's duel ask accepted) **pauses** the race clock and
  Rio; it goes on after the battle.
- Nothing about the race is saved or sent. Each player races their own Rio (the Boss person is local to each browser);
  a friend sees their own Rio standing. Racing a friend is a later idea (not in this task).

### 6.4 Rio's speed (closed form of his track distance; simulated)

Rio's position is a closed form of one number, his track distance `bossS` (`track.at(bossS, 1.5)`). `bossS` grows by
`bossSpeed(...) * dt` every frame of the **race clock** (summed frame dt, only while racing and not paused, like
`CourseLive.clock`). No `Math.random`.

```js
export const RACE = {
  laps: 2, lane: 1.5, grid: -1.5,                 // Rio's lane; both start 1.5 behind the line
  count: 3, hold: 1.2,                            // countdown seconds; how far you may move before GO
  speed: { straight: 6.0, corner: 5.0, chicane: 4.4, boost: 7.6 },
  band: { near: 1, slow: 0.08, min: 0.55, behind: 3, fast: 0.03, max: 1.12, ease: 0.06, final: 45, cap: [0.75, 0.5, 0.35] },
  boost: { dist: 5, speed: 14 },                  // a boost pad zooms you 5 blocks at 14 blocks/s
  leave: 40,                                      // further than this from the track ends the race
  parade: { speed: 5, lanes: [-1.5, 1.5] },
};
// lead = bossS - P (P = your progress, 6.5). losses = races lost this session (0, 1, 2+).
export function bossSpeed(bossS, lead, losses, track) {
  const b = RACE.band, end = RACE.laps * track.L;
  let f = 1;
  if (lead > b.near) f = Math.max(b.min, 1 - b.slow * (lead - b.near));          // ahead: slows down
  else if (lead < -b.behind) f = Math.min(b.max, 1 + b.fast * (-lead - b.behind)); // far behind: a little faster
  f *= 1 - b.ease * Math.min(losses, 3);                                          // gentler after every lost race
  if (bossS > end - b.final && (losses > 0 || lead > 0)) f = Math.min(f, b.cap[Math.min(losses, 2)]); // waves to the crowd
  return RACE.speed[track.kindAt(bossS)] * f;
}
```
Simulation (a player at a constant speed along the centreline, no boosts, dt 1/30 and 1/8; the spec re-runs it with
`raceRun(v, losses, dt)`, a pure loop in racetrack.js):

| losses | 3.0 (slow walk) | 4.3 (walk) | 5.0 | 6.2 (sprint / full stick) | 9.5 (riding, sprint) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Rio, by 6.7 blocks | **Rio, by a nose** (< 1) | Rio, by a nose | **you, by 17** | you, by 121 |
| 1 | Rio, by ~1 | **you, by 14** | you, by 22 | you, by 40 | you |
| 2 | **you, by 12** | you, by 25 | you | you | you |

So: sprinting wins the first try, walking wins the second, even a slow walk wins the third. Losing shows a tip:
after loss 1 "Tip: run! Hold Shift, or push the stick all the way."; after loss 2 "Tip: ride a creature to go even
faster!". The spec asserts the bold cells (and that 1/30 and 1/8 give the same winners).

### 6.5 Flags, laps and your progress

- Every frame while racing: `prev` = last frame's feet (x, z), `cur` = now. The **next flag** `next` (index into
  `gates`, 0..3) is passed when the segment prev -> cur crosses its gate line `a`-`b` (2D segment intersection) moving
  forwards (`dot(cur - prev, gateDir) > 0`) and your feet are within `Y - 1 .. Y + 9` (riders and flyers count). A
  segment test, not a box, so riding (9.5 blocks/s), boosts (14 blocks/s) and slow test frames never jump over a flag.
- Crossing a flag backwards: "Wrong way! Turn around." (toast at most every 5 s), nothing changes.
- At GO: `lap = 0`, `next = 0` (crossing the start line just after GO starts lap 1). After G3 `next = 4`: crossing G0
  then is a lap (`lap++`, `next = 1`; toast "Last lap!" + `lapbell`, or the finish). Finish when `lap === RACE.laps`.
- **Your progress** `P` (for the rubber band and "1st / 2nd"): `proj = track.project(x, z)`. With `next = 0`:
  `P = proj.s - L` if `proj.s > L / 2` else `proj.s` (just behind or just over the line). With `next = k` (1..4):
  `s = proj.s` (if `k === 4` and `s < gates[3].s - L / 2`, `s += L`), clamped to `[gates[k-1].s, k === 4 ? L : gates[k].s]`,
  `P = lap * L + s`. So cutting across the grass never gives more progress than the next flag.
- Rio finishes when `bossS >= RACE.laps * L` (he started at -1.5 like you). If both finish in the same frame, you win.
- Missed flag hint: `next`'s gate is more than 25 track blocks behind `proj.s` (wrapping) -> toast once per flag "Missed
  a flag! Follow the arrow back to the 🚩" and the guide aims at it (it always aims at `next` anyway).

### 6.6 `RaceLive` (racetrack.js; `LIVE.race = RaceLive` or the base's registry)

Constructor `(game, part)`: keeps `part`, `track = part.track`, makes a `LiveCells` for the lights and pads, the kart
mesh (lazy), state `idle`. All per player, all in memory.

| State | What happens | Rio | Action button (`talkPrompt`) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `idle` | Rio stands on his grid spot facing the player (normal npc turning, `steer` off). Boost pads still work (fun). | standing | **Race!** (`verb: 'race'`); after a loss **Race again!**; with the race won this session **Battle!** (`talk` returns false so the base's Boss battle starts) |
| `count` | `talk` (Race!): hide-and-seek active -> toast "Finish hide and seek first!" and stay idle; else put you on `part.grid.player` (`p.pos.set`, yaw +x), Rio on `part.grid.boss`; `countT` += dt: lamp `i` red at `countT >= i` (i = 0, 1, 2; `racebeep` + toast "3" / "2" / "1"), at `countT >= 3`: all green, `racego`, toast "GO!" -> `race`. Hold rule (6.1). | on the grid, `steer` on, `moving = false`, kart under him | `{ verb: 'wait', action: 'Ready...' }` |
| `race` | `clock += dt` (not while paused); `bossS += bossSpeed(...) * dt` (after `delay` = 0); flags/laps/P (6.5); boost pads; leave check; green lamps go dark 2 s after GO. | `n.pos` / `n.heading` from `track.at(bossS, 1.5)`, `n.moving = false` (he sits in the kart; legs still) | `{ verb: 'race', action: 'Go go go!' }` (talking does nothing) |
| `done` | You won: `won = true`, toast "You won the race! 🏆" + sound `win`, Rio's `raceWin` line; you lost: `losses++`, Rio's `raceLose` line + the tip. Rio drives on to `bossS = laps * L + 4` and stops; after 3 s -> `idle` (Rio back on his grid spot; the kart hides). | slows to a stop | as `idle` |

- **Paused** = `game.inBattle || game.ui.asking || game.ui.panelOpen || game.paused` (the same checks as
  `DojoLive.canSpot`; use whatever the pause screen flag is called in main.js). Nothing moves while paused.
- **`stand(x, y, z, id)`**: `id` is `BOOST_PAD` or `BOOST_PAD_LIT` and no zoom is running -> `zoom = { dir, left:
  RACE.boost.dist }` (the pad's `dir`), `boostzoom`, first time per session toast "Zoom! Boost pads make you fast."
  (`game.toldOnce`). `update`: `step = min(left, 14 * dt)`, `p.moveAxis('x' or 'z', sign * step)`; stop when the
  position moved less than half the step (a wall) or `left` is 0 (copy CourseLive's shove).
- **Lamps** (`part.lights`: 3 cells) and **pads** (`part.pads` cells) are drawn with `LiveCells` / `world.setLive`
  only (never an edit, never inside the player).
- **The kart**: `buildModel('vroomster')` scaled with `fitRide` (riding.js), added to the scene while Rio drives;
  placed at Rio's feet each frame, Rio's person lifted by `fit.seat`, `animateModel(kart, t, true)` (wheels spin);
  removed and disposed (`disposeModel`) in `dispose()`.
- **`battleEnded(result, opts)`**: the Rio battle (2.5) won -> `won = false`, `losses = 0` (a rematch races again);
  lost -> `won` stays true. Any other battle: nothing (the race just un-pauses).
- **`aim(p)`** for the guide (section 9). **`info()`** for tests: `{ state, lap, next, P, bossS, losses, won, clock,
  countT, zoom, parade }`; `live.now`-style test setters are not needed (everything follows `dt`).
- **Starting up again** (with the race star, state `idle`, you within 60 blocks of the zone): two parade karts
  (`buildModel('vroomster')`, `buildModel('karthog')`, plain meshes, not creatures, never pickable) drive laps at
  `RACE.parade.speed` in lanes -1.5 / +1.5, half a lap apart, from a parade clock (summed dt): `track.at(clock * 5 +
  i * L / 2, lane)`. The boost pads pulse (`BOOST_PAD` <-> `BOOST_PAD_LIT` every 0.5 s of the clock, within 30 blocks
  of you) and the start lamps cycle red -> green slowly. Hidden during a race and outside the zone. Removed on dispose.

## 14. Decided (recommendations, the orchestrator may change them)

1. The race is the **gate** to the Boss battle (not the whole Boss challenge): win the race, then battle Rio for the star.
2. **2 laps** of a 167-block loop (~54 s sprinting). Laps make "Last lap!" exciting; one lap felt too short to come back.
3. **No question box** to start: Rio's action button says **Race!** and starts the countdown.
4. A rubber band that makes sprinting win at once and walking win on the second try; after every loss Rio gets gentler
   (table 6.4). No head start trick, no "Easy/Hard" choice.
5. Riding is allowed (and fun: two Racer species can be ridden). The race zone is a `rideIn` keep.
6. Missed flags are not punished, only pointed at; cutting corners and the chicane grass shortcut are allowed.
7. A won race is kept for the session; a lost Boss battle needs no new race. A rematch races again.
8. Any battle pauses the race; leaving the zone stops it (not a loss). Nothing is saved or sent.
9. Two optional Pit Crew helpers (60-62) in the pit garage, if the base supports helpers.
10. Playland "starting up" for the race zone = two parade karts, pulsing boost pads, cycling start lamps (only with the
    star). Festival bunting is inside the race zone. Playland-wide decoration (hub flags, still rides) is the base's
    (or T40d's / wave 15's) zone; this task does not draw outside `race`.
11. The `dash` gait is its own gait (not a `hopaway` option): straight-line ground runs, rev warning, tired after 3.
12. Rio's team is 4 creatures (61-64), the easy side.
