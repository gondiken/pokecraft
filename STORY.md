# The Story of PokeCraft

The lore behind the game (owner, 2026-10-23, 2026-10-24). New features should fit it; `ROADMAP.md` says which wave
adds which part. **Chapter 1** is our world (32 gyms and badges, the Champion's Tower), **chapter 2** the Swirl
(missions, the Picture Worlds, the Wizard), **chapter 3** the Scribble (still being worked out).

# Chapter 1: Our world

## The Wizard and the world

Long ago a **Wizard** made this world: the hills and forests, the deserts and snowy peaks, and the caves deep
underground. Then he made the **creatures**, hundreds of them, each one different. He never made the same creature
twice.

His first creature was the strongest of all: **Zenithar**, the rainbow guardian.

## Why creatures battle

Creatures love to battle. It's how they play, and how they grow. A battle doesn't hurt them. When one faints it's
just worn out, and a rest with **Healer Hana** gets it back on its feet. The more a creature battles, the stronger
it gets, until one day it grows into its next form.

**Wild creatures** battle you to find out what kind of trainer you are. They only join someone who has proven to be
strong and fair.

## Capture Cubes

The Wizard made the **Capture Cubes** so that people and creatures could travel together. Inside, a creature has a
small, quiet place of its own to rest.

He hid what cubes are made of all over the world, which is why you sometimes find a cube in **leaves**, in **gold**
or in old **chests**. A creature only goes into a cube once it's tired from battling, when it has decided you're
worth following.

## Gym leaders and badges

The world grew too big for one Wizard to look after. So he chose the best trainers and gave each one a **type** to
watch over: Fire, Water, Sky, Bone and many more. They became the **gym leaders**.

He told them to give their **badge** only to trainers they trust. A badge means: *"I've seen how you handle
creatures, and I trust you."* Beating a leader doesn't make them your enemy. They're proud of you, and they'll
battle you again whenever you want to see how much you've grown.

## The Ninja
The last gym of our world is the **Shadow Dojo**, home of the **Ninja creatures**: they move through the shadows,
vanish in a puff of smoke and seem to know more than they say. Its trainers keep watch with lanterns; sneak past
them, or sneak up on them.

## The Dream

There's one world the Wizard didn't make. When you sleep in your cozy home, **you** make it. That's why everything
there floats, hangs upside down and makes no sense, and why you can jump so high. It has a gym too, and its badge
counts like any other.

## The Wizard disappears

The Wizard made his worlds in **the Swirl**, his workshop between the worlds. Every world he made there left the
Swirl a little weaker, and one day it **cracked**.

The Wizard went into the Swirl to hold it together, and **sealed himself inside his tower** so the crack couldn't
spread. He can't come out, or the crack would grow again.

Before he left, he did three things:
- He built the **Champion's Tower**, for the trainer who would one day earn the trust of every gym leader.
- He asked his oldest friends, **Champion Solenne** and **Zenithar**, to guard it and wait for that trainer.
- He hid a **star** in each of his other worlds. Together, the stars make a bridge to his tower.

## The Champion's Tower

Only a trainer with **all 32 badges of our world** gets in:
- **Two guards** check your badges.
- **Coach Dex** trains you for what's at the top.
- **Champion Solenne** and **Zenithar** battle you. Win, and you're the **Champion**. That ends chapter 1.

# Chapter 2: The Swirl

## Champion missions

With the Wizard gone, somebody has to look after the world. Solenne asks the new Champion to help:
- **Treasure maps** lead to things the Wizard hid long ago.
- **Building challenges:** the world keeps growing, and the Champion builds the new parts.
- **Giants:** some creatures have started growing **far too big**, confused and grumpy. One trainer can't calm a
  giant down, so you need **a friend**.

The rare rewards (Star Blocks, Rainbow Bricks, Giant Trophies) are things the Wizard made himself and left with
Solenne for the Champion. The missions **never run out**: there is always another map, another build, another
giant, because a world always needs looking after.

## Where the giants come from

The giants keep coming, and Solenne finally tells the Champion the truth: they leak through the **crack in the
Swirl**. Someone has to go in.

The only creatures who can move between worlds, the **Portal creatures**, have built a **Portal Door** right next
to the Champion's Tower door: big, bright and swirling, but it only opens for a Champion. You can step through any
time, alone or with a friend, and come back through the same door.

## The Swirl

The Swirl is a purple sky full of floating platforms, rivers that run *upwards*, stairs that loop back on
themselves, and big frames hanging in the air. Each frame is a **doorway to another world** the Wizard made.

Far out in the sky floats **the Wizard's tower**. You can see it the moment you arrive, but you can't reach it:
there is no bridge yet, only an empty row of stepping-stone spots leading out to it.

The worlds behind the frames are the **Picture Worlds**: you step into a picture, and you're somewhere else. They
are not gyms, and there are no more badges. Explore them as much as you like; at the end of each one waits a
**Boss**. Beat the Boss and you win that world's **star**, the one the Wizard hid there. Bring it back and it becomes
a glowing stepping stone on the **star bridge** to his tower. (Each star comes with a little note from the Wizard.)
Nobody knows how many stars there are: the Wizard made a lot of worlds.

## The Picture Worlds

**The Giant House.** Step through and you're the size of a mouse. The Wizard made this house from the point of view
of his pet mouse. The kitchen is as big as a town:
- a fan blows you up onto the shelves
- a toy train crosses the floor
- a dripping tap becomes a river you float down on a bottle cap
- a cat sleeps by the table: don't wake it

The Boss waits on a giant cookie on the table.

**The Pirate Seas.** Open ocean and scattered islands, ruled by **Captain Squawk**, a parrot pirate who took over
this world once the Wizard was gone. Fly from island to island by cannon, swing on ropes, and board the Captain's
ship. The star is in his treasure chest.

**Playland.** The Wizard built it for a great festival: a race track, a football stadium and a fair with a roller
coaster. The festival never happened, and everything has stood still, waiting. Beat the racer, the football captain
and the fair leader, and Playland starts up again.

**The Portal House.** In the Swirl itself, the Portal creatures' oldest, who has been watching the crack the
longest, is the Boss at the end of a chain of portal rooms.

**The Room of Doors.** The Wizard locked away the gifts he never got to hand out: the **Surprise creatures**. You
only know what's inside when they open, and every door in the room is a surprise too.

## The Wizard's Tower

With every star found, the star bridge is whole and you can cross. The tower is the Wizard's **studio**. Before he made any creature, he
drew it first, so every floor holds a drawing to copy, each harder than the last. Climbing it teaches you how a
creature is designed.

At the top is the Wizard, still holding the Swirl together. He's tired. What would close the crack for good is a
**new guardian creature** for the Swirl, but he has no strength left to make one.

He battles you first, the hardest battle in the game, to see if your team is strong enough. When you win, he hands
you his drawing board:

*"You design it."*

You design your own creature, unlike any other. It becomes the Swirl's guardian, the crack closes, and the Wizard
can finally come out. That ends chapter 2.

# Chapter 3: The Scribble

(The outline; it will be filled in over time.)

Long ago the Wizard drew a creature he didn't like. He scribbled over it and threw it away. When the Swirl cracked,
**the Scribble** slipped out. Now it's angry and lonely, and it **scribbles over the worlds**: patches of our world
and of the Picture Worlds turn into messy grey scribble, and the creatures caught in it go grey and grumpy.

The Wizard is weak after holding the Swirl together for so long, so this time he needs a partner: **you**.
- **The Wizard battles beside you**, two against one, in the scribbled places.
- **His workshop:** he teaches you to make things yourself, like Capture Cubes (he made the first ones, after all).
- **Eggs:** the Wizard can save a scribbled creature by turning it back into an egg. Raise it, and it hatches bright
  and new. Eggs are how you heal the world.
- **Friends:** some eggs only hatch with a friend's help, and friends can give each other presents and trade
  creatures. The Scribble is beaten by friends working together.

In the end you don't destroy the Scribble. You help the Wizard **finish drawing it**, and it becomes a friend.
